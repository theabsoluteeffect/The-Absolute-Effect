Breast Cancer Trial Evidence

The Absolute Effect presents breast cancer trials in a clinically interpretable format, emphasizing what the treatment changes for patients rather than relative measures alone.

Featured evidence

  • CALGB 9343 — radiotherapy omission in older women after lumpectomy and endocrine therapy.
  • PRIME II — radiotherapy omission in older women with low-risk hormone-receptor-positive disease.
  • NSABP B-04 — extent of breast and axillary surgery.
  • NSABP B-18 — neoadjuvant chemotherapy before surgery.
  • NSABP B-27 — neoadjuvant chemotherapy and pathologic response.
  • DBCG 82b — postmastectomy radiotherapy in high-risk disease.

How to interpret the evidence

For each trial, look for the absolute difference at a specified time horizon, the endpoint used to calculate it, and the corresponding NNT or NNH when a defensible calculation is possible.
