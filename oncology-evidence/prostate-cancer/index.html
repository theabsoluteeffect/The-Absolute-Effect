Prostate Cancer Trial Evidence

Clinical trial evidence for prostate cancer, organized around the decisions that matter: observation or treatment, postoperative radiotherapy, systemic intensification and management of metastatic hormone-sensitive disease.

Featured evidence

  • ProtecT — long-term trade-offs between monitoring, surgery and radiotherapy for localized prostate cancer.
  • CHAARTED — addition of docetaxel to androgen-deprivation therapy in metastatic hormone-sensitive disease.
  • RTOG 0534 SPPORT — postoperative radiotherapy, androgen-deprivation therapy and pelvic nodal treatment.
  • GETUG-AFU 16 — salvage radiotherapy with short-term androgen deprivation.

Absolute treatment effect

The key question is not only whether a treatment changes a hazard ratio or relative risk, but how many patients experience a clinically meaningful difference over a defined time horizon.
