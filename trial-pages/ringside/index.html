Desmoid-type fibromatosis · Medical Oncology · Phase II

RINGSIDE

Adults with progressive or symptomatic desmoid-type fibromatosis requiring systemic therapy. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignRandomized phase II dose-finding study
Population42 adults with progressive desmoid tumours enrolled across dose cohorts.
Primary endpointDose-limiting toxicity and dose selection; tumour-volume change and response were key activity outcomes.
Data maturityEarly dose-finding assessment through approximately 16 weeks; longer-term durability remains under study.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 42 adults with progressive desmoid tumours enrolled across dose cohorts.

Intervention: Oral varegacestat at protocol dose levels including 1.2 mg daily, 2 mg intermittent dosing and 4 mg intermittent dosing (2 days on, 5 days off).

Comparator: Randomized dose cohorts; no standard-of-care control arm.

Primary endpoint: Dose-limiting toxicity and dose selection; tumour-volume change and response were key activity outcomes.

Follow-up and maturity: Early dose-finding assessment through approximately 16 weeks; longer-term durability remains under study.

Registry: NCT04871282. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

Key efficacy outcome

Reported median tumour-volume reduction at early assessment

Varegacestat highest-dose cohort
37%

Tumour-volume reduction was observed across dose cohorts; the highest-dose schedule showed approximately 37% median volume reduction at the reported early assessment. This is not a randomized efficacy comparison against standard therapy.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3 adverse events were uncommon; no grade 4 or 5 treatment-related events were reported in the primary abstract. Dose interruption and tolerability guide selection.

No NNT is calculated because this is a single-arm or non-comparative study without a randomized comparator.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive patient-reported QoL advantage was reported.

Interpretation and limitations

RINGSIDE provides early dose/safety and activity data for varegacestat in desmoid tumours; it is not a phase III comparative efficacy result.

Small phase II dose-finding cohort, short follow-up and no standard comparator. Tumour-volume change is not equivalent to durable symptom control or surgery avoidance.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
