Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 42 adults with progressive desmoid tumours randomized across three varegacestat dose schedules (approximately 14 per cohort).

Intervention: Varegacestat 1.2 mg orally once daily, 2 mg on an intermittent 2-days-on/5-days-off schedule, or 4 mg on an intermittent 2-days-on/5-days-off schedule.

Comparator: Randomized dose cohorts; no standard-of-care control arm.

Primary endpoint: Safety/dose-limiting toxicity and dose selection; tumour-volume change at week 16 was a key activity endpoint.

Follow-up and maturity: Early dose-finding through week 16; median treatment exposure approximately 50.5 weeks in the reported study population.

Registry: NCT04871282. Arm denominators and endpoint definitions are preserved as reported; no missing denominator is inferred.

Median tumour-volume change at week 16 was −51.91% with 1.2 mg daily, −15.25% with 2 mg intermittent dosing and −9.50% with 4 mg intermittent dosing. ORR was 35.7%, 21.4% and 21.4%, respectively. The 1.2-mg daily schedule was selected for further development.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3 adverse events occurred in 9/42 patients (21.4%); no serious treatment-related adverse event and no grade 4 or 5 event was reported in the abstract.

No NNT is calculated: the primary result is a median time-to-event or continuous/single-arm outcome without a valid common-horizon absolute risk difference.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No comparative QoL advantage was reported.

Interpretation and limitations

RINGSIDE supports the 1.2-mg daily varegacestat schedule for further study. The earlier staged page incorrectly assigned the greatest tumour reduction to the highest dose; this correction uses the dose-specific primary results.

Small phase II dose-finding study without standard comparator; tumour-volume reduction does not establish durable symptom control or surgery avoidance.
