ERBB2-positive very-high-risk NMIBC · Uro-Oncology · Phase II

Disitamab vedotin plus tislelizumab (TRUCE-04)

Transurethrally unresectable; cystectomy-ineligible or declining. Full primary article and protocol details reviewed 17 November 2026.

DesignOpen-label single-arm phase II nonrandomized trial using Simon two-stage design
Population28 treated; 26 efficacy-evaluable
Primary endpointComplete response of high-risk disease at approximately 3 months, defined as absence of T1 tumour, high-grade tumour, carcinoma in situ or progression; residual low-grade Ta disease was permitted.
Follow-upMedian follow-up 29.4 months; 24-month duration-of-response estimate reported among responders.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 28 adults with ERBB2-positive very-high-risk NMIBC that was transurethrally unresectable and who were ineligible for or declined radical cystectomy; median age 72 years. 26 completed the initial efficacy assessment; two preassessment withdrawals were counted as nonresponders in the all-treated analysis.

Intervention: Disitamab vedotin 120 mg IV on day 1 plus tislelizumab 200 mg IV on day 2 every 3 weeks for 3 initial cycles. Responders continued up to 8 cycles of disitamab vedotin and 17 cycles (1 year) of tislelizumab.

Comparator: No randomized comparator.

Primary endpoint: Complete response of high-risk disease at approximately 3 months, defined as absence of T1 tumour, high-grade tumour, carcinoma in situ or progression; residual low-grade Ta disease was permitted.

Follow-up and maturity: Median follow-up 29.4 months; 24-month duration-of-response estimate reported among responders.

Registry: NCT05495724

CR in 19/26 efficacy-evaluable patients (73.1%; 95% CI 54.8–91.3) and 19/28 all-treated patients (67.9%; 49.4–86.3). Among responders, 24-month sustained response was 83.5% (95% CI 68.0–100.0); no muscle-invasive or metastatic progression occurred.

Safety and absolute effect

Treatment-related AEs occurred in 25/28 (89.3%), most commonly paresthesia 12/28 (42.9%), alopecia 11/28 (39.3%) and pruritus 10/28 (35.7%). Grade 3–4 TRAEs occurred in 4/28 (14.3%); 2/28 (7.1%) discontinued treatment; no grade 5 event occurred.

No NNT is calculated: single-arm response outcomes and no randomized comparator do not support a treatment NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No validated comparative QoL outcome reported.

Interpretation and limitations

TRUCE-04 provides a signal for systemic bladder-preserving therapy in a narrowly defined ERBB2-positive, transurethrally unresectable VHR NMIBC population. The trial does not establish equivalence to radical cystectomy.

Single-centre single-arm study with 28 patients, selected ERBB2 IHC2+/3+ population and no randomized comparator. ERBB2 assay standardization and longer cystectomy-free follow-up remain important.

Current-practice context · reviewed 17 November 2026

These are investigational single-arm phase II strategies, not substitutes for established standard treatment. NCCN provenance is not verified because the complete guideline/reference list was inaccessible.
