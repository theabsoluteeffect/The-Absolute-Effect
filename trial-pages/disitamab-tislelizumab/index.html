Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 28 patients with ERBB2-positive NMIBC considered transurethrally unresectable were treated; 26 formed the efficacy-evaluable population.

Intervention: Disitamab vedotin plus tislelizumab on the protocol-defined intravenous schedule; the exact dose/schedule is retained in the linked primary protocol and must be reconciled with the final supplement before release.

Comparator: No randomized comparator.

Primary endpoint: Complete response at the protocol-defined early assessment.

Follow-up and maturity: Response durability assessed during follow-up to 24 months; bladder surveillance and salvage cystectomy remain essential.

Registry: NCT05495724. Arm denominators and endpoint definitions are retained from the primary report; no missing denominator is inferred.

Complete response occurred in 19/26 efficacy-evaluable patients (73.1%) and 19/28 treated patients (67.9%). The 24-month durable-response estimate was reported in the primary article; denominator and definition must remain distinct from the early CR denominator.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time effects are shown only when the primary source supplies a comparable endpoint and horizon.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3 or higher adverse events occurred in approximately 14.3%; no treatment-related death was reported in the abstract. Cytopenias, neuropathy and immune-mediated events require review.

No NNT is calculated: the study uses time-to-event medians, single-arm cohorts or continuous outcomes without a comparable fixed-time absolute risk difference.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No comparative QoL outcome was reported.

Interpretation and limitations

The combination is an investigational bladder-preserving approach in a narrowly defined ERBB2-positive NMIBC population. Randomized evidence and durable cystectomy-free outcomes remain necessary.

Small single-arm cohort with a highly selected ERBB2-positive, transurethrally unresectable population. Do not generalize to all BCG-unresponsive NMIBC or use response to replace cystectomy discussion.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The source hierarchy and analysis population are explicit.
