The Absolute Effect / Evidence / KEYNOTE-426
Renal Cell Carcinoma · Medical · Advanced / metastatic

KEYNOTE-426

Clinical question

In previously untreated advanced clear-cell RCC, does pembrolizumab plus axitinib improve outcomes over sunitinib?

Trial context
Population
861 patients
Intervention
Pembrolizumab 200 mg IV q3w up to 35 cycles + axitinib 5 mg PO BID; control sunitinib 50 mg PO daily 4 weeks on/2 weeks off.
Comparator
Sunitinib
Primary / key evidence
12-mo OS 89.9% vs 78.3% (+11.6 pp); HR death 0.53. Median PFS 15.1 vs 11.1 mo; ORR 59.3% vs 35.7%.
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

12-mo OS 89.9% vs 78.3% (+11.6 pp); HR death 0.53. Median PFS 15.1 vs 11.1 mo; ORR 59.3% vs 35.7%.

NNT/NNH: Not calculated unless a defensible fixed-time event-rate comparison was available in the cited primary analysis; median-survival differences alone are not converted to NNT.

Clinical interpretation

The combination improved OS, PFS and response compared with sunitinib in first-line advanced ccRCC.

Limitations / maturity

Interpret the effect in the context of the trial population, endpoint definition, follow-up and treatment era. Subsequent trials and evolving standards may limit direct applicability to contemporary patients.

Primary publication

Rini BI et al. N Engl J Med. 2019;380:1116–1127.
