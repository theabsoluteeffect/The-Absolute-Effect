JAVELIN Renal 101

In untreated advanced RCC, does avelumab plus axitinib improve disease control compared with sunitinib?

Population
886 patients with previously untreated advanced RCC.
Intervention / comparator
Avelumab 10 mg/kg IV every 2 weeks + axitinib 5 mg twice daily versus sunitinib 50 mg daily, 4 weeks on/2 weeks off.
Primary/major efficacy result
5-year PFS event-free rate: 12.0% vs 4.4%; absolute improvement 7.6 points; NNT 14.
Overall survival
Final OS HR 0.88 (95% CI 0.75–1.04) in the overall population; median OS 44.8 vs 38.9 months, P=.0669.

Absolute Effect

5-year PFS event-free rate: 12.0% vs 4.4%; absolute improvement 7.6 points; NNT 14.

Clinical interpretation: The primary PFS benefit persisted long-term, but the final OS analysis did not demonstrate a statistically significant difference.

Toxicity / treatment burden: Grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events: 66.8% vs 61.5%. Patient-reported outcomes were similar. NNH not calculated.

Important limitations

Open-label design and treatment evolution after progression can affect absolute survival comparisons. Absolute effects are reported only where a reliable common timepoint or directly reported rate permits calculation; otherwise NNT/NNH is not forced from medians or hazard ratios.

Primary source

Motzer RJ, et al. N Engl J Med. 2019;380:1103-1115; final analysis.
