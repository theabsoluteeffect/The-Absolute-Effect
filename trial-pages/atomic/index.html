In resected stage III mismatch-repair-deficient (dMMR) colon cancer, does adding atezolizumab to adjuvant mFOLFOX6 improve disease-free survival?

Population and treatment

Patients with completely resected stage III dMMR colon cancer (711 adults and one pediatric participant). Randomized 1:1 within 10 weeks after surgery. The trial regimen was atezolizumab 840 mg IV every 2 weeks for 12 cycles (6 months) plus mFOLFOX6 for 12 cycles, followed by atezolizumab monotherapy for 13 cycles (12 months total), versus mFOLFOX6 for 12 cycles (6 months). mFOLFOX6: 5-FU 400 mg/m² bolus plus 2400 mg/m² continuous infusion over 46 hours, oxaliplatin 85 mg/m² and leucovorin 400 mg/m². Stratification: N stage, T stage and tumor sidedness.

Primary endpoint: disease-free survival (DFS), defined as time from randomization to recurrence or death from any cause. Secondary endpoints: overall survival and adverse-event profile; PRO-CTCAE and quality-of-life outcomes are to be reported separately and were not available in the primary paper.

53.9% had high-risk T4 and/or N2 disease. Results should not be extrapolated to pMMR/MSS disease or stage II disease.

Efficacy

3-year disease-free survival · primary endpoint

At median follow-up 40.9 months, 3-year DFS was 86.3% (95% CI 81.8–89.8) vs 76.2% (95% CI 70.9–80.6); HR 0.50 (95% CI 0.35–0.73; P<0.001).

Overall survival

At median OS follow-up 45.8 months, 5-year OS was 89.7% vs 87.9%; HR 0.90 (95% CI 0.55–1.47). No statistically significant OS difference was demonstrated; OS remains immature.

Absolute excess of all-cause grade 3–4 AEs: 12.2 percentage points (291/346 [84.1%] vs 240/334 [71.9%]). No NNH is assigned.

Median atezolizumab exposure was 10.8 months versus 5.4 months for mFOLFOX6 in the control arm; cumulative toxicity risks were therefore observed over unequal treatment exposure. A reciprocal NNH would be misleading. Grade 5 events occurred in 6 versus 2 patients; two events in the atezolizumab arm were considered treatment-related.

Detailed adverse-event categories, discontinuation, dose intensity, patient-reported outcomes and longer-term safety should be taken from the primary paper and supplement; do not substitute treatment-related toxicity for all-cause toxicity.

Interpretation

Adding atezolizumab to adjuvant mFOLFOX6 significantly improved DFS in resected stage III dMMR colon cancer. The 3-year absolute DFS gain was 10.1 percentage points (NNT 10). OS was immature and not significantly different at this analysis (median OS follow-up 45.8 months; 5-year OS 89.7% vs 87.9%; HR 0.90, 95% CI 0.55–1.47; P=.6787). Grade 3–4 all-cause toxicity was higher, but no NNH is calculated because exposure duration differed.

Regulatory update — 8 October 2026: The FDA approved atezolizumab with fluoropyrimidine/oxaliplatin for adjuvant treatment of adult and pediatric patients aged ≥2 years with stage III dMMR colon cancer. The FDA-approved IV schedule is 840 mg every 2 weeks, 1200 mg every 3 weeks, or 1680 mg every 4 weeks with chemotherapy for 6 months, followed by atezolizumab monotherapy for 6 months or until recurrence/unacceptable toxicity. The FDA also approved the subcutaneous atezolizumab/hyaluronidase formulation for eligible patients aged ≥12 years and weighing ≥40 kg. This regulatory update is based on ATOMIC; it is not a separate trial.
