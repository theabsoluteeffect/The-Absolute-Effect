ATOMIC (Alliance A021502)

In resected stage III mismatch-repair-deficient (dMMR) colon cancer, does adding atezolizumab to adjuvant mFOLFOX6 improve disease-free survival?

712Randomized patients
355 vs 357Atezolizumab+mFOLFOX6 vs mFOLFOX6
40.9 monthsMedian follow-up for primary DFS analysis

Population and treatment

Adults with completely resected stage III dMMR colon cancer. Randomized 1:1 to adjuvant mFOLFOX6 for 6 months plus atezolizumab for 6 months followed by atezolizumab monotherapy to complete 12 months, or mFOLFOX6 alone for 6 months.

Primary endpoint: disease-free survival (DFS). Secondary endpoints: overall survival and adverse-event profile.

53.9% had high-risk T4 and/or N2 disease. Results should not be extrapolated to pMMR/MSS disease or stage II disease.

Efficacy

3-year disease-free survival · primary endpoint

At median follow-up 40.9 months, 3-year DFS was 86.3% (95% CI 81.8–89.8) vs 76.2% (95% CI 70.9–80.6); HR 0.50 (95% CI 0.35–0.73; P<0.001).

Overall survival

At median OS follow-up 45.8 months, 5-year OS was 89.7% vs 87.9%; HR 0.90 (95% CI 0.55–1.47). No statistically significant OS difference was demonstrated; OS remains immature.

Detailed adverse-event categories, discontinuation, dose intensity, patient-reported outcomes and longer-term safety should be taken from the primary paper and supplement; do not substitute treatment-related toxicity for all-cause toxicity.

Interpretation

Adding atezolizumab to adjuvant mFOLFOX6 significantly improved DFS in resected stage III dMMR colon cancer. The DFS gain was accompanied by a higher incidence of grade 3–4 adverse events. An OS benefit has not yet been demonstrated.
