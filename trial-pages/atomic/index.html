ATOMIC

In resected stage III dMMR colon cancer, does adding atezolizumab to adjuvant FOLFOX improve DFS?

Population and treatment

712 patients with resected stage III mismatch-repair-deficient colon cancer.

Intervention vs comparator: mFOLFOX6 for 6 months plus atezolizumab followed by atezolizumab to 12 months vs mFOLFOX6 alone for 6 months.

Registry: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=ATOMIC+atezolizumab+dMMR+colon+cancer

Results

3-year DFS 86.3% vs 76.2%; HR 0.50 (95% CI 0.35–0.73; p<0.001). Grade 3–4 AEs 84.1% vs 71.9%. Later 5-year OS 89.7% vs 87.9%; HR 0.90 (95% CI 0.55–1.47), no demonstrated OS benefit.

Interpretation and limitations

Strong DFS effect in stage III dMMR disease, but no demonstrated OS benefit in cited later analysis. Do not generalize to pMMR/MSS cancer.

Distinguish prespecified from exploratory subgroups, mature from immature endpoints, and superiority from non-inferiority. Verify current guidelines and eligibility.
