Testicular germ-cell tumours · Medical Oncology · Salvage chemotherapy

Modified PEI first-salvage cohort

Can dose-modified cisplatin, etoposide and ifosfamide (PEI) achieve durable outcomes as first salvage chemotherapy for male germ-cell tumours?

Histology scope: Male GCT · long-term cohort. This is not a seminoma-only efficacy estimate.

Study population
189
Evidence type
Mixed GCT · long-term cohort

Reported outcomes

In a long-term cohort treated with modified PEI, 35/189 (18.5%) achieved complete response to chemotherapy alone and 67/189 (35.4%) achieved marker-negative partial response. Overall disease-free status after chemotherapy and selected surgery was 76/189 (40.2%); 2-year PFS was 34.3% and 5-year OS 42.1%.

Interpretation and guideline context

Long-term single-centre cohort spanning 1985–2012; the final disease-free rate includes selected post-chemotherapy surgery. Mixed GCT and not a randomized comparison with TIP or HDCT.

ESMO–EURACAN 2022 lists standard-dose VIP/PEI, TIP or VeIP among first-salvage options after relapse. NCCN Testicular Cancer Guidelines Insights Version 2.2025 is linked as a contemporary guideline source. Neither guideline converts mixed-histology cohorts into seminoma-specific evidence.
