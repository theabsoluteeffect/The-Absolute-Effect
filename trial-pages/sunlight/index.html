Colorectal cancer · Medical oncology · Phase III

SUNLIGHT

Trifluridine/tipiracil plus bevacizumab versus trifluridine/tipiracil alone in refractory metastatic colorectal cancer. Primary publication: 2023; HRQoL/performance-status analysis: 2025. Evidence review: 24 November 2026.

DesignInternational, open-label, randomized phase III trial; 1:1 allocation.
Population492 adults with refractory metastatic colorectal cancer after no more than two prior chemotherapy regimens for advanced disease; 246 per arm.
Primary endpointOverall survival. Secondary endpoints included PFS, safety and time to ECOG performance-status worsening.
Data maturityPrimary OS and PFS analyses reported in 2023; later HRQoL/performance-status analysis published in 2025.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility: Adults with refractory metastatic colorectal cancer who had received no more than two previous chemotherapy regimens for advanced disease. Prior exposure to fluoropyrimidine, oxaliplatin and irinotecan was required; anti-VEGF and, for RAS wild-type disease, anti-EGFR therapy were permitted/expected according to prior treatment history. ECOG performance status was 0–1 at enrolment.

Randomization: 492 patients were assigned 1:1 to combination treatment (n=246) or trifluridine/tipiracil alone (n=246). The study was open-label and international.

Intervention: Trifluridine/tipiracil 35 mg/m² per dose orally twice daily on days 1–5 and 8–12 of each 28-day cycle, plus bevacizumab 5 mg/kg intravenously on days 1 and 15.

Comparator: Trifluridine/tipiracil 35 mg/m² per dose orally twice daily on days 1–5 and 8–12 of each 28-day cycle.

Primary endpoint: OS. Secondary endpoints included PFS, objective response, safety, time to ECOG worsening from 0/1 to ≥2, and patient-reported outcomes assessed with EORTC QLQ-C30 and EQ-5D-5L.

OS: Median OS was 10.8 versus 7.5 months; HR 0.61 (95% CI 0.49–0.77; P<0.001).

PFS: Median PFS was 5.6 versus 2.4 months; HR 0.44 (95% CI 0.36–0.54; P<0.001).

Functional outcome: Median time to ECOG worsening from 0/1 to ≥2 was 9.3 versus 6.3 months; HR 0.54 (95% CI 0.43–0.67).

12-month OS landmark: 43% versus 30% in the ASCO 2023 conference abstract. This is a same-horizon Kaplan–Meier estimate and is used for the time-specific NNT below; it is not inferred from the median OS or HR.

Absolute effect and NNT

12-month OS: Absolute benefit 13 percentage points (43%−30%). Calculation: ceil[1/0.13]=8. This is an approximate time-specific NNT based on the ASCO 2023 conference-reported landmark rates; the primary NEJM article reports the median OS and HR.

Safety, quality of life and treatment burden

Grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in 72.4% with FTD/TPI plus bevacizumab and 69.5% with FTD/TPI alone. Grade 3/4 neutrophil decreases occurred in 52% versus 39%, haemoglobin decreases in 5% versus 11%, and platelet decreases in 4% versus <1%. No treatment-related deaths were reported in the primary trial.

The 2025 HRQoL analysis found similar EORTC QLQ-C30 and EQ-5D-5L scores from baseline through cycle 6, without clinically relevant deterioration in either arm. The combination significantly prolonged time to definitive deterioration of QoL scores and time to ECOG performance-status worsening. These functional outcomes are secondary analyses, not the primary OS endpoint.

Interpretation and limitations

SUNLIGHT established an OS and PFS advantage for adding bevacizumab to trifluridine/tipiracil in refractory mCRC after standard fluoropyrimidine, oxaliplatin and irinotecan exposure. The comparator was FTD/TPI alone, not every other active later-line option. The 12-month NNT is based on conference-reported landmark rates, while the primary peer-reviewed paper reports median outcomes and HRs. Toxicity remains substantial, particularly myelosuppression.

Current-practice context · reviewed 24 November 2026

The combination is an evidence-based later-line option for appropriately pretreated mCRC. Selection should account for prior biologic exposure, marrow reserve, performance status, access and alternative later-line therapies. The current full NCCN Colon Cancer guideline/reference list was not accessible for exact mapping; no NCCN version, section, reference number or recommendation category is claimed.
