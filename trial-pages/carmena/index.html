Renal Cell Carcinoma · Surgical Oncology · Metastatic Disease

CARMENA

Clinical question

In intermediate/poor-risk metastatic RCC, is upfront cytoreductive nephrectomy necessary before systemic therapy?

Absolute Effect

Median OS was 18.4 vs 13.9 months. Sunitinib alone met the prespecified non-inferiority criterion; this is not proof that surgery is harmful.

Clinical interpretation

Immediate systemic therapy is an evidence-based strategy for selected intermediate/poor-risk metastatic RCC and avoids mandatory cytoreductive nephrectomy.
