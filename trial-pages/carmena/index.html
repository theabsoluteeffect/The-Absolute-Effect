The Absolute Effect / Evidence / CARMENA
Renal Cell Carcinoma · Surgical Oncology · Metastatic Disease

CARMENA

Clinical question

In intermediate/poor-risk metastatic RCC, is upfront cytoreductive nephrectomy necessary before systemic therapy?

Trial context
Population
450 patients with metastatic clear-cell RCC, MSKCC intermediate or poor risk
Intervention
Sunitinib alone vs cytoreductive nephrectomy followed by sunitinib
Key endpoint
Overall survival
Time horizon / follow-up
Median follow-up 50.9 months
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

Sunitinib alone was non-inferior to nephrectomy followed by sunitinib for OS; median OS was 18.4 vs 13.9 months. The upper 95% CI of the HR remained below the prespecified NI margin of 1.20. This is a non-inferiority result, not proof that surgery is harmful or never useful.

NNT: Not applicable; non-inferiority trial

Treatment burden / harm

Major surgery burden

NNH: Not quantifiable from the primary endpoint

Relative effect

NON-INFERIOR; HR: 0.89

95% CI 0.71–1.10; NI margin 1.20

Clinical interpretation

Interpret the absolute difference together with the endpoint, time horizon, relative effect and limitations of the study design.
