Prostate Cancer · Medical Oncology · Metastatic Hormone-Sensitive

CHAARTED

Clinical question

In metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer, does adding docetaxel to ADT improve survival?

Trial context
Population
790 men with metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer
Intervention
ADT + docetaxel vs ADT alone
Key endpoint
Overall survival
Time horizon / follow-up
Median follow-up 53.7 months
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

Updated follow-up showed median OS 57.6 vs 47.2 months, a 10.4-month median difference, with HR 0.72. Benefit was greatest in high-volume disease. NNT is not derived from median survival because a reliable fixed-time absolute mortality estimate is not the appropriate basis here.

NNT: Not appropriately calculable from median survival

Treatment burden / harm

Docetaxel treatment toxicity

NNH: Not quantifiable from the selected primary endpoint; febrile neutropenia and other chemotherapy toxicity reported in text

Relative effect

HR: 0.72

95% CI 0.59–0.89

Clinical interpretation

Interpret the absolute difference together with the endpoint, time horizon, relative effect and limitations of the study design.
