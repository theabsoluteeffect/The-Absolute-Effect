CHAARTED

Clinical question

In metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer, does adding docetaxel to ADT improve survival?

Trial at a glance

790 men; ADT + docetaxel vs ADT alone; overall survival.

Absolute Effect

Updated median OS was 57.6 vs 47.2 months, a 10.4-month median difference; HR 0.72. NNT is not derived from median survival.

Clinical interpretation

Docetaxel provides a clinically meaningful survival gain, particularly in high-volume metastatic disease, at the cost of chemotherapy toxicity.
