KEYNOTE-407

First-line metastatic squamous NSCLC

Registry: NCT02775435 · Evidence reviewed 9 November 2026 · Aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase III, double-blind randomized; n=559.Primary-source evidence summary

Study design, population and eligibility

Design and sample

Phase III, double-blind randomized; n=559.

Population

Untreated metastatic squamous NSCLC, all PD-L1 strata.

Treatment and comparator

Intervention: Pembrolizumab + carboplatin + paclitaxel/nab-paclitaxel, then pembrolizumab.

Comparator: Placebo + same chemotherapy.

Endpoints

OS and PFS by blinded independent review.

Key efficacy and clinical outcomes

At ~5 y: OS HR 0.71; PFS HR 0.62; 5-y OS 18.4% vs 9.7%; PFS 10.8% vs 3.5%; ORR 62.2% vs 38.8%.

Toxicity and treatment burden

Grade 3–5 toxicity is common with chemo-immunotherapy; immune-mediated events add to platinum-taxane burden.

Absolute effect and NNT/NNH

Applies to squamous histology. Landmark rates are not NNTs.
