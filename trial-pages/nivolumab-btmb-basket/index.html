Nivolumab ctDNA-bTMB basket phase II

This amended Japanese phase II basket study met its prespecified response threshold in a 19-patient tumour-agnostic bTMB-high cohort, but earlier GI cohorts were descriptive and the single-arm results require randomized validation.

Study identity and design

Multicentre, open-label, nonrandomized multi-basket phase II study in Japan. The protocol evolved through three phases: initial GI high-bTMB cohorts, an ultrahigh-bTMB GI cohort, then a tumour-agnostic bTMB-High cohort. Cohorts 1 and 2 and the ultrahigh GI cohort were stopped before planned enrolment and are descriptive only.

Advanced/unresectable or recurrent solid tumours with ctDNA-defined bTMB. Cohorts 1 and 2 used Guardant360 CDx in GI cancers; the ultrahigh GI cohort used the top 2% bTMB within each GI tumour type; the final tumour-agnostic cohort included solid tumours with FoundationOne Liquid CDx bTMB ≥14 mut/Mb.

Intervention and comparator

Experimental: Nivolumab 360 mg IV every 3 weeks until progression or unacceptable toxicity.

Comparator: None — single-arm, nonrandomized cohorts.

Key efficacy outcome

Primary inferential cohort: bTMB-High, n=19; ORR 4/19 (21.1%), 90% CI 7.5–41.9% (95% CI 6.1–45.6%). The lower bound of the prespecified two-sided 90% CI exceeded the 5% response threshold, meeting the primary endpoint for this cohort.

Descriptive cohorts: GI high-bTMB cohort 1, 4/28 (14.3%; 95% CI 4.0–32.7%); cohort 2, 0/4 (0%; 95% CI 0–60.2%); ultrahigh-bTMB GI cohort, 4/19 (21.1%; 95% CI 6.1–45.6%). Early ctDNA decline at week 3 was associated with longer PFS (HR 0.51, 95% CI 0.29–0.87; p=0.013); day-21 landmark analysis HR 0.53 (95% CI 0.30–0.91; p=0.020). This is exploratory, not a randomized treatment effect.

Safety and absolute effect

Treatment-related grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in 6/70 (8.6%) overall: 2/28 (7%) in cohort 1, 1/4 (25%) in cohort 2, 3/19 (16%) in the ultrahigh GI cohort and 0/19 in the bTMB-High cohort. The accessible primary abstract does not provide a complete grade 3–5 event breakdown or treatment-related death count.

Quality of life: Not reported in the primary abstract.

Treatment burden / discontinuation: Nivolumab was continued until progression or unacceptable toxicity. Cohort-specific discontinuation counts were not reported in the accessible abstract.

Absolute effect and NNT / NNH

NNT is not applicable: all cohorts were single-arm and no randomized comparator or absolute between-arm risk difference exists.

Interpretation and limitations

The study supports prospective evaluation of ctDNA-defined bTMB selection, but the signal is preliminary. Absolute bTMB did not clearly distinguish responders from nonresponders; early ctDNA dynamics and baseline ctDNA concentration appeared more informative. Cohort amendments and assay changes limit cross-cohort comparisons.

Limitations: Single-arm, small cohorts; three cohorts were stopped early and are descriptive; different assays and thresholds were used across phases; short/unstated follow-up; no randomized comparator; no QoL data.
