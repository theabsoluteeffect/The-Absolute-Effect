Non-small-cell lung cancer · Medical oncology · Phase III

AENEAS2

Aumolertinib plus platinum–pemetrexed versus aumolertinib alone in untreated EGFR-sensitive advanced NSCLC. Primary publication: 2026. Evidence review: 24 November 2026.

DesignMulticentre, open-label, randomized phase III trial; 1:1 allocation.
Population624 adults with untreated locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC harbouring EGFR exon 19 deletion or L858R; 310 combination and 314 monotherapy.
Primary endpointProgression-free survival by blinded independent review using RECIST 1.1.
Data maturityMedian follow-up 23.4 months. Overall-survival data were not mature in the primary report.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility: Adults with histologically or cytologically confirmed locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC, an EGFR exon 19 deletion or L858R mutation, ECOG performance status 0–1, and no previous systemic treatment for advanced disease. Stable brain metastases were permitted; prior EGFR-TKI exposure was not.

Randomization: 624 participants were randomized 1:1: aumolertinib plus chemotherapy (n=310) or aumolertinib alone (n=314). Randomization was stratified by EGFR mutation type and baseline brain-metastasis status.

Intervention: Aumolertinib 110 mg orally once daily plus pemetrexed 500 mg/m² and cisplatin 75 mg/m² or carboplatin AUC 5 intravenously on day 1 every 21 days for 4–6 cycles, followed by maintenance aumolertinib 110 mg daily plus pemetrexed 500 mg/m² every 3 weeks.

Comparator: Aumolertinib 110 mg orally once daily.

Primary endpoint: PFS assessed by blinded independent review according to RECIST 1.1. Secondary endpoints included OS and safety. The safety-evaluable population comprised 304 and 316 patients, respectively.

PFS: At median follow-up 23.4 months, median PFS by blinded independent review was 28.9 months with combination therapy and 18.9 months with aumolertinib alone; HR 0.47 (95% CI 0.37–0.60; P<0.0001).

OS: Immature at the primary analysis; no mature OS hazard ratio or common-horizon absolute OS rates were reported in the primary abstract.

NNT/NNH: Not calculated. The primary report provides median PFS and a hazard ratio but no comparable fixed-time absolute PFS rates. NNT is not derived from medians or an HR.

Safety and absolute effect

In the safety population, grade 3–4 neutropenia occurred in 55% (168/304) with combination therapy versus 1% (4/316) with aumolertinib alone; leukopenia in 34% (103/304) versus <1% (1/316); and thrombocytopenia in 20% (62/304) versus 1% (2/316). Serious adverse events occurred in 36% versus 17%. Treatment-related deaths were reported in 1 patient (<1%) versus 2 patients (1%).

These haematologic toxicities are clinically important and require blood-count monitoring, dose interruption or reduction, and supportive care as appropriate. The primary report did not provide a validated quality-of-life result.

Interpretation and limitations

AENEAS2 provides phase III evidence that adding platinum–pemetrexed to aumolertinib improves PFS over aumolertinib alone in untreated EGFR exon 19 deletion/L858R advanced NSCLC. It does not compare aumolertinib with osimertinib-based combinations, and its results should not be treated as proof of interchangeability across EGFR-TKIs or regions. The open-label design, China-based setting, immature OS and substantially greater myelosuppression are important considerations.

Current-practice context · reviewed 27 November 2026

Aumolertinib plus platinum–pemetrexed is an emerging post-EGFR-TKI option supported by a Chinese phase III trial; it is not a universal global standard. Osimertinib-based regimens remain key reference options in many jurisdictions.
