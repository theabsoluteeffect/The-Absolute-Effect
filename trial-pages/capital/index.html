Gastric cancer · Surgical/Medical Oncology · Phase III

CAPITAL

Adjuvant treatment for high-risk stage II/III gastric cancer after D2 gastrectomy. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignOpen-label randomized phase III trial
Population724 patients with high-risk stage II/III gastric cancer after D2 gastrectomy randomized to S-1 or SOX adjuvant treatment.
Primary endpointOverall survival at 5 years.
Data maturityFive-year OS and DFS results reported.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 724 patients with high-risk stage II/III gastric cancer after D2 gastrectomy randomized to S-1 or SOX adjuvant treatment.

Intervention: SOX: oxaliplatin 130 mg/m² intravenously on day 1 plus S-1 40–60 mg orally twice daily on days 1–14 every 3 weeks for protocol-defined cycles.

Comparator: S-1 alone at 40–60 mg orally twice daily on days 1–14 every 3 weeks.

Primary endpoint: Overall survival at 5 years.

Follow-up and maturity: Five-year OS and DFS results reported.

Registry: NCT01795027. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

Five-year OS 70.9% versus 62.9%; HR 0.76, P=0.01. Five-year DFS 66.2% versus 55.6%; HR 0.76. The 8.0-percentage-point OS difference corresponds to NNT 13 at 5 years.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3–4 adverse events occurred in approximately 25% versus 13%; oxaliplatin adds neuropathy, cytopenias and gastrointestinal toxicity.

NNT 13Overall survival · 5 years

70.9% versus 62.9%; absolute benefit 8 percentage points. Alive at 5 years (reported landmark estimate). Calculation: ceil(100 / (70.9 − 62.9)) = ceil(12.5) = 13.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage was reported in the primary abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

CAPITAL supports adding oxaliplatin to adjuvant S-1 in the studied high-risk post-D2 population, with a modest 5-year OS absolute benefit and increased grade 3–4 toxicity.

Post-D2 population and regional treatment context; the benefit must be weighed against cumulative oxaliplatin toxicity and applicability to different surgical standards.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
