Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 724 patients with pathological stage II–III gastric or GEJ adenocarcinoma after D2 gastrectomy were randomized 1:1: 362 to SOX and 362 to S-1. Safety analyses included 349 and 347 patients, respectively.

Intervention: SOX: oxaliplatin 130 mg/m² IV day 1 plus S-1 orally twice daily on days 1–14 every 3 weeks for the protocol-defined adjuvant course.

Comparator: S-1 alone on the protocol schedule.

Primary endpoint: Overall survival; 5-year OS was the principal reported landmark.

Follow-up and maturity: Median follow-up 74.0 months (IQR 35.5–89.3).

Registry: NCT01795027. Arm denominators are given where available; missing values are not inferred.

Five-year OS 70.9% (95% CI 66.0–76.1) versus 62.9% (57.8–68.5); HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.58–0.95), P=0.018. Five-year DFS 66.2% (61.2–71.6) versus 55.6% (50.4–61.3); HR 0.76 (0.61–0.96). The 8.0-point OS difference gives NNT 13 at 5 years.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3–4 treatment-related AEs occurred in 87/349 (25%) versus 45/347 (13%). Grade 3–4 neutropenia occurred in 44/349 (13%) versus 23/347 (7%).

NNT 13

70.9% versus 62.9%; absolute benefit 8 percentage points at 5 years. Alive at 5 years (reported Kaplan–Meier estimate). Calculation: ceil(100 / (70.9 − 62.9)) = 13.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage was reported in the primary abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

CAPITAL shows an OS and DFS benefit from adding oxaliplatin to adjuvant S-1 in the studied stage II–III post-D2 population. The earlier staged page's HR/P-value and safety denominators were not source-faithful and are corrected here.

The study is in a Chinese post-D2 population; cumulative oxaliplatin neuropathy and haematologic toxicity must be weighed against the 5-year OS benefit.
