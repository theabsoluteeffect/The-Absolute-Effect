Cervical Cancer · Medical · First-line persistent, recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer

KEYNOTE-826

Does pembrolizumab added to platinum chemotherapy with or without bevacizumab improve outcomes in advanced cervical cancer?

Trial design and population

Population / eligibility
617 patients with persistent, recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer.
Treatment vs comparator
Pembrolizumab 200 mg IV q3 weeks + paclitaxel and cisplatin/carboplatin, with or without bevacizumab, vs placebo plus the same chemotherapy.
Sample size
617 (reported trial population)
Featured endpoint
Median OS, CPS ≥1 · final OS analysis

Registry: undefined.

Key results

PD-L1 CPS ≥1: median OS 28.6 vs 16.5 months (HR 0.60, 95% CI 0.49–0.74). All-comer median OS 26.4 vs 16.8 months (HR 0.63, 95% CI 0.52–0.77).
