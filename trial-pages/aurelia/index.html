Design, population and treatment

Design: Open-label, randomized phase III trial with investigator-selected chemotherapy backbone.

Eligibility and population: 361 patients with platinum-resistant recurrent ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal carcinoma; progression within 6 months of platinum. Investigator selected weekly paclitaxel, pegylated liposomal doxorubicin or topotecan before randomization.

Intervention: Selected chemotherapy plus bevacizumab 10 mg/kg IV every 2 weeks or 15 mg/kg every 3 weeks, according to chemotherapy schedule.

Comparator: The same investigator-selected chemotherapy alone.

Randomization and endpoint: Progression-free survival. The full randomization stratification variables and protocol amendments remain subject to protocol-level reconciliation before release.

Key efficacy outcome

Median PFS 6.7 versus 3.4 months (HR 0.48, 95% CI 0.38–0.60; p<0.001). ORR was 27.3% versus 11.8% (p=0.001). Median OS was 16.6 versus 13.3 months (HR 0.85, 95% CI 0.66–1.08; p=0.174).

Data maturity: Primary PFS analysis after 301 PFS events; OS follow-up continued. Primary versus secondary endpoints are kept distinct; no survival estimate is inferred from a hazard ratio or median alone.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 hypertension occurred in approximately 7% versus 1%; GI perforation occurred in 2.2% of bevacizumab-treated patients and none in chemotherapy alone. Grade ≥2 hypertension and proteinuria were more common with bevacizumab.

Delivery and discontinuation: Treatment burden depends on the selected chemotherapy backbone; bevacizumab adds repeated IV infusions and monitoring for hypertension, proteinuria, bleeding and GI perforation.

Quality of life and patient experience

The AURELIA patient-reported outcome analysis reported improvement in abdominal/GI symptoms for some patients receiving bevacizumab-containing therapy; full domain-level estimates and denominators remain to be extracted.

Where a separate patient-reported outcome publication exists, it is treated as a secondary analysis attached to this underlying study and not as a separate trial page. Unextracted domains remain explicitly open rather than inferred.

Absolute effect and NNT / NNH

No NNT/NNH is calculated. The primary endpoint is reported as a median survival/PFS estimate or a non-inferiority comparison without a valid same-horizon absolute event difference; NNT/NNH is not derived from median survival or a hazard ratio.

Only same-endpoint, same-time-horizon absolute differences are eligible for NNT/NNH. Median survival and hazard ratios alone are not used. No NNT/NNH appropriate from the reported primary endpoint.

Interpretation and limitations

AURELIA demonstrated improved PFS and response with bevacizumab plus chemotherapy in selected platinum-resistant ovarian cancer, without a statistically significant OS improvement. Bowel involvement, obstruction and perforation risk are critical selection factors.

Limitations: Open-label design and investigator-selected chemotherapy; OS was not statistically significant. No NNT is derived from median PFS/OS or HR.
