← The Absolute Effect

Breast Cancer · Surgical · Early-stage · 1–2 Positive Sentinel Nodes

ACOSOG Z0011

Clinical question

Can ALND be omitted in selected women with T1–2 cN0 breast cancer and 1–2 positive sentinel nodes undergoing breast-conserving therapy?

Population / intervention / comparator

Population: 891 women with T1–2 invasive breast cancer, no palpable adenopathy and 1–2 metastatic SLNs; all had lumpectomy, whole-breast RT and systemic therapy.

Intervention: SLND alone.

Comparator: SLND + ALND.

Sample / endpoint: NNT: not appropriate for a non-inferiority surgical de-escalation endpoint. NNH: approximately 10 for the 11-point excess in 1-year subjective lymphedema with ALND.

Absolute Effect
10-year OS
86.3% vs 83.6% (+2.7 pp)

Non-inferiority was demonstrated.

10-year DFS
80.2% vs 78.2% (+2.0 pp)

HR 0.85 (95% CI 0.62–1.17; P=.32).

Relative effect / statistics

10-year OS HR 0.85; one-sided 95% CI upper bound 1.16; non-inferiority P=.02. Locoregional recurrence remained very low.

Toxicity / treatment burden

Subjective lymphedema at 1 year was 2% with SLND alone versus 13% with ALND; adverse surgical effects were 25% versus 70%.

NNT/NNH: NNT: not appropriate for a non-inferiority surgical de-escalation endpoint. NNH: approximately 10 for the 11-point excess in 1-year subjective lymphedema with ALND.

QoL / subgroups

Applicability requires breast conservation, tangential whole-breast RT and 1–2 positive SLNs; higher-risk axillary presentations were excluded.

Limitations / maturity

Not applicable to palpable nodes, >2 positive SLNs, mastectomy or many neoadjuvant settings. Radiation-field details matter.

Clinical interpretation

In carefully selected Z0011-eligible patients, omission of ALND preserves long-term survival while reducing axillary morbidity.
