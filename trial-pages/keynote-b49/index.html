Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 342 patients with PD-L1-positive HR-positive/HER2-negative advanced breast cancer after endocrine therapy were randomized 1:1 to pembrolizumab or placebo with chemotherapy.

Intervention: Pembrolizumab 200 mg intravenously every 3 weeks plus protocol-permitted investigator-choice chemotherapy; exact chemotherapy backbone and dose table require supplement verification before release.

Comparator: Placebo plus the same investigator-choice chemotherapy.

Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival in the prespecified PD-L1-positive population; overall survival was a key secondary endpoint.

Follow-up and maturity: Final PFS analysis reported; OS maturity and prespecified subgroup interpretation should follow the complete paper.

Registry: NCT04895358. Arm denominators and endpoint definitions are retained from the primary report; no missing denominator is inferred.

Key efficacy outcome

Median progression-free survival

Pembrolizumab + chemotherapy
6.7 months
Placebo + chemotherapy
6 months

Median PFS was 6.7 versus 6.0 months; HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.58–0.95), P=0.0089. The effect is modest in absolute median-time terms; do not extrapolate to PD-L1-negative disease.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time effects are shown only when the primary source supplies a comparable endpoint and horizon.

Safety and absolute effect

Immune-mediated toxicity is added to chemotherapy burden. Exact arm-specific grade 3–5 rates and discontinuations require the full primary safety table; the page is held until these are extracted.

No NNT is calculated: the study uses time-to-event medians, single-arm cohorts or continuous outcomes without a comparable fixed-time absolute risk difference.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage is claimed pending full patient-reported outcome extraction.

Interpretation and limitations

The final analysis suggests a modest PFS benefit from pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy in PD-L1-positive HR+/HER2-negative advanced breast cancer; treatment selection must account for absolute effect and toxicity.

PD-L1-positive population only, modest PFS difference and no inference for PD-L1-negative disease. Full chemotherapy and safety details are required before release.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The source hierarchy and analysis population are explicit.
