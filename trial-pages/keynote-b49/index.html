HR-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer · Medical Oncology · Phase III

KEYNOTE-B49

Advanced HR-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer requiring chemotherapy after endocrine therapy. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignDouble-blind randomized phase III trial
Population1236 patients with HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer randomized to pembrolizumab or placebo added to investigator-choice chemotherapy.
Primary endpointProgression-free survival and overall survival in prespecified populations.
Data maturityPrimary PFS analysis reported; OS was not statistically significant at the reported cutoff.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 1236 patients with HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer randomized to pembrolizumab or placebo added to investigator-choice chemotherapy.

Intervention: Pembrolizumab 200 mg intravenously every 3 weeks plus investigator-choice chemotherapy (protocol-permitted taxane, capecitabine or other specified chemotherapy backbone).

Comparator: Placebo plus the same investigator-choice chemotherapy.

Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival and overall survival in prespecified populations.

Follow-up and maturity: Primary PFS analysis reported; OS was not statistically significant at the reported cutoff.

Registry: NCT04895358. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

Key efficacy outcome

Median progression-free survival

Pembrolizumab + chemotherapy
16.5 months
Placebo + chemotherapy
13.4 months

Median PFS 16.5 versus 13.4 months; HR 0.77, P=0.001. OS HR 0.89, P=0.12; no OS advantage is established.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events were approximately 78% versus 74%; immune-mediated toxicity is added to chemotherapy burden.

No NNT is calculated because Median PFS and HR are time-to-event measures; no verified fixed-time PFS rate was used for NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage was reported in the primary abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

KEYNOTE-B49 suggests a modest PFS improvement from adding pembrolizumab to chemotherapy in selected HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced disease; OS and biomarker-defined benefit remain important.

The benefit is a PFS result in a chemotherapy-requiring population; it should not be extrapolated to endocrine-sensitive disease or interpreted as an OS benefit.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
