PD-L1-positive HR-positive/HER2-negative advanced breast cancer · Medical Oncology · Phase III

KEYNOTE-B49

Advanced/metastatic after endocrine therapy. Primary analysis hierarchy and source limitations explicitly stated.

DesignRandomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase III
Population342 randomized; 171 per arm in PD-L1 CPS ≥1 population
Primary endpointPFS by BICR in PD-L1 CPS ≥1; OS was a key secondary endpoint.
Follow-upMedian follow-up 34.9 months at final OS analysis; earlier PFS interim median follow-up 29.1 months.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 342 patients with PD-L1 CPS ≥1 HR-positive/HER2-negative advanced breast cancer after endocrine therapy randomized 1:1: pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy n=171 and placebo plus chemotherapy n=171.

Intervention: Pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every 3 weeks plus investigator-choice chemotherapy (taxane or capecitabine) per protocol; exact regimen distribution and component doses require the full protocol/supplement.

Comparator: Placebo plus the same investigator-choice chemotherapy.

Primary endpoint: PFS by BICR in PD-L1 CPS ≥1; OS was a key secondary endpoint.

Follow-up: Median follow-up 34.9 months at final OS analysis; earlier PFS interim median follow-up 29.1 months.

Registry: NCT04895358

PFS at interim analysis: median 6.7 versus 6.0 months; HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.58–0.95), P=0.0089. Final OS: median 21.0 versus 22.2 months; HR 1.04 (95% CI 0.81–1.33), P=0.6185. No OS benefit was established.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs occurred in 37.6% versus 32.7% (rounded 38% vs33%); treatment-related AEs led to discontinuation in 11.2% versus 5.4%.

No NNT is calculated: no common-horizon absolute PFS or OS rate difference is reported in the verified analysis; medians and HR are not converted to NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage reported.

Interpretation and limitations

KEYNOTE-B49 met its PFS endpoint in PD-L1-positive HR+/HER2− advanced breast cancer but did not improve OS. The prior page's PFS values were correct; final OS and safety have now been added.

PD-L1 CPS ≥1 population only; modest median PFS separation and no OS improvement. Exact chemotherapy backbone/dose distribution and complete safety tables require protocol/supplement extraction.
