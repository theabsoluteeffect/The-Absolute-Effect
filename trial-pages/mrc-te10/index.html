MRC TE10

In stage I seminoma, can the radiation field be reduced from dog-leg to para-aortic only without compromising relapse control?

Population478 men with stage I seminoma.InterventionPara-aortic strip radiotherapy, 30 Gy in 15 fractions.ComparatorPara-aortic + ipsilateral iliac (dog-leg) field, 30 Gy in 15 fractions.Primary clinical outcomeRelapse-free survival and toxicity.

Absolute Effect

Five-year relapse-free rates were 96.1% vs 96.2%, an absolute difference of only 0.1 percentage point. The mature relapse HR was 1.15 (90% CI 0.54–2.44).

Clinical interpretation

Reducing the field did not materially change overall relapse-free survival, while acute gastrointestinal/hematologic toxicity and gonadal toxicity were lower with para-aortic treatment. Pelvic relapse was more frequent after para-aortic-only RT, but total relapse remained very low.

Source

Fossa SD, et al. J Clin Oncol. 1999;17:1146. Mature follow-up: Mead GM, et al. JNCI. 2011;103:241–249.
