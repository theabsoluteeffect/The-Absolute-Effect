TIVO-3

Clinical question: In heavily pretreated metastatic RCC, does tivozanib improve disease control versus sorafenib?

Design: Phase III randomized open-label trial; 350 patients with metastatic RCC after 2–3 prior systemic regimens, including prior VEGFR-TKI therapy. Primary endpoint PFS by blinded independent review.

PFS

Median PFS 5.6 vs 3.9 months; HR 0.73 (95% CI 0.56–0.95), P=.02.

Tivozanib 2-y PFS 18%
Sorafenib 2-y PFS 5%

2-year PFS absolute difference 13 points; approximate NNT ≈ 8 at 2 years for remaining progression-free. This is a landmark/time-to-event NNT and should not be confused with an NNT based on median PFS.

Response

ORR 18% vs 8%, absolute increase 10 points; NNT = 10 for one additional objective response.

OS and toxicity

Final OS analysis did not demonstrate an OS advantage; reported HR approximately 0.97. Grade 3 treatment-related adverse events occurred in 44% vs 55%; dose reduction 24% vs 38%, interruption 48% vs 63%, discontinuation 21% vs 29%.

Methodology

Open-label phase III trial with blinded independent radiological PFS assessment. Patients were heavily pretreated and included prior checkpoint-inhibitor exposure. Because PFS was the primary endpoint, the absolute-effect interpretation focuses on PFS and response rather than forcing an OS NNT.

Our take

TIVO-3 demonstrates a real late-line PFS and response advantage with tivozanib, including a striking 13-point difference in 2-year PFS. The absence of an OS improvement means the treatment should be understood as a disease-control strategy rather than a proven survival-extending intervention in this trial.
