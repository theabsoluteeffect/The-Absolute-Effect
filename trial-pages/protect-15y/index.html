Prostate Cancer · Surgical Oncology · Localized Disease

ProtecT

Clinical question

For localized prostate cancer, what are the long-term trade-offs between monitoring, surgery and radiotherapy?

Trial at a glance

1,643 men with clinically localized prostate cancer; active monitoring vs prostatectomy vs radiotherapy; 15-year metastatic disease and prostate-cancer mortality.

Absolute Effect

Metastases occurred in 4.7%, 5.0% and 9.4% after prostatectomy, RT and monitoring. Prostate-cancer mortality remained low: 2.2%, 2.9% and 3.1%.

Clinical interpretation

Definitive treatment reduces metastatic progression, but absolute prostate-cancer mortality remains low and similar across strategies; treatment morbidity differs substantially.
