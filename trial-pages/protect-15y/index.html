The Absolute Effect / Evidence / ProtecT
Prostate Cancer · Uro-oncology · Localized Disease

ProtecT

Clinical question

For localized prostate cancer, what are the long-term trade-offs between monitoring, surgery and radiotherapy?

Trial context
Population
1,643 men with clinically localized prostate cancer
Intervention
Active monitoring vs prostatectomy vs radiotherapy
Key endpoint
Metastatic disease at 15 years
Time horizon / follow-up
15 years
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

At 15 years, prostate-cancer mortality was low and similar: 2.2% prostatectomy, 2.9% RT and 3.1% active monitoring. Metastases were less frequent after prostatectomy or RT than monitoring: 4.7%, 5.0% and 9.4%, respectively. NNT 21 refers specifically to prostatectomy vs monitoring for preventing one metastasis by 15 years, not death.

NNT: 21 for prostatectomy vs monitoring

Treatment burden / harm

Treatment morbidity

NNH: Not quantifiable from the selected endpoint; urinary, sexual and bowel harms reported separately

Relative effect

HR: No significant difference in prostate-cancer mortality; metastases 4.7% vs 9.4% for prostatectomy vs monitoring

Cancer-specific mortality P=.53 overall

Clinical interpretation

Interpret the absolute difference together with the endpoint, time horizon, relative effect and limitations of the study design.
