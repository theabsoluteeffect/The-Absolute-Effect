The Absolute Effect / Evidence

Hepatocellular Carcinoma · Surgical · Definitive / Curative

Feng et al. 2012 (RFA vs resection)

Clinical question: What are the efficacy and safety outcomes of small hcc <4 cm, up to two nodules?

Registry: No registry identifier reported · Randomized controlled trial; n=168 (84 per group). Evidence cut-off: 13 November 2026.

Design, eligibility and treatment

Population

Small HCC <4 cm and up to two nodules.

Intervention / comparator

Percutaneous RFA versus surgical resection.

Design

Randomized controlled trial; n=168 (84 per group).

Primary endpoint

Overall survival and recurrence-free survival.

Key efficacy outcomes

3-y OS 74.8% resection vs 67.2% RFA; 3-y RFS 61.1% vs 49.6%. No significant OS (p=0.342) or RFS (p=0.122) differences.

Safety

Complete grade-specific adverse-event table not available in abstract; no rates inferred.

Interpretation and limitations

Not proof of equivalence; RFA may be incomplete at selected anatomical sites.

Median survival differences and hazard ratios must not be converted into NNT. A patient pictogram is omitted unless a defensible fixed-time absolute event difference is reported.
