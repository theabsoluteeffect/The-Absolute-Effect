ECOG E9897: paclitaxel plus gemcitabine

What activity does paclitaxel plus gemcitabine have in germ-cell tumours considered incurable with chemotherapy or surgery?

Phase II mixed-GCT study

Population
28 assessable

Reported outcomes

In ECOG E9897, 6 of 28 assessable patients (21.4%) responded to paclitaxel plus gemcitabine, including 3 complete responses. Two complete responders remained disease-free at 15+ and 25+ months.

Interpretation and limitations

This was a small phase II study in highly refractory disease; it supports later-line activity but is not evidence that this combination should replace standard first salvage. Mixed histology.

Guideline context: ESMO–EURACAN recommends standard-dose VIP/PEI, TIP or VeIP as conventional first salvage options after relapse following cisplatin-based chemotherapy. NCCN Testicular Cancer Guidelines Insights (Version 2.2025) is a contemporary US guideline source. The study summarized here is not necessarily seminoma-specific; histology scope is stated explicitly below.
