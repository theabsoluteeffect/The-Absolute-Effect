Design, population and treatment

Design: Open-label, randomized, 2:1 phase II trial.

Eligibility and population: 217 adults with grade 1–3a follicular lymphoma after at least two prior systemic lines, including anti-CD20 antibody and alkylating-agent exposure; 145 assigned to zanubrutinib plus obinutuzumab and 72 to obinutuzumab.

Intervention: Zanubrutinib 160 mg orally twice daily with obinutuzumab 1,000 mg IV on the protocol schedule; zanubrutinib continued until progression or unacceptable toxicity. Exact obinutuzumab maintenance schedule requires protocol-level recheck.

Comparator: Obinutuzumab 1,000 mg IV on the same protocol schedule without zanubrutinib.

Randomization and endpoint: Overall response rate by independent central review. The full randomization stratification variables and protocol amendments remain subject to protocol-level reconciliation before release.

Key efficacy outcome

ORR 69% versus 46% (p=0.0012); complete response 39% versus 19%. Median PFS was approximately 28.0 versus 10.4 months (HR 0.50, 95% CI 0.33–0.75). These are primary-report estimates; the complete time-to-event table and data cutoff require final table-level reconciliation.

Data maturity: Primary JCO report; median follow-up approximately 20 months. Long-term OS maturity and full follow-up table extraction remain open. Primary versus secondary endpoints are kept distinct; no survival estimate is inferred from a hazard ratio or median alone.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 toxicity, serious adverse events, dose interruptions, infections, cytopenias, bleeding and treatment discontinuations require full primary-table/supplement extraction before release. Do not infer missing rates.

Delivery and discontinuation: Continuous oral BTK inhibition plus protocol-scheduled IV anti-CD20 therapy versus IV anti-CD20 alone; exact dose intensity, interruptions and discontinuations remain to be reconciled.

Absolute effect and NNT / NNH

No NNT/NNH is calculated. The primary endpoint is reported as a median survival/PFS estimate or a non-inferiority comparison without a valid same-horizon absolute event difference; NNT/NNH is not derived from median survival or a hazard ratio.

Only same-endpoint, same-time-horizon absolute differences are eligible for NNT/NNH. Median survival and hazard ratios alone are not used. No NNT/NNH appropriate from the reported primary endpoint.

Interpretation and limitations

The randomized signal supports activity of adding zanubrutinib to obinutuzumab in heavily pretreated follicular lymphoma, but this page remains a draft until dose schedule, full safety tables and follow-up maturity are checked directly against the article and supplement.

Limitations: Phase II, open-label, modest sample size and 2:1 randomization. ORR is assessed at a study cutoff rather than a fixed landmark, so no NNT is calculated. Full grade 3–5 safety tables and protocol schedule remain open.
