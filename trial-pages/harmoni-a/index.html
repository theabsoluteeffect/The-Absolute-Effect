Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer · Metastatic · Post-EGFR-TKI

HARMONi-A

After EGFR-TKI progression in locally advanced or metastatic EGFR-variant non-squamous NSCLC, does ivonescimab plus pemetrexed–carboplatin improve outcomes versus chemotherapy alone?

DesignPhase III · double-blind · randomized 1:1
Population322 patients · post-EGFR-TKI EGFR-variant non-squamous NSCLC
Primary endpointPFS by independent radiology review; OS key secondary
RegistryNCT05184712

Study identity and design

HARMONi-A is a China-based, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase III trial conducted at 55 sites. It enrolled 322 adults with locally advanced or metastatic EGFR-variant non-squamous NSCLC after progression on EGFR-TKI therapy; 161 patients were assigned to each arm. This is a distinct underlying study from the global HARMONi trial already indexed in the Atlas: HARMONi-A is NCT05184712, whereas global HARMONi is NCT06396065. They must not be merged as aliases or follow-up publications.

Randomization was 1:1. Stratification included prior third-generation EGFR-TKI exposure and brain-metastasis status. The primary endpoint was PFS assessed by an independent radiology review committee; OS was a key secondary endpoint tested hierarchically. The 2026 JAMA paper reports final OS follow-up (median 32.5 months).

Treatment and comparator

  • Experimental: ivonescimab (AK112) 20 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks plus pemetrexed 500 mg/m² and carboplatin (AUC 5) every 3 weeks for four cycles, followed by maintenance ivonescimab plus pemetrexed.
  • Comparator: placebo plus the same pemetrexed–carboplatin regimen and maintenance pemetrexed.

Ivonescimab is a PD-1/VEGF bispecific antibody. The study addresses post-EGFR-TKI progression; it is not a first-line EGFR-mutant NSCLC study.

Key efficacy outcomes

In the primary PFS report, median PFS was 7.1 versus 4.8 months (HR 0.46, 95% CI 0.36–0.60; P<0.001). Objective response was 50.6% versus 35.4% (P=.006). The final OS analysis reported median OS of 16.8 versus 14.1 months (stratified HR 0.74, 95% CI 0.58–0.95; P=.02) after median follow-up of 32.5 months.

Absolute effect and NNT/NNH

The 30-month Kaplan–Meier OS estimates were 29.1% versus 18.4%, an absolute survival difference of 10.7 percentage points. The 30-month landmark NNT is 10 (ceil[100/10.7]=10) for one additional patient alive at 30 months from randomization. This is a fixed-time survival NNT, not an estimate from median OS or the hazard ratio.

No NNH is calculated. Grade ≥3 adverse-event rates are cumulative over follow-up and the publication does not provide a common fixed-time toxicity risk difference suitable for an NNH.

Toxicity, treatment burden and quality of life

In the final OS report, grade ≥3 treatment-emergent adverse events occurred in 67.1% with ivonescimab plus chemotherapy and 54.7% with placebo plus chemotherapy. The primary PFS report also described grade ≥3 immune-related events (6.2% vs 2.5%) and VEGF-related events (3.1% vs 2.5%). Treatment included four cycles of platinum doublet followed by maintenance, with additional bispecific-antibody infusions every three weeks.

Quantitative quality-of-life results were not reported in the accessible primary reports. Treatment discontinuation and patient-reported outcomes require full supplement review before any more granular claim is added.

Interpretation and limitations

  • The final OS analysis supports a survival benefit in the studied Chinese post-EGFR-TKI population, alongside the previously reported PFS benefit.
  • The study was conducted in China; external validity, local access and regulatory status should be assessed separately.
  • The comparator was platinum–pemetrexed, not every contemporary post-osimertinib option. This is not a head-to-head comparison with amivantamab plus chemotherapy or other targeted strategies.
  • Do not conflate HARMONi-A (NCT05184712) with global HARMONi (NCT06396065); their populations, registries and evidence packages are distinct.
  • QoL and detailed discontinuation data need full supplement review.

Clinical interpretation and current-practice context · reviewed 16 November 2026

HARMONi-A is a positive phase III post-EGFR-TKI study with a statistically significant final OS result. Its evidence should be considered in the context of the studied population, regional availability and the evolving post-osimertinib standard. It does not establish interchangeability with the separate global HARMONi program. NCCN full-guideline recommendation category and exact reference mapping were not verified in this run.
