Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 322 adults with locally advanced/metastatic EGFR-variant nonsquamous NSCLC after EGFR-TKI progression randomized 1:1: ivonescimab n=161 and placebo n=161.

Intervention: Ivonescimab 20 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks plus pemetrexed and carboplatin every 3 weeks for four cycles, followed by maintenance ivonescimab plus pemetrexed.

Comparator: Placebo plus the same pemetrexed/carboplatin induction and placebo/pemetrexed maintenance.

Primary endpoint: PFS by independent radiology review; OS was the key secondary endpoint in the final follow-up report.

Follow-up and maturity: Original PFS report in 2024; final OS report published 17 June 2026 after median follow-up 32.5 months.

Registry: NCT05184712. The primary analysis population and denominators are stated explicitly.

Final OS: median 16.8 versus 14.1 months; HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.58–0.95), P=0.02. Thirty-month OS 29.1% (95% CI 22.1–36.4) versus 18.4% (12.8–24.8), absolute difference 10.7 points (NNT 10). Original PFS analysis showed significant PFS improvement; keep that estimate attached to the 2024 primary report.

Safety and absolute effect

Final OS report: grade ≥3 TEAEs 67.1% versus 54.7%. Original report: grade ≥3 TEAEs 61.5% versus 49.1%, grade ≥3 immune-related AEs 6.2% versus 2.5%, and grade ≥3 VEGF-related AEs 3.1% versus 2.5%.

NNT 10

29.1% versus 18.4%; absolute benefit 10.7 percentage points at 30 months. Alive at 30 months (Kaplan–Meier estimate). Calculation: ceil(100 / (29.1 − 18.4)) = 10.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage reported.

Interpretation and limitations

HARMONi-A is distinct from HARMONi. Ivonescimab plus chemotherapy improved PFS and final OS in EGFR-variant NSCLC after EGFR-TKI progression; toxicity was higher than chemotherapy alone.

China-based study, post-EGFR-TKI population; final OS is a secondary endpoint. Do not confuse HARMONi-A with the separate global HARMONi trial.
