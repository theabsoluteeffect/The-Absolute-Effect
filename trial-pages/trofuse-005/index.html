Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 776 patients randomized: sacituzumab tirumotecan n=386 and physician's-choice treatment n=390. At least one dose was received by 384/386 and 367/390, respectively.

Intervention: Sacituzumab tirumotecan (sac-TMT) 6 mg/kg IV on days 1 and 8 every 3 weeks.

Comparator: Physician's choice of single-agent doxorubicin or paclitaxel.

Primary endpoint: Dual primary endpoints: PFS by blinded independent central review and OS, tested with prespecified alpha allocation.

Follow-up and maturity: Median follow-up 14.0 months (IQR 11.0–16.9); ongoing follow-up for longer-term OS and safety.

Registry: NCT06132958. The primary analysis population and denominators are stated explicitly.

Median PFS 7.2 months (95% CI 5.6–7.6) versus 3.9 (3.7–5.3); HR 0.59 (0.50–0.70), P<0.0001. Median OS 19.0 months (16.1–NR) versus 12.1 (11.0–15.2); HR 0.68 (0.55–0.84), P=0.0001. ORR 35.1% versus 17.3%.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 265/384 (69%) versus 210/367 (57%); serious AEs 35% versus 32%. Treatment-related deaths occurred in 3 patients in each group. Anaemia was a common grade ≥3 toxicity.

No NNT is calculated: Median PFS and OS are time-to-event medians; no fixed-time absolute event-free rates are used for NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

QoL was described as maintained in the available report; detailed patient-reported outcome estimates should be extracted from the full paper before release.

Interpretation and limitations

TroFuse-005 demonstrated PFS and OS improvement over physician-choice doxorubicin/paclitaxel after platinum and immunotherapy. It is a major practice-changing phase III result, but longer follow-up and detailed QoL remain relevant.

Follow-up is relatively short and OS is not yet mature despite a significant interim/primary analysis. ADC-related cytopenia and treatment burden are important.
