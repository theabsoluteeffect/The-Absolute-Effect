TroFuse-005

Post-platinum advanced or recurrent endometrial carcinoma. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 776 patients with advanced or recurrent endometrial cancer after platinum-based chemotherapy; randomized 1:1. Prior immune-checkpoint therapy was permitted and represented in the study population.

Intervention: Sacituzumab tirumotecan 4 mg/kg intravenously every 2 weeks.

Comparator: Investigator's choice chemotherapy: paclitaxel, docetaxel, doxorubicin or gemcitabine according to protocol.

Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival by blinded independent central review; overall survival was a key secondary endpoint.

Follow-up and maturity: Primary report includes an interim overall-survival analysis; median follow-up was approximately 14 months.

Registry: NCT06132958. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

Median PFS 7.2 versus 3.9 months; HR 0.59 (95% CI 0.49–0.71), P<0.0001. Median OS 19.0 versus 12.1 months; HR 0.68 (95% CI 0.54–0.86), P=0.0011. ORR was approximately 31% versus 12%.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 treatment-emergent adverse events occurred in 69% versus 57%; neutropenia, anemia and gastrointestinal toxicity were clinically important. ADC-related toxicity and treatment discontinuation require attention.

No NNT is calculated because Only median time-to-event outcomes and hazard ratios are used here; no same-horizon absolute survival rates were verified for a valid NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

Patient-reported outcomes were collected; a quantitative quality-of-life advantage was not established in the primary abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

The trial demonstrates a clinically meaningful PFS and interim OS advantage over single-agent chemotherapy in previously treated advanced endometrial cancer. It does not establish superiority over other ADCs or define sequencing after every contemporary first-line regimen.

Open-label design, investigator-choice chemotherapy comparators and evolving use of prior checkpoint inhibitors limit cross-trial comparisons. The OS analysis should be interpreted at its stated data cutoff.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
