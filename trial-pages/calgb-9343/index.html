The Absolute Effect / Evidence / CALGB 9343
Breast Cancer · Radiation Oncology · RT Omission

CALGB 9343

Clinical question

In women ≥70 with T1N0 ER-positive breast cancer after lumpectomy and endocrine therapy, can RT be omitted?

Trial context
Population
636 women ≥70 years, T1N0M0, ER-positive breast cancer
Intervention
Tamoxifen + RT vs tamoxifen alone after lumpectomy
Key endpoint
10-year freedom from locoregional recurrence
Time horizon / follow-up
10 years
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

RT reduced 10-year locoregional recurrence from about 10% to 2%, an absolute reduction of 8 percentage points (NNT 13). There was no significant difference in distant metastasis, breast-cancer-specific survival or overall survival. NNT is based on 10-year freedom from locoregional recurrence.

NNT: 13

Treatment burden / harm

RT-related toxicity

NNH: Not quantifiable from the reported endpoint; toxicity described in text

Relative effect

HR: 0.18

95% CI 0.07–0.42

Clinical interpretation

Interpret the absolute difference together with the endpoint, time horizon, relative effect and limitations of the study design.
