Breast Cancer · Radiation Oncology · RT Omission

CALGB 9343

Clinical question

In women ≥70 with T1N0 ER-positive breast cancer after lumpectomy and endocrine therapy, can RT be omitted?

Trial at a glance

Population

636 women ≥70 years with T1N0M0, ER-positive breast cancer

Intervention / comparator

Tamoxifen + RT vs tamoxifen alone after lumpectomy

Endpoint

10-year freedom from locoregional recurrence

Time horizon

10 years

Absolute Effect

RT reduced 10-year locoregional recurrence from about 10% to 2%: absolute reduction 8 percentage points; NNT 13.

Relative Effect

HR 0.18 (95% CI 0.07–0.42).

Harm / treatment burden

RT-related toxicity was reported descriptively; no defensible comparative NNH from the selected endpoint.

Absolute Effect interpretation

RT substantially improves local control, but in this older low-risk population it did not improve distant metastasis, breast-cancer-specific survival or overall survival.

NNT/NNH is reported only when the endpoint, time horizon and study design make that calculation defensible. Median time-to-event differences are not converted into NNT.
