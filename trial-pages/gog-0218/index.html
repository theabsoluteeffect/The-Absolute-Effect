GOG-0218

Does concurrent and maintenance bevacizumab improve outcomes when added to carboplatin–paclitaxel in advanced ovarian cancer?

One underlying study; aliases and follow-up reports consolidated. Primary discipline: Medical.

Population and eligibility

1,873 patients with incompletely resected stage III or stage IV epithelial ovarian, fallopian-tube or primary peritoneal cancer.

Newly diagnosed advanced disease after cytoreductive surgery.

Sample size

1,873 randomised/evaluable as reported.

Intervention

Carboplatin AUC 6 plus paclitaxel 175 mg/m² for six cycles; bevacizumab 15 mg/kg every 3 weeks from cycle 2 through cycle 22.

Comparator

Carboplatin–paclitaxel plus placebo; a third arm received bevacizumab during chemotherapy only.

Endpoints and results

Primary PFS; OS and toxicity secondary.

  • Median PFS 14.1 vs 10.3 months for bevacizumab continued through maintenance vs control (HR 0.717, 95% CI 0.625–0.824; p<0.001).
  • Original overall-population analysis did not establish significant OS benefit.
  • Hypertension requiring treatment 22.9% vs 7.2%; gastrointestinal-wall disruption requiring intervention 2.6% vs 1.2%.

Toxicity, discontinuation and quality of life

Trial-specific harms: Hypertension and GI perforation/disruption are important; also monitor bleeding, thrombosis, wound healing and proteinuria.

Treatment burden/discontinuation: Maintenance requires prolonged infusions and monitoring; exact discontinuation rates require the full report.

Quality of life: No numerical QoL advantage claimed.

Subgroups, maturity and limitations

Subgroups: Potential variation by baseline risk/residual disease should not be asserted without formal interaction evidence.

Maturity: Long-term follow-up available; original overall-population analysis did not establish OS benefit.

Limitations: Predates current PARP maintenance and molecular stratification; PFS benefit is not OS superiority.

Clinical interpretation

GOG-0218 showed a PFS benefit from bevacizumab continued into maintenance, without significant OS benefit in the original overall population.

Current-practice context must be checked against regional guidelines, patient fitness, biomarkers, alternatives and regulatory status before clinical application. Clinical freshness review and browser-rendered QA remain pending.
