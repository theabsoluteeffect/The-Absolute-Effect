In previously untreated unresectable malignant pleural mesothelioma, does nivolumab plus ipilimumab improve overall survival over platinum-pemetrexed chemotherapy?

Registry: NCT02899299 · Evidence reviewed 9 November 2026 · Aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase III randomized open-label; n=605.Primary-source evidence summary

Study design, population and eligibility

Design and sample

Phase III randomized open-label; n=605.

Population

Previously untreated unresectable MPM, ECOG 0–1.

Treatment and comparator

Intervention: Nivolumab + ipilimumab up to 2 years.

Comparator: Up to six cycles platinum + pemetrexed.

Endpoints

OS primary.

Key efficacy and clinical outcomes

Median OS 18.1 vs 14.1 mo (HR 0.74); 3-y OS 23% vs 15%.
