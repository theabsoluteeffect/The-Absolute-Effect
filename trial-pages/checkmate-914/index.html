CheckMate 914

Clinical question

Does adjuvant nivolumab plus ipilimumab improve disease-free survival after nephrectomy for high-risk RCC?

Trial context
Population
816 patients with localized RCC at high risk of recurrence after nephrectomy
Intervention
Adjuvant nivolumab + ipilimumab vs placebo after nephrectomy
Key endpoint
24-month disease-free survival
Time horizon / follow-up
24 months
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

At 24 months, disease-free survival was 76% with nivolumab plus ipilimumab versus 74% with placebo, an absolute difference of only 2 percentage points (NNT 50). The primary DFS endpoint was not met. Grade 3–5 adverse events were 38% vs 10%, and treatment discontinuation was 32% vs 2%.

NNT: 50

Treatment burden / harm

Grade 3–5 adverse events

NNH: 4

Relative effect

HR: 0.92

95% CI 0.71–1.19; P=.53

Clinical interpretation

Interpret the absolute difference together with the endpoint, time horizon, relative effect and limitations of the study design.
