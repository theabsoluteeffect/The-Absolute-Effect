German Testicular Cancer Study Group AH 01/94

Clinical question

In stage I non-seminoma, does one cycle of BEP provide better disease control than RPLND?

Trial context
Population
382 patients with clinical stage I non-seminomatous germ-cell tumours
Intervention
One cycle BEP vs nerve-sparing RPLND
Key endpoint
Recurrence-free survival
Time horizon / follow-up
Mean follow-up 4.7 years
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

One cycle of BEP produced fewer recurrences than RPLND in this randomized trial: 1.15% vs 7.5%, an absolute reduction of 6.35 percentage points (NNT 16). Tumour-specific survival was 100% in both groups. NNT is based on recurrence by approximately 4.7 years.

NNT: 16

Treatment burden / harm

Treatment-specific morbidity

NNH: Not quantifiable from the primary publication; RPLND carries operative morbidity while BEP carries chemotherapy-related toxicity

Relative effect

HR: Recurrence 1.15% vs 7.5%

P=.0033; tumour-specific survival 100% in both arms

Clinical interpretation

Interpret the absolute difference together with the endpoint, time horizon, relative effect and limitations of the study design.
