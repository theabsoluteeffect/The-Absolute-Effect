The Absolute Effect / Evidence

ASSURE

Renal Cell Carcinoma · Adjuvant Therapy

Clinical question

Does adjuvant sunitinib or sorafenib improve disease-free or overall survival after nephrectomy for high-risk RCC?

Trial context

ASSURE was a large randomized adjuvant RCC trial evaluating targeted therapy after nephrectomy. It is an important negative study because it tested whether postoperative VEGF-targeted therapy could reduce recurrence in patients at elevated risk.

Absolute Effect perspective

The clinical interpretation depends on the reported disease-free and overall-survival estimates together with treatment toxicity and discontinuation burden.
