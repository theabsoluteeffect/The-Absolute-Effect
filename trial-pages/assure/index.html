The Absolute Effect

ASSURE

Renal Cell Carcinoma · Adjuvant Therapy

Clinical question

Does adjuvant sunitinib or sorafenib improve disease-free or overall survival after nephrectomy for high-risk RCC?

Key result

ASSURE did not demonstrate a disease-free or overall survival benefit for adjuvant sunitinib or sorafenib compared with placebo after nephrectomy.

Absolute Effect perspective

This is an important negative adjuvant RCC trial. The absence of benefit, together with treatment toxicity, illustrates why relative-risk reasoning alone should not lead to routine postoperative targeted therapy.
