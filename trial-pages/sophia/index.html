Breast Cancer · Medical · Relapsed / Refractory

SOPHIA

In pretreated HER2-positive advanced breast cancer, does margetuximab plus chemotherapy improve outcomes versus trastuzumab plus chemotherapy?

Trial design and population

Population

536 patients with HER2-positive advanced breast cancer previously treated with ≥2 anti-HER2 regimens in metastatic setting, including pertuzumab and T-DM1.

Intervention vs comparator

Margetuximab 15 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks plus investigator-selected chemotherapy vs trastuzumab plus the same chemotherapy.

Sample size

536

Primary endpoint

PFS; OS key secondary endpoint

Key results

Median PFS was 5.8 vs 4.9 months (HR 0.76, 95% CI 0.59–0.98; P=.03). Final median OS was 21.6 vs 21.9 months (HR 0.95, 95% CI 0.77–1.17); no OS improvement was demonstrated.

Absolute Effect

Median PFS difference 0.9 months is not an NNT. OS was not improved.

NNT: Not calculated; median PFS difference is not a valid NNT. Endpoint/time horizon are stated above; do not convert median survival differences into NNT.

NNH: Not quantifiable. Infusion-related reactions were more common with margetuximab; grade ≥3 AEs largely reflected chemotherapy and advanced disease. Modest PFS gain must be weighed against infusion burden and absence of OS benefit.

Safety and treatment burden

Infusion-related reactions were more common with margetuximab; grade ≥3 AEs largely reflected chemotherapy and advanced disease. Modest PFS gain must be weighed against infusion burden and absence of OS benefit.

Follow-up, maturity and limitations

Final OS analysis with approximately 30 months follow-up.

Compared with trastuzumab plus chemotherapy, not newer ADCs. Treatment landscape has changed with trastuzumab deruxtecan and tucatinib-based regimens.

Clinical interpretation

Margetuximab produced a modest PFS improvement but no OS advantage over trastuzumab in heavily pretreated HER2-positive disease.

Current-practice context · reviewed 20 October 2026

Historical evidence is separated from current sequencing. Confirm local regulatory status, current guidelines, biomarkers, prior therapies and patient-specific contraindications before applying these results. Newer treatments may have changed the preferred sequence; where NNT/NNH cannot be defensibly quantified, no surrogate calculation is made.
