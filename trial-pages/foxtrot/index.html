Colorectal Cancer · Medical

FOxTROT

Does preoperative oxaliplatin-fluoropyrimidine chemotherapy improve outcomes versus postoperative chemotherapy alone in operable locally advanced colon cancer?

Trial design and population

Population: 1,053 patients with radiologically staged T3–4, N0–2, M0 operable colon cancer.

Treatment vs comparator: Six weeks preoperative oxaliplatin-fluoropyrimidine followed by 18 postoperative weeks vs 24 postoperative weeks.

Registry: ISRCTN87163246

Key results

Residual/recurrent disease by 2 years 16.9% vs 21.5% (rate ratio 0.72, 95% CI 0.54–0.98; p=0.037). Complete resection 94% vs 89%. Approximate NNT 22 to avoid one residual/recurrent event by 2 years.

Absolute effect

Endpoint/time horizon: Residual or recurrent disease · 2 years. NNT is not calculated from median survival or hazard ratios; use fixed-time absolute risks only. NNH not calculated without a defensible fixed-time excess-harm risk difference.

Safety, burden and QoL

About 4.3% allocated to neoadjuvant treatment developed obstructive symptoms requiring expedited surgery. Also consider neuropathy, cytopenias, diarrhoea and surgical delay.

QoL and discontinuation percentages are not asserted without full source-table verification; source-level QoL/toxicity audit remains a QC item.

Subgroups, maturity and limitations

Selective strategy for radiologically locally advanced disease, not all operable colon cancers. NNT is approximate and specific to the 2-year endpoint.

Distinguish prespecified from exploratory subgroup analyses and mature from immature endpoints. Verify current guideline context before applying historical trial findings.

Current-practice context · 21 October 2026
