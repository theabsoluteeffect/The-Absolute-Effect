Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: Two nonrandomized cohorts: 11 patients received ivonescimab monotherapy (10 efficacy-evaluable) and 79 received ivonescimab plus chemotherapy (67 efficacy-evaluable).

Intervention: Ivonescimab 20 mg/kg intravenously every 3 weeks alone in cohort 1 or with protocol-defined platinum-doublet chemotherapy in cohort 2; surgery and protocol-directed adjuvant ivonescimab followed.

Comparator: No randomized control arm; separate monotherapy and chemo-combination cohorts.

Primary endpoint: Major pathological response.

Follow-up and maturity: Pathological response and perioperative safety reported; DFS/OS follow-up is ongoing.

Registry: NCT05247684. Arm denominators and endpoint definitions are retained from the primary report; no missing denominator is inferred.

MPR was 60.0% (6/10) in the monotherapy cohort and 70.1% (47/67) in the chemo-combination cohort. These are single-arm cohort estimates, not a randomized comparison.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time effects are shown only when the primary source supplies a comparable endpoint and horizon.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events occurred in 4/11 (36.4%) in cohort 1 and 24/79 (30.4%) in cohort 2. No grade ≥3 hemorrhage or treatment-related death was reported; surgery delay was uncommon.

No NNT is calculated: the study uses time-to-event medians, single-arm cohorts or continuous outcomes without a comparable fixed-time absolute risk difference.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No comparative QoL outcome was reported.

Interpretation and limitations

The combination cohort showed a high MPR rate with manageable reported perioperative safety, but randomized confirmation and mature EFS/OS are required.

Nonrandomized phase II cohorts, small monotherapy cohort and pathological response as a surrogate. Exact chemotherapy backbone and dose require protocol/supplement confirmation.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The source hierarchy and analysis population are explicit.
