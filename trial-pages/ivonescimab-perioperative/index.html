Resectable non-small-cell lung cancer · Medical Oncology · Phase II

Perioperative ivonescimab in resectable NSCLC

Neoadjuvant/perioperative. Full primary article and protocol details reviewed 17 November 2026.

DesignNonrandomized, noncomparative, open-label multicentre phase II; original exploratory randomization followed by cohort expansion
PopulationCohort 1 n=11; cohort 2 n=79
Primary endpointMajor pathological response in the neoadjuvant cohort.
Follow-upPathological response and perioperative safety reported; DFS/OS follow-up ongoing.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: Cohort 1 included 11 patients treated with ivonescimab monotherapy (10 efficacy-evaluable). Cohort 2 included 79 patients treated with ivonescimab plus chemotherapy (67 efficacy-evaluable). Cohort 2 was expanded nonrandomly after the initial exploratory phase.

Intervention: Cohort 1: ivonescimab 20 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks for 3–4 neoadjuvant cycles, surgery, then 20 mg/kg every 3 weeks for 16 adjuvant cycles. Cohort 2: ivonescimab 20 or 30 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks plus cisplatin 75–100 mg/m² or carboplatin AUC 5–6 and paclitaxel 175–200 mg/m² for 3–4 cycles, then surgery and adjuvant ivonescimab 20 or 30 mg/kg for 16 cycles. The dose in cohort 2 was increased from 20 to 30 mg/kg during later enrollment.

Comparator: No randomized standard-of-care control; two cohorts are not directly comparative.

Primary endpoint: Major pathological response in the neoadjuvant cohort.

Follow-up and maturity: Pathological response and perioperative safety reported; DFS/OS follow-up ongoing.

Registry: NCT05247684

Key efficacy outcome

Major pathological response

Ivonescimab + chemotherapy (47/67)
70.1%
Ivonescimab monotherapy (6/10)
60.0%

MPR 47/67 (70.1%; 95% CI 57.7–80.7) in cohort 2 and 6/10 (60.0%; 26.2–87.8) in cohort 1. Among MPR responders, residual viable tumour ≤5% occurred in 46/47 (97.9%) and 5/6 (83.3%), respectively.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs occurred in 4/11 (36.4%) in cohort 1 and 24/79 (30.4%) in cohort 2; no grade ≥3 haemorrhage or treatment-related death. Treatment-related surgery delay occurred in 1/79 (1.3%). Ivonescimab-related surgery AEs occurred in 1/10 (10.0%) and 7/67 (10.4%), with no grade ≥3 surgical events.

No NNT is calculated: single-arm response outcomes and no randomized comparator do not support a treatment NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No comparative QoL outcome reported.

Interpretation and limitations

The full Nature Medicine article confirms the exact chemotherapy backbone and dose adaptation. Cohort 2 is an expanded nonrandomized cohort; its MPR cannot be compared causally with cohort 1.

Single-arm phase II, small monotherapy cohort and historical-control primary threshold. The dose change within cohort 2 and cohort expansion limit dose-response interpretation.

Current-practice context · reviewed 17 November 2026

These are investigational single-arm phase II strategies, not substitutes for established standard treatment. NCCN provenance is not verified because the complete guideline/reference list was inaccessible.
