Perioperative ivonescimab in resectable NSCLC

Perioperative treatment for resectable stage II–IIIA non-small-cell lung cancer. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignOpen-label, multicohort phase II study; non-comparative
PopulationApproximately 89 patients with resectable stage II–IIIA NSCLC treated in the perioperative study cohorts
Primary endpointMajor pathological response, with pathological complete response and surgical outcomes as key secondary endpoints.
Data maturityPathological response and perioperative safety analysis; long-term DFS/OS follow-up is ongoing.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: Approximately 89 patients with resectable stage II–IIIA NSCLC treated in the perioperative study cohorts; cohort-specific analyses and denominators are reported in the primary article.

Intervention: Ivonescimab 20 mg/kg intravenously every 3 weeks with platinum-doublet chemotherapy, followed by protocol-defined perioperative/adjuvant treatment.

Comparator: No randomized control arm; single-arm cohort design.

Primary endpoint: Major pathological response, with pathological complete response and surgical outcomes as key secondary endpoints.

Follow-up and maturity: Pathological response and perioperative safety analysis; long-term DFS/OS follow-up is ongoing.

Registry: NCT05247684. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

Major pathological response was approximately 71.8% and pCR approximately 60.0% in the reported expanded cohort. These are single-arm pathological-response estimates, not comparative treatment effects.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events occurred in approximately one-third of patients; immune-mediated events and perioperative pneumonitis require attention. Surgical feasibility and timing are important.

No NNT is calculated because this is a single-arm or non-comparative study without a randomized comparator.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No comparative QoL result is available.

Interpretation and limitations

The findings support further randomized perioperative testing of ivonescimab plus chemotherapy; they do not establish superiority over established perioperative immunotherapy regimens.

Non-randomized phase II design, cohort amendments and modest sample size. Pathological response cannot substitute for mature EFS/OS.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
