OptiTROP-Lung06

Clinical question: In previously untreated locally advanced or metastatic nonsquamous NSCLC with PD-L1 TPS below 1% and no targetable genomic alteration, does sacituzumab tirumotecan plus pembrolizumab improve outcomes compared with platinum-pemetrexed plus pembrolizumab?

Prespecified interim analysis; median follow-up 9.7 months. Median BICR-assessed PFS was 15.0 versus 5.7 months (HR 0.49, 95% CI 0.36–0.65; p<0.001). OS remains immature.

Registration: NCT06711900

Design, population and treatment

Phase IIIOpen-label, multicentre, randomised 1:1
455Sacituzumab tirumotecan n=229; control n=226
81 centresChina; stratified by sex, ECOG performance status and disease stage

Eligibility: Stage IIIB/IIIC not suitable for definitive chemoradiotherapy or stage IV nonsquamous NSCLC; no prior systemic therapy for advanced disease; no sensitizing EGFR or ALK alteration or other targetable genomic alteration; central PD-L1 TPS <1% (22C3); ECOG performance status 0–1.

Experimental: Sacituzumab tirumotecan 4 mg/kg IV every 2 weeks plus pembrolizumab 400 mg IV every 6 weeks, for treatment until progression or unacceptable toxicity; pembrolizumab for up to 18 cycles.

Comparator: Pemetrexed 500 mg/m² plus carboplatin AUC 5 or cisplatin 75 mg/m² every 3 weeks for up to four platinum doses, with pembrolizumab 400 mg every 6 weeks; maintenance pemetrexed plus pembrolizumab thereafter. Pembrolizumab was limited to 18 cycles.

Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival by blinded independent central review (RECIST 1.1). Secondary endpoints included OS, investigator-assessed PFS, ORR, disease control, duration of response and safety.

Safety

Grade 3 or higher treatment-emergent adverse events occurred in 47.6% with sacituzumab tirumotecan plus pembrolizumab and 60.6% with chemotherapy plus pembrolizumab.

Reported grade 3 or higher events included neutrophil-count decrease (19.7% vs 28.8%), white-cell-count decrease (9.6% vs 16.4%), anaemia (6.1% vs 20.4%) and platelet-count decrease (3.9% vs 14.6%). Grade 3 or higher stomatitis occurred in 2.2% with sacituzumab tirumotecan; stomatitis overall was predominantly grade 1–2.

No NNT/NNH is assigned to cumulative adverse-event proportions because treatment exposure and follow-up are variable; the absolute rates are displayed directly.

Patient-reported outcomes and maturity

Numerical quality-of-life outcomes and detailed treatment-discontinuation data are not stated in the accessible primary abstract. OS is immature after median follow-up of 9.7 months; no OS p value or mature survival estimate is claimed.

Interpretation and limitations

In this PD-L1-negative, driver-negative nonsquamous NSCLC population, sacituzumab tirumotecan plus pembrolizumab substantially prolonged median PFS versus platinum-pemetrexed plus pembrolizumab in the prespecified interim analysis. Response rates were similar, while response duration was longer in the experimental arm and grade ≥3 adverse events were less frequent.

Interpretation remains limited by short follow-up and immature OS. The trial was conducted in China, so external generalisability requires consideration. The comparator was chemoimmunotherapy; the study does not establish benefit in PD-L1-positive disease or in molecularly targetable NSCLC.

Guideline provenance: Exact current NCCN guideline/version/section/reference was not verified; no NCCN recommendation is claimed.
