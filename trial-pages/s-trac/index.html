Renal Cell Carcinoma · Medical Oncology · Adjuvant

S-TRAC

Clinical question

Does adjuvant sunitinib improve disease-free survival after nephrectomy in high-risk clear-cell RCC?

Trial context
Population
615 patients with high-risk clear-cell RCC after nephrectomy
Intervention
Adjuvant sunitinib vs placebo after nephrectomy
Key endpoint
5-year disease-free survival
Time horizon / follow-up
5 years
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

At 5 years, disease-free survival was 59.3% with sunitinib versus 51.3% with placebo, an absolute benefit of 8 percentage points (NNT 13). Median DFS was 6.8 vs 5.6 years. The DFS benefit came with substantial toxicity and treatment discontinuation.

NNT: 13

Treatment burden / harm

Grade 3 treatment-emergent adverse events

NNH: 4

Relative effect

HR: 0.76

95% CI 0.59–0.98; P=.03

Clinical interpretation

Interpret the absolute difference together with the endpoint, time horizon, relative effect and limitations of the study design.
