Design, population and treatment

Design: Open-label, randomized phase III trial.

Eligibility and population: 245 patients with stage III epithelial ovarian cancer without progression after at least three cycles of neoadjuvant carboplatin/paclitaxel and considered suitable for interval cytoreductive surgery.

Intervention: Interval cytoreductive surgery plus HIPEC with cisplatin 100 mg/m² heated intraperitoneally for approximately 90 minutes; detailed perfusion temperature, carrier and sodium-thiosulfate nephroprotection protocol require protocol/supplement recheck.

Comparator: Interval cytoreductive surgery alone.

Randomization and endpoint: Recurrence-free survival. The full randomization stratification variables and protocol amendments remain subject to protocol-level reconciliation before release.

Key efficacy outcome

Median RFS 14.2 versus 10.7 months (HR 0.66, 95% CI 0.50–0.87; p=0.003). Median OS 45.7 versus 33.9 months (HR 0.67, 95% CI 0.48–0.94; p=0.02). The long-term follow-up reported persistent RFS/OS separation; exact table-level estimates remain to be reconciled.

Data maturity: Primary analysis at median follow-up approximately 4.7 years; a later long-term analysis at approximately 10 years is linked as follow-up. Primary versus secondary endpoints are kept distinct; no survival estimate is inferred from a hazard ratio or median alone.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3–4 adverse events were approximately 25% versus 27% (p=0.76) in the primary report. Renal toxicity, postoperative morbidity and perfusion protocol details require full supplement review.

Delivery and discontinuation: HIPEC adds a 90-minute heated intraperitoneal cisplatin procedure to interval surgery and requires anaesthetic, renal-protection and specialist perfusion infrastructure.

Absolute effect and NNT / NNH

No NNT/NNH is calculated. The primary endpoint is reported as a median survival/PFS estimate or a non-inferiority comparison without a valid same-horizon absolute event difference; NNT/NNH is not derived from median survival or a hazard ratio.

Only same-endpoint, same-time-horizon absolute differences are eligible for NNT/NNH. Median survival and hazard ratios alone are not used. No NNT/NNH appropriate from the reported primary endpoint.

Interpretation and limitations

OVHIPEC-1 reported improved RFS and OS with cisplatin HIPEC at interval cytoreduction in a selected stage III population. Reproducibility depends on patient selection, complete cytoreduction, surgical expertise and protocol-level renal protection.

Limitations: The trial applies to selected stage III patients responding to neoadjuvant chemotherapy and undergoing interval surgery; it does not establish a role for HIPEC at primary debulking or in all ovarian cancer. No NNT from median RFS/OS alone.
