ALTA-1L

First-line ALK-positive advanced NSCLC

Registry: NCT02737501 · Evidence reviewed 9 November 2026 · Aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase III randomized; n=275.Primary-source evidence summary

Study design, population and eligibility

Design and sample

Phase III randomized; n=275.

Population

ALK-inhibitor-naive locally advanced/metastatic ALK-positive NSCLC.

Treatment and comparator

Intervention: Brigatinib 90 mg daily 7-day lead-in then 180 mg daily.

Comparator: Crizotinib 250 mg twice daily.

Endpoints

PFS by BICR primary.

Key efficacy and clinical outcomes

At second interim: median PFS 24.0 vs 11.0 mo (HR 0.49); final analysis 3-y PFS 43% vs 19%.

Toxicity and treatment burden

Early pulmonary events, hypertension, CPK/lipase elevation and hepatotoxicity; follow lead-in dosing.

Absolute effect and NNT/NNH

Crizotinib comparator; cross-trial comparisons with CROWN are invalid.

NNT/NNH is not calculated unless a directly reported, comparable fixed-time risk difference supports it. Median survival and hazard ratios alone do not provide an NNT.

Critical interpretation and limitations

Crizotinib comparator; cross-trial comparisons with CROWN are invalid.
