Clinical question: In second-line pembrolizumab in asian patients after sorafenib/oxaliplatin-based chemotherapy, what is the efficacy and safety of the evaluated intervention?

Registry: NCT03062358 · One underlying study; aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase III, double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT; 453 Asian patients. Evidence cut-off: 13 November 2026.

Design, population and eligibility

Design / sample size

Phase III, double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT; 453 Asian patients.

Eligibility

Advanced HCC previously treated with sorafenib or oxaliplatin-based chemotherapy.

Intervention

Pembrolizumab versus placebo, both with best supportive care.

Pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every 3 weeks for up to 35 cycles.

Comparator

placebo, both with best supportive care.

Primary endpoint: OS primary; PFS and ORR secondary.

Key efficacy outcomes

Median OS 14.6 vs 13.0 months, HR 0.79 (95% CI 0.63–0.99), p=0.018. PFS 2.6 vs 2.3 months, HR 0.74 (0.60–0.92); ORR 12.7% vs 1.3%.

14.6

Safety and treatment delivery

Pembrolizumab TRAEs grade 3 12.0%, grade 4 1.3%, grade 5 1.0%; placebo grade 3 5.9%, no grade 4/5 TRAEs.

Clinical interpretation and limitations

Positive phase III result in an Asian population and distinct from KEYNOTE-240. Regional aetiology, prior treatment and access context should be considered when generalizing.

Interpret the reported hazard ratios in the context of the prespecified endpoint, statistical hierarchy, follow-up maturity, crossover/subsequent therapy and enrolled population. Median survival differences are not risk differences and must not be converted into NNT.

Absolute effect

No NNT/NNH is displayed unless an absolute event-risk difference at a clearly stated fixed timepoint can be calculated from reported trial data. Median survival differences and hazard ratios alone do not support an NNT. The visual above uses reported outcome data only.
