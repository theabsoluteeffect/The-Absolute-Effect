EV-301

Advanced urothelial carcinoma after platinum and PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor

Registry: NCT03478592 · Evidence reviewed 9 November 2026 · Aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase III open-label randomized; n=608.Primary-source evidence summary

Study design, population and eligibility

Design and sample

Phase III open-label randomized; n=608.

Population

Locally advanced/metastatic UC after platinum chemotherapy and PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor.

Treatment and comparator

Intervention: Enfortumab vedotin days 1,8,15 q28d.

Comparator: Docetaxel, paclitaxel or vinflunine.

Endpoints

OS primary; PFS/ORR secondary.

Key efficacy and clinical outcomes

Median OS 12.88 vs 8.97 mo (HR .70); 12-mo OS 51.5% vs 39.2%.

Toxicity and treatment burden

Rash, peripheral neuropathy, hyperglycaemia, ocular toxicity; rare severe skin reactions/pneumonitis.

Absolute effect and NNT/NNH

Distinct from EV-302 first-line enfortumab-pembrolizumab and perioperative EV-303/304.
