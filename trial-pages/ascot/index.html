Biliary Tract Cancer · Medical · Adjuvant / Postoperative

ASCOT (JCOG1202)

After curative resection of biliary tract cancer, does adjuvant S-1 improve survival compared with observation?

Trial design and population

Population / eligibility
440 patients with resected biliary tract cancer in Japan.
Intervention vs comparator
Oral S-1, body-surface-area adjusted, twice daily for 4 weeks followed by 2 weeks off, for 4 cycles vs observation.
Sample size
440
Primary endpoint
Overall survival

Key results

At 3 years, overall survival was 77.1% with S-1 vs 67.6% with observation (HR 0.694; 95% CI 0.514–0.935; one-sided p=0.008).

Safety and treatment burden

Grade 3–4 biliary tract infection 7.2%, diarrhoea 2.9%, appetite loss 2.9%, fatigue 2.9% in S-1 arm; toxicity comparisons require full trial table.

Follow-up, maturity and limitations

Japanese population and S-1 regimen; regional availability and practice differ. The 3-year absolute difference is a Kaplan–Meier estimate, so NNT is approximate and time-specific.

Clinical interpretation

ASCOT provides randomized phase III evidence for adjuvant S-1 after resection in Japan. It complements, rather than directly compares against, BILCAP; do not assume cross-trial equivalence.
