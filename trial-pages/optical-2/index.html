Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 123 patients with locally advanced rectal cancer randomized across three arms (41 patients per arm): cadonilimab plus mFOLFOXIRI, mFOLFOXIRI alone and mFOLFOX6.

Intervention: Cadonilimab 10 mg/kg intravenously every 3 weeks plus protocol-defined mFOLFOXIRI; exact chemotherapy component doses and cycle count require full protocol/supplement reconciliation before release.

Comparator: Two randomized control arms: mFOLFOXIRI alone and mFOLFOX6.

Primary endpoint: Pathological complete response at surgery.

Follow-up and maturity: Pathological response at resection; longer-term DFS and organ-preservation outcomes remain immature.

Registry: NCT05571644. Arm denominators and endpoint definitions are preserved as reported; no missing denominator is inferred.

pCR was 26.8% with cadonilimab plus mFOLFOXIRI, 14.6% with mFOLFOXIRI alone and 9.8% with mFOLFOX6. The prespecified primary contrast with mFOLFOX6 yields an absolute difference of 17.0 percentage points (NNT 6); the three-arm structure must be retained.

Interpretation and limitations

OPTICAL-2 suggests higher pCR with cadonilimab plus mFOLFOXIRI versus mFOLFOX6, but the intermediate mFOLFOXIRI-only arm and regimen intensity are essential to interpretation.

Three-arm phase II study; the prior page omitted the mFOLFOXIRI-alone arm and overstated the sample size. Exact regimen/safety details remain a release hold.
