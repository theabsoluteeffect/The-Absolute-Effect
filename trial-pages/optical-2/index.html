Rectal cancer · Multimodality Oncology · Phase II

OPTICAL-2

Neoadjuvant systemic therapy for locally advanced rectal adenocarcinoma. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignRandomized phase II multi-arm trial
PopulationApproximately 150 patients with locally advanced rectal cancer randomized across neoadjuvant chemotherapy strategies.
Primary endpointPathological complete response at surgery.
Data maturityPathological response at resection; longer-term DFS and organ-preservation follow-up are required.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: Approximately 150 patients with locally advanced rectal cancer randomized across neoadjuvant chemotherapy strategies.

Intervention: Cadonilimab 10 mg/kg intravenously every 3 weeks plus mFOLFOXIRI (oxaliplatin, irinotecan, leucovorin and infusional 5-FU) for protocol-defined neoadjuvant cycles.

Comparator: mFOLFOXIRI alone or mFOLFOX6 according to the randomized study arms.

Primary endpoint: Pathological complete response at surgery.

Follow-up and maturity: Pathological response at resection; longer-term DFS and organ-preservation follow-up are required.

Registry: NCT05571644. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

Key efficacy outcome

Pathological complete response

Cadonilimab + mFOLFOXIRI
26.8%
mFOLFOX6 comparator
9.8%

pCR was 26.8% with cadonilimab plus intensive chemotherapy versus 9.8% in the mFOLFOX6 comparator; absolute difference 17.0 percentage points. The pCR endpoint is a surrogate and should not be equated with survival benefit.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 chemotherapy-related toxicity, including cytopenias and gastrointestinal events, was observed; immune-mediated adverse events require additional monitoring.

NNT 6Pathological complete response · At surgery

26.8% versus 9.8%; absolute benefit 17 percentage points. No viable tumour in the resection specimen. Calculation: ceil(100 / (26.8 − 9.8)) = ceil(5.88) = 6.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage was reported in the primary abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

The combination increased pCR in the reported comparison; confirmation of durable DFS, organ preservation and safety is needed before routine adoption.

Phase II multi-arm design, modest sample size and pCR as a surrogate endpoint. Regimen intensity and surgical/pathological assessment affect generalizability.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
