Design, population and treatment

Design: Open-label, randomized phase III trial.

Eligibility and population: 608 patients with recurrent or metastatic cervical carcinoma progressing after at least one prior systemic therapy for recurrence; squamous, adenocarcinoma and adenosquamous histologies were included.

Intervention: Cemiplimab 350 mg IV every 3 weeks.

Comparator: Investigator's choice of single-agent chemotherapy: topotecan, vinorelbine, gemcitabine, irinotecan or pemetrexed.

Randomization and endpoint: Overall survival. The full randomization stratification variables and protocol amendments remain subject to protocol-level reconciliation before release.

Key efficacy outcomes

Median OS 12.0 versus 8.5 months (HR 0.69, 95% CI 0.56–0.84; p<0.001). Median PFS was 2.8 versus 2.9 months (HR 0.75, 95% CI 0.63–0.89; p<0.001). ORR was 16.4% versus 6.3%. OS benefit was observed in squamous and adenocarcinoma/adenosquamous subgroups.

Data maturity: Primary analysis after a median follow-up of approximately 18 months; subsequent biomarker and patient-reported outcome analyses were published separately. Primary versus secondary endpoints are kept distinct; no survival estimate is inferred from a hazard ratio or median alone.

Safety and treatment burden

Grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in 45.0% versus 53.4%; treatment discontinuation for adverse events was approximately 8.7% versus 5.2%. Immune-mediated toxicity requires standard monitoring.

Delivery and discontinuation: Fixed-dose IV PD-1 therapy every 3 weeks versus investigator-selected single-agent chemotherapy; treatment exposure and immune-related adverse-event management differ materially.

Quality of life and patient experience

A separate EMPOWER-Cervical-1 patient-reported outcome analysis (NCCN Insights reference 64) reported better patient-reported quality of life, functioning and symptoms with cemiplimab than chemotherapy; exact domain estimates require full PRO-paper extraction.

Where a separate patient-reported outcome publication exists, it is treated as a secondary analysis attached to this underlying study and not as a separate trial page. Unextracted domains remain explicitly open rather than inferred.

Absolute effect and NNT / NNH

No NNT/NNH is calculated. The primary endpoint is reported as a median survival/PFS estimate or a non-inferiority comparison without a valid same-horizon absolute event difference; NNT/NNH is not derived from median survival or a hazard ratio.

Only same-endpoint, same-time-horizon absolute differences are eligible for NNT/NNH. Median survival and hazard ratios alone are not used. No NNT/NNH appropriate from the reported primary endpoint.

Interpretation and limitations

Cemiplimab improved OS after prior systemic therapy for recurrent/metastatic cervical cancer, with benefit reported across histologies. Applicability depends on prior treatment, immune contraindications, regulatory access and current local guideline status.

Limitations: Open-label design and a heterogeneous investigator-choice comparator; median PFS was short in both arms despite the OS benefit. No NNT is calculated from median OS/HR.
