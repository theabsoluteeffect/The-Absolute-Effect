The Absolute Effect / Evidence / BC2001
Bladder Cancer · Radiation Oncology · Bladder Preservation

BC2001

Clinical question

For bladder-preserving RT, does concurrent chemotherapy improve locoregional control?

Trial context
Population
360 patients with T2–T4a N0 M0 muscle-invasive bladder cancer
Intervention
Concurrent 5-FU + mitomycin C with RT vs RT alone
Key endpoint
2-year locoregional disease-free survival
Time horizon / follow-up
2 years
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

Concurrent chemotherapy improved 2-year locoregional disease-free survival from 58% to 71%, an absolute benefit of about 13 percentage points (NNT 8). At 10 years, locoregional control remained significantly better, while OS was not significantly different. NNT is based on 2-year locoregional disease-free survival.

NNT: 8

Treatment burden / harm

Grade 3–4 acute toxicity

NNH: 30

Relative effect

HR: 0.61

95% CI 0.42–0.90; P=.01

Clinical interpretation

Interpret the absolute difference together with the endpoint, time horizon, relative effect and limitations of the study design.
