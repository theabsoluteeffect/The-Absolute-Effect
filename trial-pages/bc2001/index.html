BLADDER CANCER · RADIATION ONCOLOGY · BLADDER PRESERVATION

BC2001

For bladder-preserving RT, does concurrent chemotherapy improve locoregional control?

Trial at a glance

Population: 360 patients with T2–T4a N0 M0 muscle-invasive bladder cancer.

Intervention: RT + concurrent 5-FU/mitomycin C. Comparator: RT alone.

Endpoint: 2-year locoregional disease-free survival.

Clinical takeaway

Concurrent chemotherapy materially improves locoregional control during bladder-preserving RT, at the cost of additional acute toxicity. The NNT is tied to 2-year locoregional DFS.
