Myelofibrosis · Haemato-Oncology · Phase III

TRANSFORM-1

Intermediate- or high-risk myelofibrosis in patients naive to JAK-inhibitor therapy. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignRandomized, double-blind phase III trial
Population252 patients with JAK-inhibitor-naive myelofibrosis randomized to navitoclax plus ruxolitinib or placebo plus ruxolitinib.
Primary endpointSpleen volume reduction of at least 35% (SVR35) at week 24.
Data maturityPrimary week-24 spleen-volume response; longer-term progression and survival follow-up ongoing.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 252 patients with JAK-inhibitor-naive myelofibrosis randomized to navitoclax plus ruxolitinib or placebo plus ruxolitinib.

Intervention: Navitoclax orally once daily with ruxolitinib 15–20 mg twice daily; navitoclax was titrated within the protocol dose range according to platelet count and tolerability.

Comparator: Placebo plus ruxolitinib at protocol-defined twice-daily dosing.

Primary endpoint: Spleen volume reduction of at least 35% (SVR35) at week 24.

Follow-up and maturity: Primary week-24 spleen-volume response; longer-term progression and survival follow-up ongoing.

Registry: NCT04472598. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

SVR35 at week 24 was 63.2% versus 31.5%; absolute difference 31.7 percentage points. Total symptom score response did not show a comparable statistically significant improvement.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3–4 thrombocytopenia and anemia were more frequent with navitoclax; cytopenia-related dose interruption and bleeding risk require monitoring.

NNT 4Spleen volume reduction ≥35% · Week 24

63.2% versus 31.5%; absolute benefit 31.7 percentage points. Achieved ≥35% spleen-volume reduction from baseline. Calculation: ceil(100 / (63.2 − 31.5)) = ceil(3.15) = 4.

Quality of life and treatment burden

Patient-reported total symptom score did not show a significant parallel advantage at the primary time point.

Interpretation and limitations

The combination improves spleen-volume response in JAK-inhibitor-naive myelofibrosis, but the absence of a clear symptom-score gain and increased cytopenias temper the clinical interpretation.

Spleen-volume response is a surrogate; symptom response and long-term survival must be considered separately. Cytopenias can limit dose intensity.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
