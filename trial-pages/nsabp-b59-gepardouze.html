NSABP B-59/GeparDouze

In stage II–III triple-negative breast cancer, does adding atezolizumab to neoadjuvant chemotherapy and continuing it after surgery improve event-free survival?

Comparative clinical evidence

Absolute Effect

The randomized phase 3 trial enrolled 1,550 patients and compared atezolizumab with placebo added to sequential taxane-carboplatin-anthracycline-based neoadjuvant chemotherapy. Four-year event-free survival was 85.2% versus 81.9%, an absolute difference of 3.3 percentage points; HR 0.80, P=.083. The primary EFS endpoint was therefore not statistically significant.

NNT / NNH: Calculated only when a defensible fixed-time absolute event difference is available. Hazard ratios alone are not converted into NNT.

Clinical interpretation

The patient-level value of an intervention depends on absolute event risk, time horizon, competing risks, toxicity and treatment burden—not on relative effect measures alone.

Limitations

Historical trials may use older staging, imaging, treatment techniques and supportive care. Subgroup findings are not automatically equivalent to randomized overall-trial evidence. This page summarizes the trial and does not replace the primary publication.
