CheckMate 274

Clinical question

Does adjuvant nivolumab reduce recurrence after radical surgery for high-risk muscle-invasive urothelial carcinoma?

Trial context
Population
709 patients with high-risk muscle-invasive urothelial carcinoma after radical resection
Intervention
Adjuvant nivolumab vs placebo
Key endpoint
Disease-free survival
Time horizon / follow-up
3-year follow-up
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

At 3-year follow-up, median DFS was 22.0 months with nivolumab vs 10.9 months with placebo, HR 0.71. The original trial showed 6-month DFS 74.9% vs 60.3%, an absolute improvement of 14.6 points. A fixed-time NNT is therefore best based on the 6-month DFS landmark: NNT 7. The later median-based difference should not be converted into an NNT.

NNT: 9 for median-event reduction interpretation; fixed-time NNT requires a specified landmark

Treatment burden / harm

Grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events

NNH: 10

Relative effect

HR: 0.71

95% CI 0.58–0.86

Clinical interpretation

Interpret the absolute difference together with the endpoint, time horizon, relative effect and limitations of the study design.
