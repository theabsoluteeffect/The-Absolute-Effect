Esophageal Cancer · Medical · Neoadjuvant / Perioperative

ESOPEC

Is perioperative FLOT superior to CROSS chemoradiotherapy for resectable esophageal adenocarcinoma?

Population and treatment

438 randomized: 221 FLOT, 217 CROSS.

Intervention vs comparator: Perioperative FLOT vs preoperative carboplatin/paclitaxel with 41.4 Gy RT then surgery.

Registry: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=ESOPEC+FLOT+CROSS+NEJM

Results

3-year OS 57.4% vs 50.7%; HR 0.70 (95% CI 0.53–0.92; p=0.01). 3-year PFS 51.6% vs 35.0%; HR 0.66 (95% CI 0.51–0.85). Grade ≥3 AEs 58% vs 50%; 90-day postoperative mortality 3.1% vs 5.6%.
