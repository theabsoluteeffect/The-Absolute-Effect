NRG/RTOG 1016

In HPV-positive oropharyngeal cancer, is cetuximab non-inferior to cisplatin with definitive radiotherapy?

Trial design and population

Population / eligibility
849 eligible patients with HPV-positive oropharyngeal SCC.
Treatment vs comparator
IMRT 70 Gy/35 fractions with cisplatin 100 mg/m² on days 1 and 22 vs cetuximab loading dose then weekly.
Sample size
849 (reported trial population)
Featured endpoint
Five-year overall survival · 5 years

Registry: undefined.

Key results

Five-year OS 84.6% with cisplatin vs 77.9% with cetuximab; cetuximab-vs-cisplatin HR 1.45 (95% CI 1.03–2.05), p=0.0163. Cetuximab failed the prespecified non-inferiority criterion.
