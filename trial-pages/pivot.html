PIVOT

In localized prostate cancer, does radical prostatectomy improve overall or prostate-cancer mortality compared with observation?

Absolute Effect

PIVOT randomized men with localized prostate cancer to radical prostatectomy or observation. Long-term follow-up did not demonstrate a statistically significant overall-survival advantage in the entire cohort; exploratory analyses suggested different effects according to disease risk and baseline PSA.

NNT / NNH: Calculated only when a defensible fixed-time absolute event difference is available. Hazard ratios alone are not converted into NNT.

Clinical interpretation

The patient-level value of an intervention depends on absolute event risk, time horizon, competing risks, toxicity and treatment burden—not on relative effect measures alone.

Limitations

Historical trials may use older staging, imaging, treatment techniques and supportive care. Subgroup findings are not automatically equivalent to randomized overall-trial evidence. This page summarizes the trial and does not replace the primary publication.
