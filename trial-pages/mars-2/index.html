MARS 2

Does extended pleurectomy/decortication add benefit to chemotherapy in pleural mesothelioma?

One underlying study; aliases and follow-up reports consolidated. Primary discipline: Surgical.

Population and eligibility

335 patients with pleural mesothelioma eligible after two cycles of chemotherapy.

Selected patients considered for surgery-based management after induction chemotherapy.

Sample size

335 randomised/evaluable as reported.

Intervention

Extended pleurectomy/decortication plus chemotherapy.

Comparator

Chemotherapy alone.

Endpoints and results

Overall survival, quality-adjusted survival and serious adverse events.

  • Median survival 19.3 months with surgery+chemotherapy vs 24.8 months with chemotherapy alone.
  • Difference in 2-year restricted mean survival time −1.9 months (95% CI −3.4 to −0.3; p=0.019), favouring chemotherapy alone.
  • Serious adverse-event incidence-rate ratio 3.6 (95% CI 2.3–5.5; p<0.0001).

Interpretation: No NNT from median survival. Restricted mean survival and excess serious adverse events communicate the balance more appropriately.

Toxicity, discontinuation and quality of life

Trial-specific harms: Surgery increased serious adverse events substantially; perioperative complications and recovery burden are central.

Treatment burden/discontinuation: Major operation, hospitalisation and recovery added to chemotherapy.

Quality of life: Quality-adjusted survival was assessed; surgery did not provide an advantage sufficient to offset its burden in the reported analysis.

Subgroups, maturity and limitations

Subgroups: No subgroup benefit claim without prespecified and adequately powered interaction evidence.

Maturity: Phase III pragmatic randomised trial, 2024.

Limitations: Open-label; surgical expertise and selection matter, but randomised data do not support routine extended surgery as survival-improving.

Clinical interpretation

MARS 2 challenges routine radical surgery for pleural mesothelioma: no survival improvement and substantially more serious harms.

Current-practice context must be checked against regional guidelines, patient fitness, biomarkers, alternatives and regulatory status before clinical application. Clinical freshness review and browser-rendered QA remain pending.
