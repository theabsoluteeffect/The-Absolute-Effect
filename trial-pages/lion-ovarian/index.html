Ovarian Cancer · Surgical

LION

After complete macroscopic resection of advanced ovarian cancer with clinically normal nodes, does systematic lymphadenectomy improve survival?

Trial design and population

Population: 647 patients with FIGO stage IIB–IV disease, complete macroscopic resection and normal nodes.

Treatment vs comparator: Systematic pelvic and para-aortic lymphadenectomy vs no systematic lymphadenectomy.

Registry: NCT00712218

Key results

Median OS 65.5 months with lymphadenectomy vs 69.2 months without (HR 1.06, 95% CI 0.83–1.34; p=0.65). Median PFS 25.5 months in both groups (HR 1.11, 95% CI 0.92–1.34; p=0.29).

Absolute effect

Safety, treatment burden and QoL

Repeat laparotomy 12.4% vs 6.5%; 60-day postoperative mortality 3.1% vs 0.9%. Added operative burden, lymphocele and lymphoedema are relevant.

Quality-of-life and discontinuation percentages are not asserted without full source-table verification; source-level QoL/toxicity audit remains a QC item.

Subgroups, maturity and limitations

Applies to clinically normal nodes after complete macroscopic resection; not grossly suspicious/bulky nodes. No survival gain offset added morbidity in this population.

Distinguish prespecified from exploratory subgroup analyses and mature from immature endpoints. Verify current guideline context before applying historical trial findings.
