TPG: paclitaxel, cisplatin and gemcitabine

What activity and long-term outcomes were reported with paclitaxel/cisplatin/gemcitabine beyond second-line salvage?

Reported outcomes

Among 75 patients, 8 (10.7%) achieved CR and 29 (38.7%) achieved marker-negative partial response, giving a major response rate of 49%. Two-year PFS was 14.8% and OS 29.5%. Surgery followed chemotherapy in selected patients.

Interpretation and limitations

Retrospective later-line data. Survival was substantially better among patients rendered disease-free, highlighting the importance of resection in selected patients. Not seminoma-specific.

Guideline context: ESMO–EURACAN recommends standard-dose VIP/PEI, TIP or VeIP as conventional first salvage options after relapse following cisplatin-based chemotherapy.
