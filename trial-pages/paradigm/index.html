Design, population and treatment

Design: Open-label, randomized phase III comparative-effectiveness trial.

Eligibility and population: 823 patients with RAS wild-type metastatic colorectal cancer; the prespecified left-sided primary population comprised 604 patients. Treatment was mFOLFOX6 plus panitumumab or bevacizumab.

Intervention: mFOLFOX6 every 2 weeks plus panitumumab 6 mg/kg IV every 2 weeks.

Comparator: mFOLFOX6 every 2 weeks plus bevacizumab 5 mg/kg IV every 2 weeks.

Randomization and endpoint: Overall survival in patients with left-sided primary tumors. The full randomization stratification variables and protocol amendments remain subject to protocol-level reconciliation before release.

Key efficacy outcomes

In the left-sided population, median OS was 37.9 versus 34.3 months (HR 0.82, 95.798% CI 0.68–0.99; p=0.03). In the overall RAS wild-type population, median OS was 36.2 versus 31.3 months (HR 0.84; p=0.03). Left-sided PFS was not significantly different; response and R0 resection rates favored panitumumab.

Data maturity: Long-term analysis; median follow-up approximately 61 months in the primary report. Primary versus secondary endpoints are kept distinct; no survival estimate is inferred from a hazard ratio or median alone.

Safety and treatment burden

Grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in 71.8% versus 64.9%. Treatment-related deaths were reported in 10 versus 2 patients. Full event-specific safety and discontinuation tables remain to be checked.

Delivery and discontinuation: Both arms used the same mFOLFOX6 backbone; panitumumab and bevacizumab have distinct dermatologic/electrolyte versus vascular, hypertensive and bleeding risk profiles.

Absolute effect and NNT / NNH

No NNT/NNH is calculated. The primary endpoint is reported as a median survival/PFS estimate or a non-inferiority comparison without a valid same-horizon absolute event difference; NNT/NNH is not derived from median survival or a hazard ratio.

Only same-endpoint, same-time-horizon absolute differences are eligible for NNT/NNH. Median survival and hazard ratios alone are not used. No NNT/NNH appropriate from the reported primary endpoint.

Interpretation and limitations

PARADIGM supports anti-EGFR therapy as a first-line option for appropriately selected left-sided RAS wild-type metastatic colorectal cancer, with molecular and sidedness selection essential. It does not establish a universal preference across all RAS wild-type disease.

Limitations: Open-label design; the OS result is specific to left-sided RAS wild-type disease and should not be extrapolated to right-sided primaries or RAS-mutant disease. No NNT is calculated from median OS/HR alone.
