AMBASSADOR / Alliance A031501

After radical surgery for high-risk muscle-invasive urothelial carcinoma, does one year of adjuvant pembrolizumab improve disease-free survival compared with observation?

Population
702 patients after radical surgery for high-risk muscle-invasive urothelial carcinoma.
Intervention / comparator
Pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every 3 weeks for 1 year versus observation.
Primary/major efficacy result
Median DFS: 29.6 vs 14.2 months; HR 0.73 (95% CI 0.59–0.90), P=.003. Fixed-time NNT not calculated because a directly reported clinically relevant DFS rate is not available in the primary abstract.
Overall survival
Overall survival was a coprimary endpoint, but the reported analysis was not mature enough to establish an OS benefit.

Absolute Effect

Median DFS: 29.6 vs 14.2 months; HR 0.73 (95% CI 0.59–0.90), P=.003. Fixed-time NNT not calculated because a directly reported clinically relevant DFS rate is not available in the primary abstract.

Clinical interpretation: Grade ≥3 adverse events: 50.6% vs 31.6%. This is an absolute 19.0-point increase in grade ≥3 adverse events, but it is not used as NNH because the site rule requires a clinically central fixed-time toxicity endpoint rather than generic grade ≥3 AE incidence.

Toxicity / treatment burden: Apolo AB, et al. N Engl J Med. 2025;392:45-55.

Important limitations

Open-label design and treatment evolution after progression can affect absolute survival comparisons. Absolute effects are reported only where a reliable common timepoint or directly reported rate permits calculation; otherwise NNT/NNH is not forced from medians or hazard ratios.
