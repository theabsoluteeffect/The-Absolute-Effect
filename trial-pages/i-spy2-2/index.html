I-SPY2.2 — rilvegostomig plus trastuzumab deruxtecan

Biomarker-directed neoadjuvant therapy for stage II–III high-risk early breast cancer. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignAdaptive platform phase II trial with concurrent controls and biomarker-defined cohorts
Population105 patients treated in the investigational regimen cohort within the adaptive I-SPY2.2 platform
Primary endpointPathological complete response at surgery, evaluated by biomarker-defined subtype.
Data maturityNeoadjuvant pathological response analysis; long-term event-free and overall survival are not mature.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 105 patients treated in the investigational regimen cohort within the adaptive I-SPY2.2 platform; high-risk stage II–III breast cancer, with outcomes stratified by immune and hormone-receptor biomarkers.

Intervention: Rilvegostomig 750 mg intravenously every 3 weeks plus trastuzumab deruxtecan 5.4 mg/kg intravenously every 3 weeks for the protocol-defined neoadjuvant block.

Comparator: Concurrent adaptive-platform control and standard neoadjuvant regimens; this is not a single fixed head-to-head comparator.

Primary endpoint: Pathological complete response at surgery, evaluated by biomarker-defined subtype.

Follow-up and maturity: Neoadjuvant pathological response analysis; long-term event-free and overall survival are not mature.

Registry: NCT01042379. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

Key efficacy outcome

Pathological complete response in the reported immune-positive cohort

Rilvegostomig + trastuzumab deruxtecan
54%

The reported pCR rate was approximately 54% in the immune-positive subgroup; response varied by HR and immune phenotype. The adaptive platform design and biomarker-specific estimates should be read with their cohort denominators and predictive probabilities.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 adverse events were reported in the primary article; interstitial lung disease/pneumonitis is a specific trastuzumab-deruxtecan risk and requires monitoring. Severe ILD was uncommon in this cohort.

No NNT is calculated because this is a single-arm or non-comparative study without a randomized comparator.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive patient-reported QoL advantage was established in the primary report.

Interpretation and limitations

The study supports further biomarker-directed evaluation of the combination in early breast cancer. It should not be interpreted as proof of superiority over every contemporary neoadjuvant regimen.

Adaptive randomization, biomarker-defined cohorts and non-identical concurrent controls make a single pooled treatment effect inappropriate. pCR is a surrogate endpoint; survival benefit is not established.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
