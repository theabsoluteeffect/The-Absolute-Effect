Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 377 eligible patients were randomized in this adaptive neoadjuvant study. 108 were assigned to RT-DXd and 88 to the concurrent control; 105 RT-DXd and 77 control patients received assigned treatment and formed the reported ITT efficacy populations.

Intervention: Block A: trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) plus rilvegostomig, followed by response-adapted block B taxane-based therapy with/without immunotherapy and block C doxorubicin/cyclophosphamide with/without immunotherapy as required.

Comparator: Contemporary standard neoadjuvant treatment strategies in the adaptive control arm; not a single fixed regimen.

Primary endpoint: Pathological complete response, analyzed by response-predictive subtype and hormone-receptor/ERBB2 status.

Follow-up and maturity: Neoadjuvant response and surgery; HRQoL recovery data were still being evaluated in the primary report.

Registry: NCT01042379. The primary analysis population and denominators are stated explicitly.

Among 105 RT-DXd-treated patients, modeled pCR was 54% in immune-positive tumours, meeting the prespecified threshold. Modeled pCR was 57% in HR-positive/immune-positive and 52% in HR-negative/immune-positive disease. In immune-positive patients achieving pCR, 21 (62%) required only block A and 33 (97%) avoided anthracycline-based chemotherapy. Across all treatment blocks, overall pCR was similar to control.

Safety and absolute effect

Fatigue and nausea were common. Serious AEs included pulmonary embolism and ILD, each in 3/105 (2.9%). ILD occurred in 13/105 (12.4%), including one grade 3 event; enrollment was paused twice for review of grade ≥2 ILD and resumed after DSMB approval.

No NNT is calculated: No common-horizon absolute risk difference is available for the primary time-to-event endpoint.

Quality of life and treatment burden

HRQoL was being evaluated and was not yet reported as a definitive comparative endpoint.

Interpretation and limitations

The signal is concentrated in immune-positive ERBB2-negative tumours; the important clinical observation is that selected patients achieved pCR after block A and many avoided anthracycline. The earlier staged page's blanket pCR estimate was incorrect.

Adaptive signal-finding phase II study with small biomarker-defined subgroups; responses cannot be attributed separately to rilvegostomig or T-DXd. Larger confirmatory trials are required.
