The Absolute Effect

SORCE

Renal Cell Carcinoma · Adjuvant Therapy

Clinical question

Does adjuvant sorafenib improve disease-free survival after nephrectomy in patients at intermediate or high risk of recurrent RCC?

Key result

SORCE did not demonstrate a disease-free survival benefit for three years of sorafenib compared with placebo. The trial therefore did not establish adjuvant sorafenib as a standard postoperative treatment.

Absolute Effect perspective

A negative randomized trial is clinically informative: the treatment burden of targeted therapy needs to be weighed against the absence of a demonstrated recurrence benefit rather than inferred from activity in metastatic RCC.
