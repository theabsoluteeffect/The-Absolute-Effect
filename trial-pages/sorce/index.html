The Absolute Effect / Evidence

SORCE

Renal Cell Carcinoma · Adjuvant Therapy

Clinical question

Does adjuvant sorafenib improve disease-free survival after nephrectomy in patients at intermediate or high risk of recurrent RCC?

Trial context

SORCE tested prolonged adjuvant sorafenib after nephrectomy in patients with intermediate- or high-risk renal cell carcinoma. The trial is a key negative study in the evolution of postoperative targeted therapy for RCC.

Absolute Effect perspective

The interpretation should distinguish recurrence outcomes from treatment burden and should not infer benefit from relative measures without the corresponding absolute event rates.
