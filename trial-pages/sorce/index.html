SORCE

Adjuvant sorafenib after nephrectomy

Clinical question

Does adjuvant sorafenib improve disease-free survival after nephrectomy in patients at intermediate or high risk of recurrent RCC?

Trial at a glance

Design
Randomized, placebo-controlled
Population
Intermediate- or high-risk RCC after nephrectomy
Intervention
Sorafenib for three years
Comparator
Placebo
Primary focus
Disease-free survival

Key result

Absolute Effect perspective
No demonstrated disease-free survival benefit

SORCE did not demonstrate a disease-free survival benefit for adjuvant sorafenib compared with placebo. The trial therefore did not establish postoperative sorafenib as a standard treatment.

Why the result matters

A negative randomized trial is clinically informative. Targeted therapy with established activity in metastatic RCC cannot be assumed to provide a recurrence benefit when moved into the adjuvant setting.

Clinical takeaway

The appropriate interpretation is absence of demonstrated benefit. Treatment burden should be considered alongside the randomized disease-control result, rather than used to explain away a negative efficacy result.
