VeIP as initial salvage chemotherapy

After relapse following cisplatin/etoposide-based chemotherapy, what outcomes were reported with VeIP salvage chemotherapy?

Mixed germ-cell tumour cohort; not seminoma-specific

Population
135

Study population as reported in the cited publication; cohort sizes and denominators are stated where available.

Mixed GCT

Not all evidence is randomized, comparative, or seminoma-specific.

Reported outcomes

In 135 patients, 67 (49.6%) achieved disease-free status after chemotherapy with or without surgery. Thirty-two (23.7%) were continuously disease-free at long-term follow-up.

Interpretation and limitations

Cisplatin-refractory patients progressing within 3 weeks were excluded. Results combine histologies and include the effect of surgery; do not interpret as a seminoma-only response rate.

Guideline context: ESMO–EURACAN recommends standard-dose VIP/PEI, TIP or VeIP as conventional first salvage options after relapse following cisplatin-based chemotherapy. NCCN Testicular Cancer Guidelines Insights (Version 2.2025) is a contemporary US guideline source. The study summarized here is not necessarily seminoma-specific; histology scope is stated explicitly below.
