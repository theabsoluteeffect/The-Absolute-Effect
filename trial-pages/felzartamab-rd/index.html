Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 295 patients randomized 2:1: felzartamab-Rd n=195 and Rd n=100. Full analysis set 289 (191 and 98); adults with RRMM after at least one systemic line and not lenalidomide-refractory.

Intervention: Felzartamab 16 mg/kg IV on days 1 and 4 in week 1, weekly weeks 2–12, every 2 weeks weeks 13–24 and every 4 weeks thereafter, plus lenalidomide 25 mg orally days 1–21 of each 28-day cycle and dexamethasone 40 mg weekly (20 mg if age >75 years).

Comparator: Lenalidomide 25 mg orally days 1–21 plus dexamethasone 40 mg weekly (20 mg if age >75 years).

Primary endpoint: PFS by independent review committee in the full analysis set.

Follow-up and maturity: Median follow-up 34.5 months (IQR 16.8–40.2) in felzartamab-Rd and 28.7 months (10.8–38.8) in Rd.

Registry: NCT03952091. Arm denominators are given where available; missing values are not inferred.

Median PFS 18.9 months (95% CI 13.6–24.0) versus 10.4 months (5.8–13.9); HR 0.62 (95% CI 0.45–0.86), P=0.0035.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3–4 neutropenia 105/192 (55%) versus 22/97 (23%); lymphopenia 41% versus 15%; leukopenia 39% versus 11%; thrombocytopenia 26% versus 11%; pneumonia 23% versus 13%; serious pneumonia 22% versus 10%. Treatment-related deaths 11/192 (6%) versus 2/97 (2%).

No NNT is calculated: Median PFS and HR are time-to-event measures; no fixed-time absolute PFS rate was used for NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive quantitative QoL advantage was reported in the primary abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

Felzartamab-Rd significantly prolonged PFS versus Rd. The prior staged page had the correct median PFS but lacked the complex loading/maintenance schedule, exact FAS denominators and treatment-related deaths; these are now corrected.

Open-label study in a Chinese population, lenalidomide-sensitive disease only. The substantial infection and treatment-related mortality burden must be considered alongside PFS.
