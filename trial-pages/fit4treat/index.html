Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 47 patients were enrolled; 40 were included in the primary analysis. Patients had head and neck cancer planned for definitive chemoradiotherapy.

Intervention: Supervised combined aerobic and resistance exercise three times weekly from baseline until initiation of radiotherapy.

Comparator: Usual care without the structured supervised prehabilitation programme.

Primary endpoint: Six-minute walk distance at the end of the pre-treatment period.

Follow-up and maturity: Primary functional assessment immediately before chemoradiotherapy; patient-reported outcomes were also collected.

Registry: NCT05418842. Arm denominators and endpoint definitions are preserved as reported; no missing denominator is inferred.

Key efficacy outcome

Adjusted between-group 6-minute walk distance difference

Prehabilitation advantage
28.6 metres

Adjusted between-group difference in six-minute walk distance was 28.6 m (95% CI 4.1–53.1), P=0.023. Health-related QoL improved in the summary score, social-function and body-image domains.

Safety and absolute effect

No exercise-related serious adverse event was reported. Treatment-related fatigue, mucositis and nutritional compromise remain relevant to feasibility during chemoradiotherapy.

No NNT is calculated: the primary result is a median time-to-event or continuous/single-arm outcome without a valid common-horizon absolute risk difference.

Quality of life and treatment burden

The primary report found improvements in the EORTC QLQ-C30 summary score, social functioning and body image; the study was not powered for survival.

Interpretation and limitations

FIT4TREAT supports supervised prehabilitation before definitive head-and-neck chemoradiotherapy. The earlier staged page's +56-m estimate and 12-week endpoint were incorrect; the primary endpoint is the pre-treatment 6MWD difference of 28.6 m.

Small randomized study with 40 patients in the primary analysis; functional effects are measured before chemoradiotherapy and should not be extrapolated to long-term survival.

Current-practice context · reviewed 17 November 2026
