EGFR-mutated non-small-cell lung cancer · Medical Oncology · Phase III

PANKU-Lung01

Advanced EGFR-mutated NSCLC after progression on a third-generation EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignOpen-label randomized phase III trial
Population432 patients with advanced EGFR-mutated NSCLC after progression on a third-generation EGFR-TKI randomized to ADC or platinum/pemetrexed chemotherapy.
Primary endpointProgression-free survival by blinded independent review.
Data maturityPrimary PFS analysis reported; OS remains immature.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 432 patients with advanced EGFR-mutated NSCLC after progression on a third-generation EGFR-TKI randomized to ADC or platinum/pemetrexed chemotherapy.

Intervention: Izalontamab brengitecan 2.5 mg/kg intravenously on days 1 and 8 of each 21-day cycle.

Comparator: Platinum plus pemetrexed chemotherapy according to protocol.

Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival by blinded independent review.

Follow-up and maturity: Primary PFS analysis reported; OS remains immature.

Registry: NCT06382116. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

Key efficacy outcome

Median progression-free survival

Izalontamab brengitecan
6.7 months
Platinum/pemetrexed
3.8 months

Median PFS 6.7 versus 3.8 months; HR 0.65, P<0.0001. The OS analysis is immature at the reported cutoff.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in approximately 55.3% versus 42.1%; myelosuppression, gastrointestinal toxicity and ADC-related events require monitoring.

No NNT is calculated because Median PFS and HR are time-to-event measures; no same-horizon absolute PFS rate was verified for NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL superiority was reported in the primary abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

PANKU-Lung01 provides phase III evidence for an ADC strategy after third-generation EGFR-TKI progression; OS maturity and longer-term toxicity remain important.

Post-third-generation EGFR-TKI population and a specific ADC dose/schedule; do not extrapolate to TKI-naive disease or compare indirectly with other ADCs.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
