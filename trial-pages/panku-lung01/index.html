Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 432 patients with EGFR-mutated advanced non-squamous NSCLC after progression on a third-generation EGFR-TKI and without prior chemotherapy for advanced disease randomized: izalontamab brengitecan n=217 and chemotherapy n=215. 422 received treatment and comprised the primary efficacy population.

Intervention: Izalontamab brengitecan 2.5 mg/kg IV on days 1 and 8 every 3 weeks.

Comparator: Pemetrexed plus carboplatin or cisplatin for 4–6 cycles; pemetrexed maintenance permitted in patients without progression. The accessible primary abstract identifies pemetrexed plus carboplatin or cisplatin for 4–6 cycles, with pemetrexed maintenance permitted, but does not state the platinum dose; no dose is inferred.

Primary endpoint: PFS assessed by blinded independent central review.

Follow-up: Median PFS follow-up 14.6 months (95% CI 12.9–16.7) with ADC and 15.2 months (11.2–18.2) with chemotherapy; interim OS analysis.

Registry: NCT06382116

Median BICR PFS 11.1 months (95% CI 9.2–12.7) versus 5.7 months (5.5–5.9); HR 0.40 (95% CI 0.31–0.51), one-sided P<0.0001. Twelve-month PFS 46.5% versus 14.4%. ORR 60.2% versus 34.5%; median duration of response 10.5 versus 5.7 months. Interim OS: median not reached versus 19.4 months; HR 0.66 (95% CI 0.47–0.91), one-sided P=0.0058, not crossing the prespecified boundary.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs occurred in 79% versus 62%; dose reductions 60% versus 23%; one treatment-related death in each group. Myelosuppression and ADC-related toxicity are important.

At 12 months, PFS was 46.5% versus 14.4% (absolute benefit 32.1 percentage points). The landmark NNT is 4: ceil[100 ÷ (46.5 − 14.4)] = ceil(3.12) = 4, to yield one additional patient alive and progression-free at 12 months. These Kaplan–Meier landmark rates were reported with ESMO 2026 LBA71; this is not calculated from median PFS or the hazard ratio.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage reported in the primary abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

PANKU-Lung01 demonstrates a major PFS improvement with izalontamab brengitecan after third-generation EGFR-TKI progression. The earlier staged page's PFS 6.7 vs3.8 and HR0.65 were incorrect and are replaced with the primary Lancet results.

China-based trial; OS is interim and did not cross the prespecified efficacy boundary. Platinum choice/dose and full safety supplement remain to be extracted.
