PANKU-Lung01

After progression on third-generation EGFR-TKI. Source correction 17 November 2026; absolute effects are reported only at explicit comparable horizons.

DesignRandomized phase III
Population432 patients with EGFR-mutated advanced non-squamous NSCLC after progression on a third-generation EGFR-TKI randomized to izalontamab brengitecan or platinum-based chemotherapy.
Primary endpointPFS by blinded independent central review.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 432 patients with EGFR-mutated advanced non-squamous NSCLC after progression on a third-generation EGFR-TKI randomized to izalontamab brengitecan or platinum-based chemotherapy.

Intervention: Izalontamab brengitecan 2.5 mg/kg IV on days 1 and 8 every 3 weeks.

Comparator: Platinum-based chemotherapy with pemetrexed according to protocol; exact cisplatin/carboplatin choice and doses should be retained from the full protocol rather than inferred from a class label.

Primary endpoint: PFS by blinded independent central review.

Follow-up and maturity: Primary PFS analysis reported; OS immature.

Registry: NCT06382116. Arm denominators are given where available; missing values are not inferred.

Key efficacy outcome

Median progression-free survival

Izalontamab brengitecan
6.7 months
Platinum-based chemotherapy
3.8 months

Median PFS 6.7 versus 3.8 months; HR 0.65 (95% CI 0.52–0.82), P<0.0001.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in approximately 55.3% versus 42.1%; myelosuppression and ADC-related toxicities require monitoring.

No NNT is calculated: Median PFS and HR are time-to-event measures; no same-horizon absolute PFS rate was verified for NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage was reported in the primary abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

PANKU-Lung01 provides phase III evidence for an ADC after third-generation EGFR-TKI progression. The prior page's primary title was not exact; the exact Lancet title is now used.

Post-third-generation EGFR-TKI population and China-based study; OS is immature and the exact platinum backbone should be checked in the full protocol.
