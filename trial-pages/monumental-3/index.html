MonumenTAL-3

Relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma after prior proteasome inhibitor and immunomodulatory therapy. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignRandomized phase III multi-arm trial
Population864 patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma after prior lines of therapy randomized across talquetamab/daratumumab-based and daratumumab/pomalidomide/dexamethasone strategies.
Primary endpointProgression-free survival.
Data maturityPrimary PFS analysis reported; OS and long-term immune-effector toxicity follow-up continue.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 864 patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma after prior lines of therapy randomized across talquetamab/daratumumab-based and daratumumab/pomalidomide/dexamethasone strategies.

Intervention: Talquetamab 0.4 mg/kg subcutaneously weekly or 0.8 mg/kg every 2 weeks with daratumumab 1800 mg subcutaneously and protocol pomalidomide/dexamethasone in the triplet arm.

Comparator: Daratumumab plus pomalidomide and dexamethasone; a talquetamab/daratumumab arm was also evaluated.

Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival.

Follow-up and maturity: Primary PFS analysis reported; OS and long-term immune-effector toxicity follow-up continue.

Registry: NCT05455320. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

Key efficacy outcome

Median progression-free survival

Talquetamab-based combination
24.7 months
Daratumumab/pomalidomide/dexamethasone
14.4 months

Median PFS was approximately 24.7 versus 14.4 months for the talquetamab/daratumumab/pomalidomide strategy versus DPd; HR approximately 0.65. Response rates were high in both groups.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 adverse events were approximately 85% versus 78%; CRS, infections, cytopenias, dysgeusia, oral toxicity and skin/nail effects are characteristic treatment burdens.

No NNT is calculated because Median PFS and HR are time-to-event measures; no fixed-time absolute PFS risk difference is used for NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

Patient-reported symptom and function outcomes should be interpreted alongside oral/skin toxicity and infection burden; no NNT is derived from median PFS.

Interpretation and limitations

MonumenTAL-3 supports a talquetamab/daratumumab-based strategy in RRMM, but infection prevention, oral toxicity, patient preference and prior BCMA exposure remain central.

Open-label multi-arm design, prior-treatment heterogeneity and substantial treatment burden. Cross-trial comparison with other bispecific regimens is not valid.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
