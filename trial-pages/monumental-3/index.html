Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 864 patients with RRMM after at least one prior line including lenalidomide and a proteasome inhibitor were randomized 1:1:1: Tal-DP n=287, Tal-D n=287, DPd n=290. Anti-CD38-refractory and prior GPRC5D-directed therapy were excluded.

Intervention: Talquetamab 0.8 mg/kg subcutaneously after step-up dosing plus daratumumab 1800 mg SC, with pomalidomide 4 mg orally days 1–21 and dexamethasone per protocol in the Tal-DP arm; Tal-D omitted pomalidomide.

Comparator: Daratumumab plus pomalidomide and dexamethasone (DPd).

Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival by independent review committee.

Follow-up and maturity: Interim analysis at median follow-up 24.6 months.

Registry: NCT05455320. Arm denominators are given where available; missing values are not inferred.

24-month PFS 81.3% with Tal-DP, 77.6% with Tal-D and 51.2% with DPd; HR 0.28 (95% CI 0.20–0.40) and 0.33 (0.24–0.46), respectively; P<0.001 for both. ORR 88.2%, 88.5% and 77.6%; CR or better 71.1%, 69.0% and 34.5%; MRD-negative CR 52.3%, 46.3% and 15.9%. 24-month OS was 89.2%, 87.9% and 79.1%; OS superiority boundaries were not crossed.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3–4 AEs occurred in 96.7% with Tal-DP, 78.8% with Tal-D and 95.8% with DPd; grade 3–4 infections 37.7%, 29.2% and 42.4%. Serious AEs 63.0%, 52.6% and 53.7%; fatal AEs 1.8%, 4.0% and 4.6%. CRS, dysgeusia, weight loss, skin/nail effects, infection and cytopenias are important.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive comparative QoL advantage was established in the primary abstract; characteristic GPRC5D-related oral/skin toxicity affects treatment burden.

Interpretation and limitations

Both Tal-DP and Tal-D significantly improve PFS versus DPd in the studied RRMM population. The previous staged page incorrectly used a two-arm comparison and wrong median PFS; this page restores the three-arm primary analysis.

Three-arm interim analysis; OS is not yet statistically established. Prior anti-CD38 and GPRC5D exposure were restricted, so generalizability to refractory populations is limited.
