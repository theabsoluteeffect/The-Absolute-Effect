Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 32 patients with localized resectable dMMR/MSI-H gastric or GEJ adenocarcinoma; 29 underwent surgery and formed the pathological-response denominator.

Intervention: Neoadjuvant nivolumab 240 mg IV every 2 weeks for six doses plus ipilimumab 1 mg/kg IV every 6 weeks for two doses, followed by surgery and adjuvant nivolumab 480 mg IV every 4 weeks per protocol.

Comparator: No randomized comparator; single-arm phase II study.

Primary endpoint: Pathological complete response at surgery.

Follow-up and maturity: Primary phase II pathological-response and safety report; a later long-term follow-up is not incorporated here until its exact publication and denominator are separately reconciled.

Registry: NCT04006262. Arm denominators and endpoint definitions are preserved as reported; no missing denominator is inferred.

pCR 17/29 (58.6%); major pathological response 20/29 (68.9%). The study is biomarker-selected and single-arm; these estimates are not comparative treatment effects.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3–4 treatment-related adverse events occurred in approximately 19%; immune-mediated toxicity and perioperative timing are important.

No NNT is calculated: the primary result is a median time-to-event or continuous/single-arm outcome without a valid common-horizon absolute risk difference.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No comparative QoL outcome was available.

Interpretation and limitations

NEONIPIGA is landmark proof-of-concept for neoadjuvant dual checkpoint blockade in localized dMMR/MSI-H gastric/GEJ cancer. The staged page's abbreviated primary title has been replaced with the exact JCO title.

Small single-arm cohort; pathological response cannot establish superiority over perioperative chemotherapy or surgery alone. Later follow-up must be attached as a secondary analysis, not substituted for the primary report.
