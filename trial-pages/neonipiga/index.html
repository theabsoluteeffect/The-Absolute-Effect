Gastric/gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma · Medical Oncology · Phase II

NEONIPIGA / GERCOR

Perioperative immune checkpoint blockade for localized dMMR/MSI-H gastric or GEJ adenocarcinoma. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignSingle-arm phase II study with surgery and adjuvant nivolumab
Population32 patients with localized resectable dMMR/MSI-H gastric or GEJ adenocarcinoma
Primary endpointPathological complete response at surgery.
Data maturityLong-term follow-up reports 4-year EFS and OS estimates; single-arm maturity limits comparative inference.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 32 patients with localized resectable dMMR/MSI-H gastric or GEJ adenocarcinoma; 29 proceeded to surgery and formed the pathological-response denominator.

Intervention: Neoadjuvant nivolumab 240 mg intravenously every 2 weeks plus ipilimumab 1 mg/kg every 6 weeks, followed by surgery and adjuvant nivolumab 480 mg every 4 weeks.

Comparator: No randomized comparator; single-arm study.

Primary endpoint: Pathological complete response at surgery.

Follow-up and maturity: Long-term follow-up reports 4-year EFS and OS estimates; single-arm maturity limits comparative inference.

Registry: NCT04006262. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

pCR 17/29 (58.6%); major pathological response 20/29 (68.9%). Four-year EFS and OS estimates were approximately 83.5% and 84.1%, respectively, in follow-up.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3–4 treatment-related adverse events occurred in approximately 19%; immune-mediated toxicity and the perioperative timing of surgery require attention.

No NNT is calculated because this is a single-arm or non-comparative study without a randomized comparator.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No comparative QoL benefit is available.

Interpretation and limitations

NEONIPIGA is landmark proof-of-concept for neoadjuvant dual checkpoint blockade in localized dMMR/MSI-H gastric/GEJ cancer; it supports biomarker-directed multidisciplinary discussion, not indiscriminate immunotherapy for all gastric cancers.

Small, single-arm, biomarker-selected cohort; pathological response and long-term survival estimates cannot establish superiority over perioperative chemotherapy or surgery alone.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
