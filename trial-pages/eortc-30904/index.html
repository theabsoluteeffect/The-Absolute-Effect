Renal Cell Carcinoma · Surgical Oncology · Localized Disease

EORTC 30904

Clinical question

For small localized RCC, can nephron-sparing surgery preserve renal function without compromising cancer outcomes?

Trial at a glance

541 patients with solitary ≤5 cm renal masses; nephron-sparing surgery vs radical nephrectomy; overall survival and renal function.

Absolute Effect

10-year OS was 75.7% vs 81.1%; non-inferiority was not demonstrated. Renal dysfunction substantially favoured nephron-sparing surgery.

Clinical interpretation

The study should not be interpreted as evidence against modern partial nephrectomy in appropriately selected cT1 RCC; its strongest clinical signal is renal preservation.
