The Absolute Effect / Evidence

Adjuvant Paclitaxel-Platinum vs 5-FU-Platinum

Penile Cancer · High-Risk Node-Positive Adjuvant Therapy

Clinical question

In high-risk node-positive penile squamous cell carcinoma, does adjuvant paclitaxel-platinum provide comparable efficacy with lower toxicity than 5-FU-platinum?

Trial context

This randomized trial compared two postoperative platinum-based chemotherapy strategies in men with high-risk node-positive penile squamous cell carcinoma. The study was stopped early and was underpowered for a definitive survival comparison.

Absolute Effect perspective

The major signal is treatment burden: severe toxicity and hospitalization were substantially more frequent with 5-FU-platinum. The efficacy comparison remains inconclusive and should not be interpreted as establishing paclitaxel-platinum as superior to TIP or as a universal standard for high-risk penile cancer.
