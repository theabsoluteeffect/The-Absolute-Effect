Adjuvant Paclitaxel-Platinum vs 5-FU-Platinum

Randomized 2026 study · median follow-up 69 months

Clinical question

In high-risk node-positive penile squamous cell carcinoma, does adjuvant paclitaxel-platinum provide comparable efficacy with lower toxicity than 5-FU-platinum?

Trial at a glance

Absolute Effect perspective
Strong tolerability signal; efficacy remains inconclusive

The study was stopped early and was underpowered for definitive efficacy comparisons. The most defensible signal is substantially less severe toxicity and better treatment completion with paclitaxel-platinum.

Comparator limitation

The trial did not directly compare paclitaxel-platinum with neoadjuvant TIP. Its postoperative results should not be generalized to establish paclitaxel-platinum as the preferred regimen for all high-risk penile cancer.

Clinical takeaway

Survival equivalence or superiority is not established. The strongest evidence is a treatment-burden signal favouring paclitaxel-platinum, while efficacy remains uncertain because of early termination and limited sample size.
