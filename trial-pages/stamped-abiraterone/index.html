STAMPEDE – abiraterone comparison

In metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer starting long-term ADT, does adding abiraterone acetate plus prednisolone improve survival?

Population
Patients with metastatic prostate cancer starting long-term androgen-deprivation therapy in the STAMPEDE platform.
Intervention / comparator
Abiraterone 1000 mg daily + prednisolone 5 mg daily added to standard of care versus standard of care alone.
Primary/major efficacy result
5-year OS: 60% vs 41%; absolute improvement 19 points; NNT 6.
Overall survival
Overall survival HR 0.60 (95% CI 0.50–0.71) at 6.1 years median follow-up.

Absolute Effect

5-year OS: 60% vs 41%; absolute improvement 19 points; NNT 6.

Clinical interpretation: Benefit was seen in both low-risk and high-risk metastatic disease groups. The comparison is a platform trial component rather than a conventional two-arm standalone trial.

Toxicity / treatment burden: Grade ≥3 toxicity at 4 years was 16% in each group; hypertension and treatment-specific mineralocorticoid effects are clinically important. NNH not quantifiable from a single central fixed-time toxicity endpoint.

Important limitations

Open-label design and treatment evolution after progression can affect absolute survival comparisons. Absolute effects are reported only where a reliable common timepoint or directly reported rate permits calculation; otherwise NNT/NNH is not forced from medians or hazard ratios.

Primary source

James ND, et al. Int J Cancer. 2022;151:422-434.
