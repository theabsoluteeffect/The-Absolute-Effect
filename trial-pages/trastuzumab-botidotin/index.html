HER2-positive advanced breast cancer · Medical Oncology · Phase III

Trastuzumab botidotin vs T-DM1

Unresectable/metastatic after at least one prior anti-HER2 therapy. Primary trial report and authoritative dosing sources reviewed 17 November 2026.

DesignOpen-label randomized phase III, 1:1
Population365 randomized; A166 n=182; T-DM1 n=183
Primary endpointPFS by blinded independent central review per RECIST 1.1.
Follow-upMedian follow-up 14.9 months at 26 April 2025 data cutoff; OS immature.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 365 patients with HER2-positive unresectable/metastatic breast cancer after at least one prior anti-HER2 therapy randomized 1:1; median age 55 years, 73.4% visceral metastases, 53.4% had received ≥2 prior anti-HER2 therapies and 55.9% prior pyrotinib.

Intervention: Trastuzumab botidotin (A166) 4.8 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks until progression or unacceptable toxicity.

Comparator: Trastuzumab emtansine (T-DM1) 3.6 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks until progression or unacceptable toxicity.

Primary endpoint: PFS by blinded independent central review per RECIST 1.1.

Follow-up: Median follow-up 14.9 months at 26 April 2025 data cutoff; OS immature.

Registry: NCT06968585

Median PFS 11.1 versus 4.4 months; HR 0.39 (95% CI 0.30–0.51), P<0.0001. ORR 76.9% versus 53.0%; median duration of response 12.2 versus 5.7 months. OS HR 0.62 (95% CI 0.38–1.03), immature.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 TEAEs occurred in 69.8% versus 63.7%. Common grade ≥3 events (≥5%) with A166 included corneal disorder, dry eye and blurred vision; with T-DM1, platelet count decrease, neutrophil count decrease, hypokalaemia and GGT increase. Among A166-treated patients with ocular AEs, instrumental ADL limitation occurred in 37 (20.3%) and self-care limitation in 13 (7.1%); these resolved in 86.5% and 92.3%, respectively. Discontinuation 1.1% versus 3.8%; no TEAE-related death with A166 versus 1.1% with T-DM1.

No NNT is calculated: median time-to-event outcomes are not converted to NNT without a common-horizon absolute risk difference.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No validated comparative QoL superiority reported; ocular symptoms can affect instrumental and self-care ADLs, usually resolving.

Interpretation and limitations

A166 significantly prolonged PFS and response duration versus T-DM1 in the studied post-anti-HER2 setting, with frequent but often reversible ocular toxicity.

OS immature; ocular toxicity is a distinctive treatment burden. The exact dosing and safety were also reported in the Annals of Oncology LBA24 conference abstract.
