Trastuzumab botidotin vs T-DM1

Unresectable/metastatic after trastuzumab and taxane. Primary-source correction 17 November 2026; unresolved details are explicitly held.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 365 adults with HER2-positive unresectable/metastatic breast cancer previously treated with trastuzumab and a taxane; 182 received trastuzumab botidotin and 183 T-DM1.

Intervention: Trastuzumab botidotin intravenously every 3 weeks per protocol; exact dose to be taken from the full protocol/supplement rather than inferred from other ADCs.

Comparator: Trastuzumab emtansine intravenously every 3 weeks per protocol.

Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival by blinded independent central review, analyzed in the intention-to-treat population.

Follow-up and maturity: Median follow-up 14.9 months; OS was immature (medians not reached).

Registry: NCT06968585. Arm denominators and endpoint definitions are preserved as reported; no missing denominator is inferred.

Median PFS 11.1 versus 4.4 months; HR 0.39 (95% CI 0.30–0.51), nominal P<0.0001. ORR 76.9% (95% CI 70.1–82.8) versus 53.0% (95% CI 45.5–60.4). OS HR 0.62 (95% CI 0.38–1.03), immature.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 TEAEs occurred in 127/182 (69.8%) versus 116/183 (63.7%). Ocular treatment-related events were frequent with trastuzumab botidotin but generally recovered/resolved with the protocol algorithm; pulmonary, hematologic, hepatic and GI toxicities were relatively infrequent.

No NNT is calculated: No same-horizon absolute PFS event-free rates are supplied in the verified primary abstract; median PFS and HR cannot be converted to NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive quantitative QoL superiority was reported.

Interpretation and limitations

Trastuzumab botidotin significantly prolonged PFS versus T-DM1 in the studied post-trastuzumab/taxane setting. No NNT is derived from medians or HR alone.

OS is immature; ocular toxicity is a distinctive burden. The previous staged page's 12-month PFS rates and NNT were unsupported and have been removed.
