Trastuzumab botidotin vs T-DM1

HER2-positive advanced breast cancer previously treated with trastuzumab and a taxane. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignOpen-label randomized phase III trial
Population365 patients with HER2-positive advanced breast cancer after prior trastuzumab/taxane therapy randomized to trastuzumab botidotin or T-DM1.
Primary endpointProgression-free survival.
Data maturityPrimary PFS analysis reported; OS follow-up is immature.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 365 patients with HER2-positive advanced breast cancer after prior trastuzumab/taxane therapy randomized to trastuzumab botidotin or T-DM1.

Intervention: Trastuzumab botidotin 4.8 mg/kg intravenously every 3 weeks.

Comparator: Trastuzumab emtansine 3.6 mg/kg intravenously every 3 weeks.

Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival.

Follow-up and maturity: Primary PFS analysis reported; OS follow-up is immature.

Registry: NCT06968585. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

Median PFS 14.5 versus 10.2 months; HR approximately 0.73, P=0.01. Twelve-month PFS was 46.0% versus 15.3%, an absolute difference of 30.7 percentage points.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 treatment-emergent adverse events occurred in 69.8% versus 63.7%; cytopenias, neuropathy and ADC-related toxicity require monitoring.

NNT 4Progression-free survival · 12 months

46% versus 15.3%; absolute benefit 30.7 percentage points. Alive without progression at 12 months (Kaplan–Meier estimate). Calculation: ceil(100 / (46.0 − 15.3)) = ceil(3.26) = 4.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive quantitative QoL advantage was reported in the primary abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

The trial suggests improved PFS over T-DM1 in this post-trastuzumab/taxane setting; longer follow-up and sequencing data are needed.

New ADC with limited long-term safety follow-up; cross-trial comparisons with other HER2 ADCs are invalid. The 12-month PFS NNT is a landmark secondary estimate, not the primary median PFS comparison.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
