The Absolute Effect / Evidence / Small-Cell Lung Cancer · Medical Oncology · Relapsed disease

TAISHAN-302

Clinical question

In SCLC progressing after first-line platinum-based therapy, does tambotatug pelitecan improve outcomes compared with topotecan?

Trial context

451 patients; phase III, randomized, open-label. Tam-Peli 2.0 mg/kg IV day 1 every 21 days versus topotecan. Primary endpoint: OS.

Absolute Effect

Median OS 13.3 vs 9.4 months; HR 0.46 (95% CI 0.35–0.62; P<0.001). Median PFS 7.4 vs 2.8 months; HR 0.29 (95% CI 0.23–0.37; P<0.001). ORR 59.1% vs 9.7%: absolute increase 49.4 percentage points.

Toxicity

Grade ≥3 adverse events were lower with Tam-Peli in the NEJM interim analysis (55.4% vs 77.9%). ILD/pneumonitis occurred in 4.9% vs 1.4%.

Mechanism

Tam-Peli (YL201) is a B7-H3/CD276-directed antibody–drug conjugate. Its antibody binds B7-H3 on tumour cells, the complex is internalized, and a protease-cleavable linker releases a camptothecin-derived/topoisomerase-I inhibitor payload. The payload inhibits topoisomerase I, causing replication-associated DNA damage, cell-cycle arrest and apoptosis. The TMALIN platform also permits tumour-microenvironment activation and extracellular payload release, supporting a bystander effect.

Primary publication
