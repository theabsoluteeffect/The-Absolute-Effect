Clinical question: Does anbenitamab plus HB1801 improve PFS over docetaxel-trastuzumab-pertuzumab in first-line HER2-positive advanced breast cancer?

24-month PFS 58.5% vs 34.4%; HR 0.46 (95% CI 0.37–0.56; P<0.001); absolute gain 24.1 points; NNT 5. ORR 87.6% vs 73.4%. · Registry:

Design, population and treatment

Phase III, multicentre, open-label, randomised trial in China; 885 patients with HER2-positive advanced breast cancer assigned 1:1 to anbenitamab plus HB1801 (n=445) or docetaxel–trastuzumab–pertuzumab (THP; n=440). The study addresses first-line advanced disease; the accessible abstract does not provide full eligibility detail or exact dose/cycle schedule.
Intervention/comparator: anbenitamab plus albumin-bound docetaxel HB1801 versus THP. Exact doses and treatment duration are not stated in the accessible NEJM/PubMed abstract; they remain unverified and are not inferred from other KN026 studies.
Primary endpoint: PFS by blinded independent central review. ORR was a secondary endpoint. At the prespecified interim analysis, 401 PFS events had occurred; median follow-up was 21.0 months.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 AEs were 204/444 (45.9%) with anbenitamab plus HB1801 and 151/439 (34.4%) with THP; absolute excess 11.5 points. Neutropenia: 13.1% vs 8.4%; adverse-event deaths: 2 patients in each group. No NNH is calculated because the abstract does not report a common fixed-time toxicity horizon.
For 24-month PFS, the absolute benefit was 24.1 percentage points; NNT 5 (1/0.241=4.15, rounded up).

Interpretation and limitations

Current-practice context · 23 November 2026: The interim analysis showed improved 24-month PFS versus THP, with higher grade ≥3 adverse-event incidence. Exact dose/schedule and full supplementary eligibility, stratification, safety and QoL details were not accessible; these are not inferred. No current NCCN recommendation category or reference is claimed.
Limitations: interim analysis, median follow-up 21.0 months, and the accessible abstract does not provide full treatment exposure/discontinuation or patient-reported outcomes. NNT applies only to 24-month PFS; no NNH is assigned.
