Breast Cancer · Medical · Relapsed / Refractory

HER2CLIMB

In heavily pretreated HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer, including brain metastases, does tucatinib added to trastuzumab–capecitabine improve outcomes?

Trial design and population

Population

612 patients previously treated with trastuzumab, pertuzumab and T-DM1; stable or active brain metastases were eligible.

Intervention vs comparator

Tucatinib 300 mg orally twice daily plus trastuzumab and capecitabine vs placebo plus trastuzumab and capecitabine.

Sample size

612

Primary endpoint

PFS; OS key secondary endpoint

Key results

Updated median OS was 24.7 vs 19.2 months (HR 0.73, 95% CI 0.59–0.90); primary-analysis median PFS was 7.8 vs 5.6 months (HR 0.54, 95% CI 0.42–0.71). Benefit was seen in patients with brain metastases.

Absolute Effect

Median OS difference 5.5 months is not an NNT. CNS activity is a major clinically relevant finding.

NNT: Not calculated; median OS difference is not a valid NNT. Endpoint/time horizon are stated above; do not convert median survival differences into NNT.

NNH: Not quantifiable. Grade ≥3 diarrhoea and aminotransferase elevation are important; capecitabine causes hand-foot syndrome. Diarrhoea, hepatotoxicity and drug interactions require monitoring.

Safety and treatment burden

Grade ≥3 diarrhoea and aminotransferase elevation are important; capecitabine causes hand-foot syndrome. Diarrhoea, hepatotoxicity and drug interactions require monitoring.

Follow-up, maturity and limitations

Updated survival analyses with multi-year follow-up.

Evidence follows prior trastuzumab, pertuzumab and T-DM1. Modern sequencing often places trastuzumab deruxtecan earlier, but inclusion of active brain metastases remains distinctive.

Clinical interpretation

Tucatinib–trastuzumab–capecitabine improves PFS and OS and provides meaningful benefit for HER2-positive disease with brain metastases after multiple prior therapies.

Current-practice context · reviewed 20 October 2026

Historical evidence is separated from current sequencing. Confirm local regulatory status, current guidelines, biomarkers, prior therapies and patient-specific contraindications before applying these results. Newer treatments may have changed the preferred sequence; where NNT/NNH cannot be defensibly quantified, no surrogate calculation is made.
