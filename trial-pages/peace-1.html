Prostate Cancer · Medical Oncology

PEACE-1

In de novo metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer, does abiraterone + prednisone added to ADT ± docetaxel improve rPFS and OS?

Population
1,172 randomized men with de novo metastatic disease; 2×2 factorial design testing abiraterone and prostate radiotherapy.
Treatment
ADT ± docetaxel 75 mg/m² every 3 weeks for 6 cycles, with or without abiraterone 1,000 mg daily + prednisone 5 mg twice daily; prostate RT was a separate factorial intervention.
Overall population
rPFS HR 0.54 (99.9% CI 0.41–0.71); OS HR 0.82 (95.1% CI 0.69–0.98).
ADT + docetaxel subgroup
rPFS HR 0.50 (99.9% CI 0.34–0.71); OS HR 0.75 (95.1% CI 0.59–0.95), P=.017.

Absolute Effect

Fixed-time OS/rPFS percentages were not used to manufacture an NNT from hazard ratios. The clinically relevant effect is therefore presented as HRs plus the treatment population and follow-up rather than a misleading median-derived NNT.

Key result: In the docetaxel population, the OS HR was 0.75. The trial also found no interaction between abiraterone and radiotherapy, supporting pooled assessment of abiraterone efficacy.

Safety: Grade ≥3 adverse events in the docetaxel population were 63% with abiraterone versus 52% without; hypertension was 22% versus 13%.

Interpretation & limitations

The factorial design simultaneously addresses systemic intensification and prostate radiotherapy. It is important not to use the abiraterone result as evidence that prostate RT and abiraterone are interchangeable interventions.

Primary source

Fizazi K, et al. Lancet. 2022;399:1695–1707.
