Gastric and Gastroesophageal Junction Cancer · Medical · Advanced / Metastatic · First-line HER2-negative advanced/recurrent gastric/GEJ cancer in East Asia

ATTRACTION-4

Clinical question: In first-line her2-negative advanced/recurrent gastric/gej cancer in east asia, does the investigational strategy improve clinically meaningful outcomes versus its control?

Registry: NCT02746796 · One underlying study; aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase III, double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT; 724 patients. Evidence cut-off: 13 November 2026.

Design, population and eligibility

Design / sample size

Phase III, double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT; 724 patients.

Eligibility

Previously untreated HER2-negative unresectable advanced or recurrent gastric/GEJ cancer in Japan, South Korea and Taiwan.

Intervention

Nivolumab + SOX or CAPOX versus placebo + SOX or CAPOX.

Nivolumab 360 mg IV every 3 weeks with oxaliplatin-based S-1 or capecitabine chemotherapy.

Comparator

placebo + SOX or CAPOX.

Primary endpoint: Co-primary centrally assessed PFS and OS.

Key efficacy outcomes

Initial PFS 10.45 vs 8.34 months, HR 0.68; OS 17.45 vs 17.15 months, HR 0.90 (95% CI 0.75–1.08), p=0.26. At 3-year follow-up, PFS 10.94 vs 8.48 months (HR 0.67); OS remained similar (HR 0.89, 95% CI 0.75–1.05).

Reported outcome
10.45

Comparator: 8.34. The bars are a simple display of the first reported pair only; consult the endpoint and time horizon above.

Safety and treatment delivery

Grade 3–4 neutrophil decrease 20% vs 16%; platelet decrease 9% vs 9%; treatment-related deaths 3 vs 3.

Clinical interpretation and limitations

PFS-positive but OS-negative. Subsequent therapies and regional population context may affect OS interpretation. This is not evidence of OS superiority.

Interpret the reported hazard ratios in the context of the prespecified endpoint, statistical hierarchy, follow-up maturity, crossover/subsequent therapy and enrolled population. Median survival differences are not risk differences and must not be converted into NNT.

Absolute effect

No NNT/NNH is displayed unless an absolute event-risk difference at a clearly stated fixed timepoint can be calculated from reported trial data. Median survival differences and hazard ratios alone do not support an NNT. The visual above uses reported outcome data only.
