FIGHT-203

In FGFR1-rearranged myeloid/lymphoid neoplasms, can pemigatinib induce complete responses and complete cytogenetic responses?

Registry: NCT03011372 · Evidence reviewed 9 November 2026 · Aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase II open-label single-arm; 47 treated, 45 efficacy-evaluable.Primary-source evidence summary

Study design, population and eligibility

Design and sample

Phase II open-label single-arm; 47 treated, 45 efficacy-evaluable.

Population

Myeloid/lymphoid neoplasms with FGFR1 rearrangement; chronic and blast phase included.

Treatment and comparator

Intervention: Pemigatinib 13.5 mg daily (2 weeks on/1 week off or continuous).

Comparator: No concurrent control.

Endpoints

Complete response rate primary.

Key efficacy and clinical outcomes

Central-review CR 74% overall (31/42), 96% chronic phase and 44% blast phase; complete cytogenetic response 73%.

Toxicity and treatment burden

Hyperphosphataemia 76%; grade ≥3 stomatitis 19%, anaemia 15%.

Absolute effect and NNT/NNH

Rare-disease single-arm evidence; transplant eligibility and chronic vs blast phase are central to interpretation.

NNT/NNH is not calculated unless a directly reported, comparable fixed-time risk difference supports it. Median survival and hazard ratios alone do not provide an NNT.
