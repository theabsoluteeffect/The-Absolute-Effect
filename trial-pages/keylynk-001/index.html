Ovarian/fallopian tube/primary peritoneal cancer · Medical Oncology · Phase III

KEYLYNK-001 / ENGOT-OV43 / GOG-3036

First-line maintenance strategy for advanced BRCA-non-mutated epithelial ovarian cancer. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignDouble-blind randomized phase III trial
Population1367 patients with newly diagnosed advanced BRCA-non-mutated epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer randomized to chemotherapy/immunotherapy and maintenance strategies.
Primary endpointProgression-free survival and overall survival in prespecified populations.
Data maturityPrimary PFS and OS analyses reported; OS was not improved at the reported cutoff.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 1367 patients with newly diagnosed advanced BRCA-non-mutated epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer randomized to chemotherapy/immunotherapy and maintenance strategies.

Intervention: Pembrolizumab 200 mg intravenously every 3 weeks with carboplatin/paclitaxel, followed by maintenance pembrolizumab plus olaparib 300 mg orally twice daily in the experimental strategy.

Comparator: Placebo plus chemotherapy with protocol maintenance olaparib/placebo strategies; the trial included more than one randomized treatment strategy.

Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival and overall survival in prespecified populations.

Follow-up and maturity: Primary PFS and OS analyses reported; OS was not improved at the reported cutoff.

Registry: NCT03740165. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

Median PFS 22.2 versus 14.6 months; HR 0.71, P=0.0003. Median OS 47.7 versus 47.1 months; HR 1.01, P=0.92. PFS benefit did not translate into an OS advantage.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 adverse events were approximately 82.3% versus 76.5%; cytopenias, fatigue, nausea and immune-mediated events add to chemotherapy/maintenance burden.

No NNT is calculated because Median PFS/OS and HR are time-to-event measures; no common fixed-time absolute risk difference was used for NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL superiority was established in the primary report.

Interpretation and limitations

KEYLYNK-001 demonstrates a PFS advantage for the pembrolizumab/olaparib-containing strategy in the studied population, without an OS benefit at the reported analysis.

Multi-arm design, BRCA-non-mutated population and multiple maintenance strategies require careful interpretation. The PFS result should not be described as an OS benefit.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
