Design, population and treatment

Design: Double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized phase III trial.

Eligibility and population: 461 patients with resectable stage IIA–IIIB NSCLC, including tumors ≥4 cm or node-positive disease; EGFR/ALK-driven tumors were excluded from the key intended population.

Intervention: Neoadjuvant nivolumab 360 mg IV every 3 weeks with platinum-doublet chemotherapy for 4 cycles, surgery, then adjuvant nivolumab 480 mg IV every 4 weeks for up to 13 cycles.

Comparator: Neoadjuvant placebo with the same platinum-doublet chemotherapy, surgery, then adjuvant placebo.

Randomization and endpoint: Event-free survival. The full randomization stratification variables and protocol amendments remain subject to protocol-level reconciliation before release.

Key efficacy outcome

18-month EFS was 70.2% versus 50.0% (HR 0.58, 97.36% CI 0.42–0.81; p<0.001). Pathologic complete response was 25.3% versus 4.7%. The 18-month landmark difference is 20.2 percentage points; NNT 5 (ceil[1/0.202]).

Data maturity: Primary analysis; 18-month EFS landmark reported. Overall survival remains immature. Primary versus secondary endpoints are kept distinct; no survival estimate is inferred from a hazard ratio or median alone.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3–4 treatment-related adverse events occurred in 32.5% versus 25.2%. Adjuvant discontinuation and surgical timing require full table-level review; no mature OS benefit is claimed.

Delivery and discontinuation: Four cycles of neoadjuvant chemoimmunotherapy, definitive surgery and up to one year of adjuvant nivolumab; postoperative recovery and immune toxicity may affect treatment completion.

Only same-endpoint, same-time-horizon absolute differences are eligible for NNT/NNH. Median survival and hazard ratios alone are not used. 18-month event-free survival: 20.2 percentage-point absolute benefit at 18 months; NNT 5.

Interpretation and limitations

Perioperative nivolumab improved EFS and pCR compared with chemotherapy alone in resectable NSCLC. Multidisciplinary surgical fitness, molecular testing and completion of postoperative therapy remain essential.

Limitations: The primary result is EFS, not mature OS; eligibility and molecular exclusions matter. NNT is an 18-month KM landmark estimate, not a lifetime treatment NNT.
