Reported outcomes

Four cycles of TIP were evaluated in a UK MRC phase II study. Among 43 patients assessable for response, 8 achieved CR and 18 achieved marker-negative partial remission (favourable response 60%; 95% CI 44–75%). One-year survival was 70% and failure-free survival 36%.

Interpretation and limitations

This regimen schedule produced lower response results than the original single-centre Kondagunta TIP series; differences in dose intensity and growth-factor support are important. Histology was mixed, not seminoma-only.

Guideline context: ESMO–EURACAN recommends standard-dose VIP/PEI, TIP or VeIP as conventional first salvage options after relapse following cisplatin-based chemotherapy.
