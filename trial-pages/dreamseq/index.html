DREAMseq (ECOG-ACRIN EA6134)

In BRAF V600-mutant metastatic melanoma, is immunotherapy-first sequencing superior to BRAF/MEK-targeted therapy-first sequencing?

Registry: NCT02224781 · Evidence reviewed 9 November 2026 · Aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase III randomized sequencing trial; n=265; stopped early for efficacy.Primary-source evidence summary

Study design, population and eligibility

Design and sample

Phase III randomized sequencing trial; n=265; stopped early for efficacy.

Population

Treatment-naive metastatic BRAF V600-mutant melanoma eligible for both strategies.

Treatment and comparator

Intervention: Nivolumab/ipilimumab first, BRAF/MEK therapy at progression.

Comparator: Dabrafenib/trametinib first, immunotherapy at progression.

Endpoints

2-y OS primary.

Key efficacy and clinical outcomes

2-y OS 71.8% immunotherapy-first vs 51.5% targeted-first (p=.010); first-line ORR 46% vs 43%.
