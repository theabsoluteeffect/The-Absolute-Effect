The Absolute Effect / Evidence / EORTC 30994
Bladder Cancer · Medical Oncology · Adjuvant

EORTC 30994

Clinical question

Does immediate adjuvant chemotherapy improve outcomes compared with deferred chemotherapy after radical cystectomy?

Trial context
Population
284 patients with pT3–pT4 and/or N+ M0 urothelial carcinoma after radical cystectomy
Intervention
Immediate adjuvant cisplatin-based chemotherapy vs deferred chemotherapy at relapse
Key endpoint
5-year progression-free survival
Time horizon / follow-up
5 years
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

Immediate chemotherapy increased 5-year progression-free survival from 31.8% to 47.6%, an absolute improvement of 15.8 points (NNT 7). Overall survival was not significantly improved: 5-year OS 53.6% vs 47.7%, HR 0.78, P=.13. The trial closed early and was underpowered for OS.

NNT: 7

Treatment burden / harm

Grade 3–4 myelosuppression

NNH: Not applicable; toxicity comparisons are confounded by more patients receiving deferred treatment at relapse

Relative effect

HR: 0.54

95% CI 0.40–0.73; P<.0001

Clinical interpretation

Interpret the absolute difference together with the endpoint, time horizon, relative effect and limitations of the study design.
