R-ALPS

Clinical question: After chemoradiotherapy for unresectable stage III NSCLC, does benmelstobart with or without anlotinib improve PFS over placebo?

Stage 1 median PFS 15.1, 9.7 and 4.2 months, respectively; HR vs placebo 0.49 and 0.53. Pooled combo vs benmelstobart alone 17.4 vs 11.2 months; HR 0.82 (95% CI 0.63–1.08; P=0.12), not significant. · Registry:

Design, population and treatment

Phase III, multicentre, double-blind, placebo-controlled randomised trial at 42 centres in China. Adults with unresectable stage III NSCLC without progression after concurrent or sequential platinum-based chemoradiotherapy. A total of 553 participants were enrolled: 390 in stage 1 (1:1:1 to combination, benmelstobart alone or placebo) and 163 in stage 2 (1:1 between the two active regimens to power their direct comparison).
Regimens (protocol): benmelstobart/TQB2450 1,200 mg IV day 1 every 3 weeks for up to 36 cycles; anlotinib 8 mg orally once daily on days 1–14 of each 21-day cycle for up to 36 cycles. Comparator arms received benmelstobart plus matched anlotinib placebo, or matched placebos. Protocol radiotherapy was 60 Gy ±10% (54–66 Gy); first study medication was scheduled 14–42 days after RT.
Primary endpoint: IRC-assessed PFS. Secondary endpoints included OS, ORR, DCR, DOR, safety and investigator-assessed 6-/12-month PFS. QoL questionnaire assessments were planned in the protocol; numerical QoL results were not in the accessible primary abstract.
Analysis maturity: stage-1 cutoff 30 November 2023; pooled stage-1/2 cutoff 8 July 2024. Median follow-up was not stated in the accessible JAMA abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

Current-practice context · 23 November 2026: Benmelstobart with or without anlotinib improved PFS versus placebo after chemoradiotherapy, but adding anlotinib did not significantly improve PFS over benmelstobart alone and increased grade 3–5/fatal treatment-related events. This trial did not compare against consolidation durvalumab. The protocol describes a China-specific access context in which enrolled participants had declined durvalumab; results should not displace durvalumab in eligible settings. No NCCN recommendation category or reference is claimed because the current full guideline source was not verified. QoL was planned but numerical results were not in the accessible abstract.
Limitations: stage-1 and pooled stage-1/2 analyses have different data cutoffs; median follow-up and detailed subgroup/safety tables require full-text/supplement review. No NNT is derived from medians or HRs.
