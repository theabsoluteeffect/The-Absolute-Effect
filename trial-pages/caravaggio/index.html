Design, population and treatment

Design: Open-label, randomized, non-inferiority phase III trial.

Eligibility and population: 1,170 patients with active cancer and acute symptomatic or incidental proximal DVT or pulmonary embolism; modified intention-to-treat efficacy population included 1,155 patients.

Intervention: Apixaban 10 mg orally twice daily for 7 days, then 5 mg twice daily for 6 months.

Comparator: Dalteparin 200 IU/kg subcutaneously daily for month 1, then 150 IU/kg daily for months 2–6.

Randomization and endpoint: Recurrent venous thromboembolism during the 6-month treatment period; major bleeding was the principal safety endpoint. The full randomization stratification variables and protocol amendments remain subject to protocol-level reconciliation before release.

Key efficacy outcomes

Recurrent VTE occurred in 5.6% versus 7.9% (HR 0.63, 95% CI 0.37–1.07); apixaban met non-inferiority (p<0.001). Superiority was not demonstrated (p≈0.09). Major bleeding was 3.8% versus 4.0%.

Data maturity: Six-month treatment and follow-up period. Primary versus secondary endpoints are kept distinct; no survival estimate is inferred from a hazard ratio or median alone.

Safety and treatment burden

Major bleeding occurred in 22/576 (3.8%) versus 23/579 (4.0%); no excess major bleeding was observed with apixaban. Clinically relevant non-major bleeding and cancer-site subgroup analyses require full supplement review.

Delivery and discontinuation: Oral twice-daily treatment avoids daily injections; drug–drug interactions, renal function, gastrointestinal/genitourinary lesions and adherence remain important.

Quality of life and patient experience

No validated QoL endpoint was verified in the primary trial report.

Where a separate patient-reported outcome publication exists, it is treated as a secondary analysis attached to this underlying study and not as a separate trial page. Unextracted domains remain explicitly open rather than inferred.

Absolute effect and NNT / NNH

No NNT/NNH is calculated. The primary endpoint is reported as a median survival/PFS estimate or a non-inferiority comparison without a valid same-horizon absolute event difference; NNT/NNH is not derived from median survival or a hazard ratio.

Only same-endpoint, same-time-horizon absolute differences are eligible for NNT/NNH. Median survival and hazard ratios alone are not used. No NNT/NNH appropriate from the reported primary endpoint.

Interpretation and limitations

Apixaban was non-inferior to dalteparin for cancer-associated VTE with similar major bleeding in CARAVAGGIO. The result should not be interpreted as proven superior efficacy; individualized bleeding-risk assessment remains essential.

Limitations: The primary hypothesis was non-inferiority. The numerically lower recurrence rate does not establish superiority; therefore no NNT is calculated. GI/GU bleeding risk and active mucosal lesions may change agent choice.
