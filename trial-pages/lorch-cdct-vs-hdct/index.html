Conventional-dose versus high-dose salvage chemotherapy

In relapsed metastatic GCT, how did conventional-dose salvage chemotherapy compare with high-dose chemotherapy (HDCT)?

International retrospective mixed-GCT comparison

Population
1,594 eligible

Study population as reported in the cited publication; cohort sizes and denominators are stated where available.

Evidence type
Mixed GCT · retrospective comparison

Not all evidence is randomized, comparative, or seminoma-specific.

Reported outcomes

In this retrospective international database, 2-year PFS was 50% with HDCT versus 28% with conventional-dose chemotherapy; 5-year OS was 53% versus 41%. Adjusted HRs favoured HDCT for PFS (0.44) and OS (0.65).

Interpretation and limitations

Non-randomized treatment allocation leaves residual confounding and selection bias. These results prompted a randomized comparison of TIP and TI-CE; they do not prove HDCT is superior for every patient or seminoma subgroup.

Guideline context: ESMO–EURACAN recommends standard-dose VIP/PEI, TIP or VeIP as conventional first salvage options after relapse following cisplatin-based chemotherapy. NCCN Testicular Cancer Guidelines Insights (Version 2.2025) is a contemporary US guideline source. The study summarized here is not necessarily seminoma-specific; histology scope is stated explicitly below.
