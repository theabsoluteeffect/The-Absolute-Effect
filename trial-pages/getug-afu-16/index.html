The Absolute Effect / Evidence

GETUG-AFU 16

Prostate Cancer · Salvage Radiotherapy

Clinical question

Does adding short-term androgen deprivation to salvage radiotherapy improve progression-free outcomes after prostatectomy biochemical recurrence?

Trial context

GETUG-AFU 16 randomized men with biochemical recurrence after prostatectomy to salvage radiotherapy with or without short-term androgen deprivation. The study established an important role for combined treatment in appropriately selected men receiving salvage radiotherapy.

Absolute Effect perspective

The benefit should be interpreted at a defined follow-up point rather than from the hazard ratio alone. The standardized evidence entry presents the reported absolute progression-free difference and its corresponding NNT when defensible.
