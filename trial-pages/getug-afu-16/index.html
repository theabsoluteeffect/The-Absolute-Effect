GETUG-AFU 16

Salvage radiotherapy ± 6 months androgen deprivation after prostatectomy biochemical recurrence

Clinical question

Does adding short-term androgen deprivation to salvage radiotherapy improve progression-free outcomes after prostatectomy biochemical recurrence?

Trial at a glance

Population
Men with biochemical recurrence after radical prostatectomy
Intervention
Salvage radiotherapy + 6 months ADT
Comparator
Salvage radiotherapy alone
Key endpoint
Progression-free survival

Key result

Absolute Effect perspective
≈18 percentage-point improvement at 5 years

The improvement is clinically meaningful. The endpoint and time horizon should accompany any NNT or absolute-effect calculation, and the benefit should be balanced against the adverse effects of androgen deprivation.

Clinical takeaway

GETUG-AFU 16 established that short-term androgen deprivation can improve progression-related outcomes when added to salvage radiotherapy in appropriately selected men with post-prostatectomy biochemical recurrence.
