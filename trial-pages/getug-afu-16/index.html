The Absolute Effect

GETUG-AFU 16

Prostate Cancer · Salvage Radiotherapy

Clinical question

Does adding short-term androgen deprivation to salvage radiotherapy improve progression-free outcomes after prostatectomy biochemical recurrence?

Key result

In the randomized trial, adding 6 months of androgen deprivation to salvage radiotherapy improved progression-free outcomes. At 5 years, progression-free survival was approximately 80% with combined treatment versus 62% with radiotherapy alone, with a hazard ratio of about 0.50.

Absolute Effect perspective

The benefit is clinically meaningful for men with biochemical recurrence, but the endpoint and time horizon must be stated whenever an absolute benefit or NNT is reported. Longer-term outcomes and patient selection remain important when applying the result.
