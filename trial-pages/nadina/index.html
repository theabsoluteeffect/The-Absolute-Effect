Design, population and treatment

Design: Open-label, randomized phase III trial.

Eligibility and population: 423 patients with resectable macroscopic stage III melanoma and clinically detectable nodal disease; randomized to neoadjuvant immunotherapy or surgery followed by adjuvant nivolumab.

Intervention: Two neoadjuvant cycles of ipilimumab 1 mg/kg plus nivolumab 3 mg/kg every 3 weeks, followed by surgery. Postoperative systemic treatment was response-adapted; patients with major pathological response generally omitted adjuvant therapy, while non-MPR patients received adjuvant treatment according to protocol and BRAF status.

Comparator: Immediate surgery followed by adjuvant nivolumab 480 mg IV every 4 weeks for up to 12 cycles.

Randomization and endpoint: Event-free survival. The full randomization stratification variables and protocol amendments remain subject to protocol-level reconciliation before release.

Key efficacy outcomes

12-month EFS was 83.7% versus 57.2% (HR 0.32, 99.9% CI 0.15–0.66; p<0.001). Major pathological response was approximately 59% in the neoadjuvant arm. The 12-month landmark difference is 26.5 percentage points; NNT 4 (ceil[1/0.265]).

Data maturity: Primary analysis with median follow-up approximately 9.9 months; long-term EFS/OS maturity remains limited. Primary versus secondary endpoints are kept distinct; no survival estimate is inferred from a hazard ratio or median alone.

Safety and treatment burden

Grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events occurred in 29.7% versus 14.7%. Immune-related toxicity and timing of surgery are important; full perioperative complication and treatment-completion tables remain open.

Delivery and discontinuation: Two preoperative combination-immunotherapy cycles followed by surgery and response-directed postoperative therapy; toxicity may delay or alter surgery and postoperative treatment.

Quality of life and patient experience

No mature QoL/PRO result was verified in the primary report.

Where a separate patient-reported outcome publication exists, it is treated as a secondary analysis attached to this underlying study and not as a separate trial page. Unextracted domains remain explicitly open rather than inferred.

Only same-endpoint, same-time-horizon absolute differences are eligible for NNT/NNH. Median survival and hazard ratios alone are not used. 12-month event-free survival: 26.5 percentage-point absolute benefit at 12 months; NNT 4.

Interpretation and limitations

NADINA supports neoadjuvant ipilimumab/nivolumab with response-directed postoperative therapy in resectable macroscopic stage III melanoma. Longer follow-up and careful toxicity/surgical planning remain essential.

Limitations: Short primary follow-up, response-adapted postoperative treatment and stage III-only population. NNT is a 12-month KM landmark estimate. The accessible NCCN Melanoma Insights Version 2.2024 predates this primary report and does not cite NADINA.
