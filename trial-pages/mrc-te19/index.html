Germ Cell Tumours · Medical Oncology · Stage I Seminoma

MRC TE19 / EORTC 30982

Clinical question

In stage I seminoma, can single-dose carboplatin replace adjuvant radiotherapy?

Trial context
Population
1,447 men with stage I seminoma after radical inguinal orchiectomy
Intervention
Single-dose carboplatin vs adjuvant radiotherapy
Key endpoint
5-year relapse-free rate
Time horizon / follow-up
5 years
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

Five-year relapse-free rate was 94.7% with single-dose carboplatin versus 96.0% with RT, an absolute difference of -1.3 percentage points. The trial established non-inferiority of carboplatin to RT for relapse control, not superiority. Carboplatin also reduced contralateral testicular GCTs (2 vs 15; HR 0.22). NNT is not appropriate because this is a non-inferiority comparison rather than a demonstrated absolute benefit.

NNT: Not applicable; non-inferiority trial

Treatment burden / harm

Treatment burden and fatigue

NNH: Not quantifiable from the primary publication; carboplatin had less lethargy and less time off work than RT, while acute and late toxicities were otherwise reported descriptively

Relative effect

NON-INFERIOR; HR: 1.25 for relapse with carboplatin vs RT

90% CI 0.83–1.89; non-inferiority margin excluded a doubling of relapse risk

Clinical interpretation

Interpret the absolute difference together with the endpoint, time horizon, relative effect and limitations of the study design.
