Breast Cancer · Medical · Advanced / Metastatic

KEYNOTE-355

In previously untreated advanced TNBC with PD-L1 CPS ≥10, does pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy improve survival versus chemotherapy alone?

Trial design and population

Population / eligibility
847 patients with previously untreated locally recurrent inoperable or metastatic TNBC; benefit is established for PD-L1 CPS ≥10.
Intervention vs comparator
Pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every 3 weeks plus investigator-selected nab-paclitaxel, paclitaxel or gemcitabine–carboplatin vs placebo plus chemotherapy.
Sample size
847
Primary endpoint
OS in PD-L1 CPS ≥10 population

Key results

In CPS ≥10 disease, median OS was 23.0 vs 16.1 months (HR 0.73, 95% CI 0.55–0.95; P=.0185). Median PFS was 9.7 vs 5.6 months (HR 0.65, 95% CI 0.49–0.86).

Absolute Effect

Median OS difference 6.9 months is not an NNT; no fixed-time OS risk difference is used here.

NNT: Not calculated; median OS difference is not a valid NNT. Use only at the stated endpoint/time horizon; median survival differences are not converted into NNT.

NNH: Not quantifiable. Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs occurred in roughly two-thirds of patients in both arms. Pembrolizumab adds immune-mediated endocrinopathy, pneumonitis, hepatitis and colitis; chemotherapy adds neuropathy and cytopenias.

Time-to-event hazard ratios and median survival are not treated as absolute risk differences. Where no defensible fixed-time NNT/NNH is available, the source-reported endpoint is visualized instead.

Safety and treatment burden

Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs occurred in roughly two-thirds of patients in both arms. Pembrolizumab adds immune-mediated endocrinopathy, pneumonitis, hepatitis and colitis; chemotherapy adds neuropathy and cytopenias.

Follow-up, maturity and limitations

Follow-up: Final OS analysis in CPS ≥10 population.

Benefit is tied to PD-L1 CPS ≥10 for first-line metastatic TNBC. Do not extrapolate to CPS <10 or conflate with atezolizumab trials.

Clinical interpretation

For PD-L1 CPS ≥10 advanced TNBC, adding pembrolizumab to chemotherapy improves PFS and OS.
