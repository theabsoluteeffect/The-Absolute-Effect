Extensive-stage small-cell lung cancer · Medical Oncology · Phase III

DeLLphi-305

First-line maintenance after chemoimmunotherapy without progression. Primary trial report and authoritative dosing sources reviewed 24 November 2026.

DesignGlobal randomized open-label phase III; 1:1
Population563 randomized; 282 vs281
Primary endpointOverall survival; PFS was a key secondary endpoint.
Follow-upPrespecified interim analysis; median follow-up approximately 16 months.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 563 patients without progression after first-line platinum-etoposide plus durvalumab were randomized: tarlatamab-durvalumab n=282 and durvalumab n=281. Patients with treated or untreated asymptomatic brain metastases were eligible.

Intervention: Tarlatamab IV step-up schedule: 1 mg on cycle 1 day 1, 10 mg on days 8 and 15, then 10 mg every 2 weeks; durvalumab 1500 mg IV every 4 weeks. The tarlatamab schedule is confirmed in the 2026 FDA prescribing information; durvalumab schedule in the trial report.

Comparator: Durvalumab 1500 mg IV every 4 weeks alone.

Primary endpoint: Overall survival; PFS was a key secondary endpoint.

Follow-up: Prespecified interim analysis; median follow-up approximately 16 months.

Registry: NCT06211036

At median OS follow-up of 15.9 months with the combination and 16.1 months with durvalumab, median OS was 21.1 months (95% CI 20.8–not estimable) versus 12.7 months (95% CI 11.2–14.8); HR 0.42 (95% CI 0.32–0.54; P<0.001). Median PFS was 7.4 months (95% CI 5.6–9.1) versus 1.9 months (95% CI 1.9–2.0); HR 0.34 (95% CI 0.28–0.42; P<0.001), with median PFS follow-up 13.9 months in each arm. Confirmed ORR was 26.4% (95% CI 20.9–32.5) versus 4.1% (95% CI 2.0–7.4; P<0.001); median duration of response was 12.7 versus 5.6 months.

Absolute effect and NNT

12-month OS: 79% versus 53% in the ESMO 2026 LBA3 conference report/official sponsor data summary. Absolute benefit 26 percentage points; calculation: ceil[1/0.26]=4. This is an approximate time-specific NNT based on Kaplan–Meier landmark rates, not on median OS or the hazard ratio.

NNT 4 at 12 months

Absolute OS benefit 26 percentage points (79%−53%). Calculation: ceil[1/0.26]=4.

12-month PFS: 38% versus 10% in the same conference report. Absolute benefit 28 percentage points; calculation: ceil[1/0.28]=4.

NNT 4 at 12 months

Absolute PFS benefit 28 percentage points (38%−10%). Calculation: ceil[1/0.28]=4.

No NNT is derived from median OS/PFS values or HRs; the two landmark NNTs are approximate and depend on the ESMO/sponsor-reported Kaplan–Meier estimates.

Safety and treatment burden

Grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in 46.8% versus 40.6%; grade 3–4 treatment-related adverse events in 20.9% versus 10.8%. CRS occurred in 49.6% of combination-treated patients, predominantly grade 1–2; one patient (0.4%) had grade 3 CRS and none had grade 4 CRS. ICANS occurred in 1.1%. Adverse events led to treatment discontinuation in 5.7% versus 5.8%. No treatment-related deaths were reported with the combination; two occurred with durvalumab alone.

Quality of life and treatment burden

Quality-of-life outcomes were not reported in the primary NEJM abstract or the ESMO LBA3 summary; no QoL benefit is inferred.

Interpretation and limitations

Maintenance tarlatamab plus durvalumab significantly improved OS and PFS over durvalumab alone after first-line chemoimmunotherapy in ES-SCLC. The exact tarlatamab step-up schedule is supported by the FDA label; no unsupported 12-month NNT is presented.

Interim analysis with relatively short follow-up; only patients without progression after induction were randomized. The trial is a maintenance strategy and does not compare tarlatamab with first-line induction therapy.

Current-practice context · reviewed 17 November 2026
