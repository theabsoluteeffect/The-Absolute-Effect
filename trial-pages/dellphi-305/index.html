Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 563 patients without progression after initial platinum-etoposide plus durvalumab were randomized: tarlatamab-durvalumab n=282 and durvalumab n=281.

Intervention: Tarlatamab plus durvalumab as maintenance; tarlatamab administered with protocol step-up dosing and subsequent scheduled doses, durvalumab per standard maintenance schedule.

Comparator: Durvalumab maintenance alone.

Primary endpoint: Overall survival; PFS was a key secondary endpoint, measured from maintenance randomization.

Follow-up and maturity: Prespecified interim analysis; median follow-up 15.9 months in the combination arm and 16.1 months in control.

Registry: NCT06211036. The primary analysis population and denominators are stated explicitly.

Median OS 21.1 versus 12.7 months; HR 0.42 (95% CI 0.32–0.54), P<0.001. Median PFS 7.4 versus 1.9 months; HR 0.34 (0.28–0.42), P<0.001. Officially reported 12-month OS was approximately 79% versus 53%, a 26-point difference (NNT 4); exact Kaplan–Meier figure values should be reconciled with the NEJM figure before final release.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 46.8% versus 40.6%. Cytokine release syndrome occurred in nearly half of combination-treated patients, predominantly grade 1–2; ICANS and infection monitoring are important.

NNT 4

79% versus 53%; absolute benefit 26 percentage points at 12 months. Alive at 12 months (rounded official sponsor-reported landmark). Calculation: ceil(100 / (79 − 53)) = 4; rounded sponsor-reported landmark pending exact NEJM figure check.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage reported in the interim abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

Maintenance tarlatamab plus durvalumab significantly improved OS and PFS over durvalumab alone after first-line chemoimmunotherapy in ES-SCLC.

Interim analysis with relatively short follow-up; the population is restricted to patients who did not progress during induction. The 12-month OS NNT uses rounded sponsor-reported rates and remains subject to figure verification.
