Extensive-stage small-cell lung cancer · Medical Oncology · Phase III

DeLLphi-305

First-line maintenance after chemoimmunotherapy without progression. Primary trial report and authoritative dosing sources reviewed 17 November 2026.

DesignGlobal randomized open-label phase III; 1:1
Population563 randomized; 282 vs281
Primary endpointOverall survival; PFS was a key secondary endpoint.
Follow-upPrespecified interim analysis; median follow-up approximately 16 months.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 563 patients without progression after first-line platinum-etoposide plus durvalumab were randomized: tarlatamab-durvalumab n=282 and durvalumab n=281. Patients with treated or untreated asymptomatic brain metastases were eligible.

Intervention: Tarlatamab IV step-up schedule: 1 mg on cycle 1 day 1, 10 mg on days 8 and 15, then 10 mg every 2 weeks; durvalumab 1500 mg IV every 4 weeks. The tarlatamab schedule is confirmed in the 2026 FDA prescribing information; durvalumab schedule in the trial report.

Comparator: Durvalumab 1500 mg IV every 4 weeks alone.

Primary endpoint: Overall survival; PFS was a key secondary endpoint.

Follow-up: Prespecified interim analysis; median follow-up approximately 16 months.

Registry: NCT06211036

Median OS 21.1 versus 12.7 months; HR 0.42 (95% CI 0.32–0.54), P<0.001. Median PFS 7.4 versus 1.9 months; HR 0.34 (95% CI 0.28–0.42), P<0.001. No NNT is calculated because the primary report's median/HR results are not converted to a fixed-horizon risk difference.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 46.8% versus 40.6%. CRS occurred in 49.6% with the combination, predominantly grade 1–2. The accessible primary abstract does not separately quantify ICANS; no rate is inferred. Monitor for CRS and neurologic toxicity including ICANS.

No NNT is calculated: median time-to-event outcomes are not converted to NNT without a common-horizon absolute risk difference.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage reported in the interim primary report.

Interpretation and limitations

Maintenance tarlatamab plus durvalumab significantly improved OS and PFS over durvalumab alone after first-line chemoimmunotherapy in ES-SCLC. The exact tarlatamab step-up schedule is supported by the FDA label; no unsupported 12-month NNT is presented.

Interim analysis with relatively short follow-up; only patients without progression after induction were randomized. The trial is a maintenance strategy and does not compare tarlatamab with first-line induction therapy.

Current-practice context · reviewed 17 November 2026

Trial conclusions are not guideline recommendations. NCCN provenance is not verified because the complete guideline/reference list was inaccessible; no recommendation category or citation number is assigned.
