The Absolute Effect / Evidence / DBCG 82b
Breast Cancer · Radiation Oncology · Locoregional Disease

DBCG 82b

Clinical question

Does postmastectomy radiotherapy improve outcomes in high-risk premenopausal stage II/III breast cancer?

Trial context
Population
1,708 premenopausal women with high-risk stage II/III breast cancer
Intervention
Mastectomy + postmastectomy RT + systemic therapy vs mastectomy + systemic therapy
Key endpoint
10-year overall survival
Time horizon / follow-up
10 years
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

DBCG 82b increased 10-year overall survival from 45% to 54%, an absolute improvement of 9 percentage points (NNT 12). Ten-year locoregional recurrence fell from 32% to 9%, an absolute reduction of 23 points (NNT 5). NNT for OS is based on 10-year survival.

NNT: 12

Treatment burden / harm

Radiotherapy toxicity

NNH: Not quantifiable from the selected long-term endpoint

Relative effect

OS absolute benefit: 10-year OS 54% vs 45%

P<.001

Clinical interpretation

Interpret the absolute difference together with the endpoint, time horizon, relative effect and limitations of the study design.
