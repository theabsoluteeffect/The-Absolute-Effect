The Absolute Effect

JAVELIN Bladder 100

Bladder Cancer · Maintenance Metastatic Therapy

Clinical question

Does maintenance avelumab after platinum chemotherapy improve overall survival in advanced urothelial carcinoma?

Population and intervention

Patients with advanced urothelial carcinoma whose disease had not progressed after 4 to 6 cycles of first-line platinum-based chemotherapy were randomized to maintenance avelumab plus best supportive care or best supportive care alone.

Key result

In the primary analysis population, median overall survival was approximately 21.4 months with avelumab versus 14.3 months with best supportive care, with a hazard ratio around 0.69.

Absolute Effect perspective

The strategy changes treatment timing rather than simply extending first-line chemotherapy: chemotherapy is stopped after disease control and maintenance immunotherapy is introduced. The survival difference is clinically meaningful and should be presented together with the eligible population, follow-up and immune-related treatment burden.

Clinical takeaway

JAVELIN Bladder 100 established first-line maintenance avelumab as an important treatment strategy for patients with advanced urothelial carcinoma whose disease remains controlled after platinum chemotherapy.
