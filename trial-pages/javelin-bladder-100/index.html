The Absolute Effect

JAVELIN Bladder 100

Bladder Cancer · Maintenance Metastatic Therapy

Clinical question

Does maintenance avelumab after platinum chemotherapy improve overall survival in advanced urothelial carcinoma?

Key result

Maintenance avelumab after 4–6 cycles of platinum-based chemotherapy improved overall survival compared with best supportive care. In the primary analysis population, median overall survival was approximately 21.4 months with avelumab versus 14.3 months with best supportive care, with a hazard ratio around 0.69.

Absolute Effect perspective

This is a patient-centred maintenance strategy: chemotherapy is stopped after response or stable disease and immunotherapy is continued. The absolute survival benefit should always be presented with its population, follow-up and treatment duration.
