JAVELIN Bladder 100

First-line maintenance avelumab after platinum chemotherapy

Clinical question

Does maintenance avelumab after platinum chemotherapy improve overall survival in advanced urothelial carcinoma?

Trial at a glance

Population
Advanced urothelial carcinoma without progression after 4–6 cycles of platinum chemotherapy
Intervention
Maintenance avelumab + best supportive care
Comparator
Best supportive care alone
Key endpoint
Overall survival

Key result

Overall survival
21.4 vs 14.3 months

Median overall survival was approximately 21.4 months with avelumab versus 14.3 months with best supportive care, with a hazard ratio around 0.69.

The strategy changes treatment timing rather than simply extending first-line chemotherapy: chemotherapy is stopped after disease control and maintenance immunotherapy is introduced.

Absolute Effect perspective

The survival difference is clinically meaningful. It should be presented together with the eligible population, follow-up and immune-related treatment burden.

JAVELIN Bladder 100 established first-line maintenance avelumab as an important treatment strategy for patients whose disease remains controlled after platinum chemotherapy.
