The Absolute Effect / Evidence

JAVELIN Bladder 100

Bladder Cancer · Maintenance Metastatic

Clinical question

Does maintenance avelumab after platinum chemotherapy improve overall survival in advanced urothelial carcinoma?

Trial context

JAVELIN Bladder 100 established maintenance immune checkpoint blockade after disease control or stable disease following platinum chemotherapy as an important treatment strategy in advanced urothelial carcinoma.

Absolute Effect perspective

The survival benefit should be interpreted as an absolute difference at a defined time point, together with the treatment-related adverse-event burden and the proportion of patients able to proceed to maintenance therapy.
