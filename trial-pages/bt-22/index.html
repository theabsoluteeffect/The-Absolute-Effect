Biliary Tract Cancer · Medical · Advanced / Metastatic

Japanese BT22

In advanced biliary tract cancer, does gemcitabine plus cisplatin improve outcomes over gemcitabine alone in a Japanese phase II study?

Trial design and population

Population / eligibility
83 Japanese patients with advanced biliary tract cancer in a randomized phase II study.
Intervention vs comparator
Gemcitabine plus cisplatin vs gemcitabine alone.
Sample size
83
Primary endpoint
Overall survival

Key results

Median OS was approximately 11.2 months with gemcitabine–cisplatin vs 7.7 months with gemcitabine alone; phase II study and estimates are imprecise. The study helped support subsequent confirmatory testing, including ABC-02.

Safety and treatment burden

Cisplatin adds myelosuppression, nausea/vomiting, nephrotoxicity and electrolyte loss; use the original trial report for exact arm-specific grade ≥3 toxicity.

Follow-up, maturity and limitations

Small phase II Japanese study; do not treat as equivalent in precision to the larger phase III ABC-02 trial.

Clinical interpretation

BT22 was an early randomized signal supporting GemCis in advanced BTC. ABC-02 is the larger confirmatory study and should be read alongside it.
