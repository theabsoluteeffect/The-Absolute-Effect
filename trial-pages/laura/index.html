LAURA

Consolidation after CRT · unresectable stage III EGFR exon19del/L858R

Registry: NCT03521154 · Evidence reviewed 9 November 2026 · Aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase III double-blind placebo-controlled; n=216 (143:73).Primary-source evidence summary

Study design, population and eligibility

Design and sample

Phase III double-blind placebo-controlled; n=216 (143:73).

Population

Unresectable stage III EGFR exon19del/L858R NSCLC without progression after definitive platinum CRT; randomize within 6 weeks.

Treatment and comparator

Intervention: Osimertinib 80 mg daily.

Comparator: Placebo.

Endpoints

PFS by BICR primary; OS immature at primary report.

Key efficacy and clinical outcomes

Median PFS 39.1 vs 5.6 mo (HR 0.16); 12-mo PFS 74% vs 22%. Interim OS HR 0.81 (95% CI 0.42–1.56), immature.
