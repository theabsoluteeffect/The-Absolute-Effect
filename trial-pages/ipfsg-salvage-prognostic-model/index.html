Reported outcomes

The IPFSG model was developed from a large international retrospective cohort. In the full eligible cohort, 3-year PFS was 38% and overall survival was 51%; prognostic factors support risk-adapted salvage discussions.

Interpretation and limitations

This is a prognostic model, not a randomized comparison of TIP, VIP/VeIP and HDCT. It includes seminoma and NSGCT and should not be used to quote seminoma-specific treatment efficacy.

Guideline context: ESMO–EURACAN recommends standard-dose VIP/PEI, TIP or VeIP as conventional first salvage options after relapse following cisplatin-based chemotherapy. NCCN Testicular Cancer Guidelines Insights (Version 2.2025) is a contemporary US guideline source. The study summarized here is not necessarily seminoma-specific; histology scope is stated explicitly below.

Guidelines: ESMO–EURACAN 2022 · NCCN Insights v2.2025
