Design, population and treatment

Design: Global, open-label, randomized phase III trial.

Eligibility and population: 502 patients with recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer after one or two prior systemic regimens; randomized to tisotumab vedotin or investigator-choice chemotherapy.

Intervention: Tisotumab vedotin 2.0 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks, with protocol-directed ocular prophylaxis and monitoring.

Comparator: Investigator's choice of topotecan, vinorelbine, gemcitabine, irinotecan or pemetrexed.

Randomization and endpoint: Overall survival. The full randomization stratification variables and protocol amendments remain subject to protocol-level reconciliation before release.

Key efficacy outcome

Median OS 11.5 versus 9.5 months (HR 0.70, 95% CI 0.54–0.89; p=0.004). Median PFS 4.2 versus 2.9 months (HR 0.67, 95% CI 0.54–0.82; p<0.001). ORR 17.8% versus 5.2%.

Data maturity: Primary phase III analysis; median follow-up approximately 10.8 months at the primary analysis. Primary versus secondary endpoints are kept distinct; no survival estimate is inferred from a hazard ratio or median alone.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in approximately 52.0% versus 62.3%. Important ADC-specific risks include ocular toxicity, peripheral neuropathy and bleeding; eye-care prophylaxis/monitoring is integral. Full event-specific and discontinuation tables remain open.

Delivery and discontinuation: Every-3-week ADC infusion with required eye-care measures and monitoring; investigator-choice chemotherapy had regimen-specific schedules.

Absolute effect and NNT / NNH

No NNT/NNH is calculated. The primary endpoint is reported as a median survival/PFS estimate or a non-inferiority comparison without a valid same-horizon absolute event difference; NNT/NNH is not derived from median survival or a hazard ratio.

Only same-endpoint, same-time-horizon absolute differences are eligible for NNT/NNH. Median survival and hazard ratios alone are not used. No NNT/NNH appropriate from the reported primary endpoint.

Interpretation and limitations

Tisotumab vedotin improved OS, PFS and response over investigator-choice chemotherapy in previously treated recurrent/metastatic cervical cancer. Ocular, neurologic and bleeding toxicities and access to prophylactic eye care affect real-world delivery.

Limitations: Open-label design and heterogeneous chemotherapy comparator; follow-up was relatively short at the primary analysis. The NCCN Insights citation is the 2023 conference abstract (reference 63), not the later NEJM primary paper. No NNT from median survival/HR.
