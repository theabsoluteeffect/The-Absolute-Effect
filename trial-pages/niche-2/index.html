Colon cancer · Medical and surgical oncology · Phase II

NICHE-2

Neoadjuvant nivolumab plus ipilimumab before surgery for locally advanced mismatch-repair-deficient colon cancer. Primary publication: 2024. Evidence review: 24 November 2026.

DesignProspective, multicentre, open-label, single-arm phase II study.
Population115 patients with previously untreated, nonmetastatic dMMR colon cancer; 113 underwent timely surgery and 111 were included in the pathological-response analysis.
Primary endpointsSafety/timely surgery and 3-year disease-free survival.
Data maturityThe 2024 primary report had median follow-up of 26 months. The 2026 long-term follow-up reported median postoperative follow-up of 58.7 months and 3-year disease-free survival of 100% (95% CI 100–100) among 111 per-protocol patients; exploratory 5-year overall survival was 100% (95% CI 100–100) at median follow-up 60.4 months from enrolment. These are single-arm estimates, not comparative treatment effects.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility: Adults with previously untreated, nonmetastatic, locally advanced dMMR colon adenocarcinoma, predominantly stage III disease, including node-positive and bulky T4 tumours. The study did not enrol a randomized comparator cohort.

Intervention: One dose of ipilimumab 1 mg/kg and two doses of nivolumab 3 mg/kg over approximately 4 weeks (nivolumab on days 1 and 15), followed by planned oncologic resection within 6 weeks of starting treatment.

Comparator: None; this was a single-arm phase II study. No randomization or stratification was performed.

Primary endpoints: Safety, including timely surgery, and 3-year DFS. Pathological response was a key secondary endpoint. Timely surgery was defined relative to the prespecified surgical schedule.

Timely surgery: 113/115 patients (98%; 97.5% CI 93–100) underwent surgery within the prespecified window. Two patients had surgery delayed by more than 2 weeks.

Pathological response: 109/111 patients (98%) had a pathological response, 105/111 (95%) had a major pathological response, and 75/111 (68%) achieved a pathological complete response.

DFS: At median follow-up 26 months, no recurrence had been observed in the evaluable cohort. The prespecified 3-year DFS estimate was not mature in the primary report.

NNT/NNH: Not applicable. NICHE-2 was single-arm and provides no concurrent comparator risk from which to calculate an NNT or NNH.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3–4 immune-related adverse events occurred in 5/115 patients (4%). The short neoadjuvant course was designed to preserve timely surgery; the primary report described high surgical completion, but the study was not powered to compare perioperative safety against standard care.

Validated quality-of-life outcomes were not reported in the primary publication. No randomized estimate of adjuvant chemotherapy omission or surgery omission can be inferred from the pathological-response rate.

Interpretation and limitations

NICHE-2 demonstrates very high pathological response rates after a brief neoadjuvant checkpoint-inhibitor course in dMMR colon cancer, with most patients proceeding to timely surgery. The study is single-arm and the primary report had a median follow-up shorter than the prespecified 3-year DFS horizon. It does not establish that surgery can be omitted, nor does it prove that adjuvant chemotherapy can be safely omitted in every patient with a pathological response.

Current-practice context · reviewed 27 November 2026

Neoadjuvant nivolumab plus ipilimumab produces deep responses in locally advanced dMMR colon cancer. The 2026 long-term report adds 3-year DFS and exploratory 5-year OS estimates, but this remains a single-arm strategy; it does not establish omission of surgery or routine non-operative management.
