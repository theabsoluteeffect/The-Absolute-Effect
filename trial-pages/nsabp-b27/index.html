The Absolute Effect / Evidence

NSABP B-27

Breast Cancer · Neoadjuvant Therapy

Clinical question

Does adding docetaxel to neoadjuvant doxorubicin and cyclophosphamide improve response and disease outcomes in operable breast cancer?

Trial context

NSABP B-27 evaluated sequential docetaxel added to neoadjuvant doxorubicin plus cyclophosphamide. The trial established a substantial increase in pathologic complete response, while long-term disease outcomes require interpretation alongside tumour subtype and subsequent therapy.

Absolute Effect perspective

The key contribution of B-27 is the improvement in pathological response with intensified neoadjuvant therapy. Pathologic response is an intermediate endpoint and should not automatically be equated with an overall-survival benefit.
