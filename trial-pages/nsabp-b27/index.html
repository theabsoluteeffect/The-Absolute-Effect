The Absolute Effect

NSABP B-27

Breast Cancer · Neoadjuvant Therapy

Clinical question

Does adding docetaxel to neoadjuvant doxorubicin and cyclophosphamide improve response and disease outcomes in operable breast cancer?

Why the trial matters

NSABP B-27 established that adding docetaxel to doxorubicin and cyclophosphamide substantially increased clinical and pathologic response rates. The improvement in pathologic complete response did not translate into a uniform overall survival advantage across the entire trial population.

Absolute Effect perspective

Pathologic response is an important treatment-response endpoint, but it is not interchangeable with event-free or overall survival. The clinical value of B-27 is strongest when interpreted alongside tumour biology and the later evidence linking response to prognosis.
