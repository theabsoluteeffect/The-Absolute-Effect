EORTC 30986

Gemcitabine-cisplatin versus M-CAVI

Clinical question

For advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma, does gemcitabine-cisplatin provide similar efficacy with less severe toxicity than methotrexate-carboplatin-vinblastine?

Trial at a glance

Design
Randomized phase III
Population
Advanced urothelial carcinoma
Intervention
Gemcitabine + cisplatin
Comparator
M-CAVI
Key endpoints
Response, overall survival, acute toxicity

Key results

OutcomeGemcitabine-cisplatinM-CAVI
Objective response41.2%30.3%
Median overall survival9.3 months8.1 months
Severe acute toxicity≈9%≈21%
Absolute Effect perspective
Similar survival with substantially better tolerability

The principal clinical signal is the combination of similar overall survival with better tolerability. The result is most useful when the endpoint, population and treatment burden are considered together rather than focusing on response rate alone.

Clinical takeaway

Gemcitabine-cisplatin became an important cisplatin-based option for advanced urothelial carcinoma because it offered effective disease control with a more favourable acute toxicity profile than M-CAVI.
