The Absolute Effect / Evidence

EORTC 30986

Bladder Cancer · Advanced/Metastatic

Clinical question

For advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma, does gemcitabine-cisplatin provide similar efficacy with less severe toxicity than methotrexate-carboplatin-vinblastine?

Trial context

EORTC 30986 compared commonly used platinum-based chemotherapy regimens in advanced urothelial carcinoma, addressing both disease control and treatment tolerability.

Absolute Effect perspective

The clinically relevant interpretation requires looking at survival and response together with severe toxicity and treatment feasibility rather than relying on response rate or hazard ratios alone.
