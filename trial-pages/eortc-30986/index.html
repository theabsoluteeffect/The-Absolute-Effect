The Absolute Effect

EORTC 30986

Bladder Cancer · Advanced/Metastatic

Clinical question

For advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma, does gemcitabine-cisplatin provide similar efficacy with less severe toxicity than methotrexate-carboplatin-vinblastine?

Key result

Objective response was 41.2% with gemcitabine-cisplatin versus 30.3% with M-CAVI. Median overall survival was 9.3 versus 8.1 months. Severe acute toxicity was substantially lower with gemcitabine-cisplatin, approximately 9% versus 21%.

Absolute Effect perspective

The trial is a useful example of a regimen comparison where the clinically important advantage is treatment tolerability rather than a large demonstrated survival difference.
