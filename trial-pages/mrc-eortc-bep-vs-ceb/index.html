The Absolute Effect

MRC/EORTC BEP vs CEB

Germ Cell Tumours · Good-Risk Metastatic NSGCT

Clinical question

Can four cycles of BEP provide superior disease control and survival compared with four cycles of CEB in good-risk metastatic nonseminomatous germ-cell tumours?

Key result

In the randomized MRC/EORTC study, four cycles of BEP produced superior disease control and survival compared with CEB. One-year failure-free survival was approximately 91% versus 77%, and 3-year survival approximately 97% versus 90%.

Absolute Effect perspective

This is a foundational regimen-selection trial in metastatic germ-cell cancer. The absolute survival difference is clinically meaningful because cure is the objective, but treatment toxicity must be considered when comparing cisplatin-based regimens.
