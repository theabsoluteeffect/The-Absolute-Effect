The Absolute Effect / Evidence

MRC/EORTC BEP vs CEB

Germ Cell Tumours · Good-Risk Metastatic NSGCT

Clinical question

Can four cycles of BEP provide superior disease control and survival compared with four cycles of CEB in good-risk metastatic nonseminomatous germ-cell tumours?

Trial context

The randomized MRC/EORTC study compared bleomycin, etoposide and cisplatin with a carboplatin-containing alternative in good-risk metastatic NSGCT, helping establish the cisplatin-based regimen as the reference approach.

Absolute Effect perspective

The clinically important comparison includes cure probability, relapse risk and treatment-related toxicity. Small numerical differences should be interpreted in the context of the trial design and long-term cure outcomes.
