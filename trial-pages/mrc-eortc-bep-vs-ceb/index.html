Absolute Effect perspective
Clinically meaningful survival advantage for BEP

The absolute survival difference is clinically meaningful because cure is the objective in good-risk metastatic germ-cell cancer. Replacing cisplatin with carboplatin was not a neutral substitution when cure rates were the primary endpoint.

Clinical takeaway

BEP became the benchmark cisplatin-based regimen for good-risk metastatic NSGCT on the basis of superior disease control and survival. Toxicity, particularly bleomycin-related pulmonary risk, remains relevant to individual regimen selection.
