Osteosarcoma · Medical Oncology · Phase III

ARTEMIS-011

Relapsed or refractory osteosarcoma after prior systemic therapy. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignRandomized phase III trial
PopulationApproximately 122 patients with relapsed or refractory osteosarcoma randomized to cabozantinib or gemcitabine/docetaxel.
Primary endpointProgression-free survival.
Data maturityPrimary PFS analysis reported; OS and post-progression therapy require continued follow-up.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: Approximately 122 patients with relapsed or refractory osteosarcoma randomized to cabozantinib or gemcitabine/docetaxel.

Intervention: Cabozantinib 60 mg orally once daily, with protocol dose reductions as needed.

Comparator: Gemcitabine 900 mg/m² on days 1 and 8 plus docetaxel 75 mg/m² on day 8 of each 21-day cycle.

Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival.

Follow-up and maturity: Primary PFS analysis reported; OS and post-progression therapy require continued follow-up.

Registry: NCT06935409. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

Key efficacy outcome

Median progression-free survival

Cabozantinib
5.5 months
Gemcitabine + docetaxel
1.4 months

Median PFS 5.5 versus 1.4 months; HR 0.33, P<0.0001. The result is a substantial time-to-event benefit in a rare, difficult-to-treat population.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 adverse events were common in both groups; cabozantinib-associated hypertension, diarrhea, mucositis and hand-foot toxicity and chemotherapy-related cytopenias require monitoring.

No NNT is calculated because Median PFS and HR are time-to-event measures; no same-horizon absolute PFS rate was used for NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL benefit was reported in the primary abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

ARTEMIS-011 provides randomized phase III evidence of PFS improvement with cabozantinib over gemcitabine/docetaxel in relapsed/refractory osteosarcoma.

Rare-tumour trial with modest sample size; results are specific to relapsed/refractory osteosarcoma and should not be extrapolated to other bone sarcomas.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
