ARTEMIS-011

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 122 patients with relapsed/refractory osteosarcoma were treated; 80 received risvutatug rezetecan and 42 gemcitabine-docetaxel (2:1 randomization). Eligibility included patients aged 12 years or older after prior systemic therapy; median age was approximately 21 years.

Intervention: Risvutatug rezetecan 12 mg/kg intravenously every 3 weeks.

Comparator: Gemcitabine plus docetaxel every 3 weeks per protocol. Exact component doses are not stated in the accessible primary abstract and are not inferred; supplement verification remains open.

Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival by blinded independent review.

Follow-up and maturity: Median follow-up approximately 7.1 months; OS remains immature.

Registry: NCT06935409. Arm denominators and endpoint definitions are preserved as reported; no missing denominator is inferred.

Key efficacy outcome

Median progression-free survival

Median PFS 5.5 versus 1.4 months; HR 0.33 (95% CI 0.20–0.55), P<0.0001. OS remains immature; the accessible primary abstract does not provide a definitive OS estimate.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events occurred in 93% versus 83%; serious treatment-related events 64% versus 29%; dose reductions 71% versus 26%; discontinuations 3.8% versus 2.4%.

No NNT is calculated: the primary result is a median time-to-event or continuous/single-arm outcome without a valid common-horizon absolute risk difference.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive patient-reported QoL result was reported in the accessible abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

ARTEMIS-011 is a positive phase III PFS result for the B7-H3 ADC risvutatug rezetecan—not cabozantinib—in relapsed/refractory osteosarcoma. Do not release until comparator dose and supplement are reconciled.

Rare tumour and modest sample; comparator component doses and full safety supplement remain to be checked. High serious toxicity and dose modification are central to benefit-risk.
