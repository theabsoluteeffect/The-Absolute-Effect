Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 178 patients randomized 1:1: neoadjuvant GOLP n=88 and surgery-first control n=90; resectable high-risk intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma.

Intervention: Gemcitabine-oxaliplatin plus toripalimab IV every 3 weeks for three cycles and oral lenvatinib once daily for 9 weeks, followed by curative resection; all patients received adjuvant capecitabine for eight cycles.

Comparator: Curative resection without neoadjuvant therapy, followed by eight cycles of adjuvant capecitabine.

Primary endpoint: Event-free survival.

Follow-up and maturity: Prespecified interim analysis at median follow-up 16.9 months.

Registry: NCT04669496. The primary analysis population and denominators are stated explicitly.

Median EFS 18.0 months (95% CI 13.8–27.6) versus 8.7 months (7.2–12.4), P<0.001; the accessible primary abstract does not report an EFS HR. 24-month OS 79% (95% CI 70–90) versus 61% (50–75); HR 0.43 (0.23–0.79), P=0.005, which did not cross the prespecified two-sided alpha 0.0019.

Safety and absolute effect

AEs occurred in 97% versus 70%; during neoadjuvant therapy, grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 28% and grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs in 26%. No treatment-related AE led to death.

No NNT is calculated: EFS is reported as medians without fixed-time rates; the 24-month OS difference did not cross the prespecified alpha and is not used for an NNT claim.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No QoL outcome reported.

Interpretation and limitations

Neoadjuvant GOLP significantly prolonged EFS in resectable high-risk iCCA. The OS signal is promising but did not meet the prespecified alpha; do not describe it as a confirmed OS benefit.

Interim analysis, short follow-up and OS result not alpha-confirmatory. The primary abstract does not provide EFS HR; none is inferred.
