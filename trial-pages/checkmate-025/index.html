The Absolute Effect / Renal Cell Carcinoma / Medical

CheckMate 025

Clinical question

In previously treated advanced clear-cell RCC, does nivolumab improve survival compared with everolimus?

Population / design

821 patients with advanced clear-cell RCC previously treated with one or two antiangiogenic regimens; randomized open-label phase III trial.

Treatment

Nivolumab 3 mg/kg IV every 2 weeks versus everolimus 10 mg orally once daily.

Absolute effect

Median overall survival was 25.0 vs 19.6 months; HR for death 0.73 (98.5% CI 0.57–0.93), p=0.002. Investigator-assessed objective response was 25% vs 5%, an absolute increase of 20 percentage points (NNT ≈ 5 for one additional response; response is not a survival endpoint).

Median PFS was 4.6 vs 4.4 months, HR 0.88 (95% CI 0.75–1.03), p=0.11.

Harm / burden

Grade 3–4 treatment-related adverse events occurred in 19% with nivolumab versus 37% with everolimus: absolute reduction 18 points. Treatment-specific immune toxicities require separate clinical consideration.

Interpretation

CheckMate 025 established a survival benefit for PD-1 blockade over everolimus despite little difference in median PFS, illustrating why OS and response should not be inferred from PFS alone.

Primary source

NEJM primary publication.
