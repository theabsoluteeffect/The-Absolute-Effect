Breast Cancer · Medical

evERA Breast Cancer

373 patients with ER+/HER2− advanced disease after CDK4/6 inhibitor and endocrine therapy.

Treatment and comparator

Giredestrant + everolimus vs standard endocrine therapy + everolimus.

Reported outcomes

Median PFS 8.8 vs 5.5 months; HR 0.56 (95% CI 0.44–0.71; P<0.001). ESR1-mutated subgroup: 10.0 vs 5.5 months; HR 0.38.

Harms and treatment burden

Grade 3–4 adverse events 51.1% vs 37.1%; discontinuation 17.0% vs 11.8%; OS immature.

NNT is not derived from median survival or hazard ratios. Use only source-reported fixed-time absolute rates. No unreported QoL values are imputed.

Interpretation and limitations

Distinguish superiority, non-inferiority and a non-significant result. Interpret subgroup analyses in light of prespecification, multiplicity and interaction tests. This page separates historical trial results from current practice; full clinical freshness review and browser-rendered visualization QA remain pending.
