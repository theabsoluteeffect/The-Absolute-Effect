Clinical question: In pembrolizumab in advanced hcc; previously sorafenib-treated cohort and separate untreated cohort, what is the efficacy and safety of the evaluated intervention?

Registry: NCT02702414 · One underlying study; aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Open-label, single-arm phase II study with two cohorts; one underlying trial, not two separate studies. Evidence cut-off: 13 November 2026.

Design, population and eligibility

Design / sample size

Open-label, single-arm phase II study with two cohorts; one underlying trial, not two separate studies.

Eligibility

Cohort 1: advanced HCC after sorafenib progression/intolerance. Cohort 2: systemic-therapy-naive advanced HCC.

Intervention

Pembrolizumab monotherapy.

Pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every 3 weeks for up to 35 cycles or progression/toxicity.

Comparator

As described in the study design; see primary publication.

Primary endpoint: ORR by RECIST 1.1 primary; DoR, PFS, OS and safety secondary.

Key efficacy outcomes

Cohort 1 (n=104): ORR 18.3%, median DoR 21.0 months, PFS 4.9 months, OS 13.2 months at long-term follow-up. Cohort 2 (n=51): ORR approximately 16%, median PFS 4.3 months and OS 16.9 months in the reported analysis.

Source-reported outcome: Cohort 1 (n=104): ORR 18.3%, median DoR 21.0 months, PFS 4.9 months, OS 13.2 months at long-term follow-up. Cohort 2 (n=51): ORR approximately 16%, median PFS 4.3 months and OS 16.9 months in the reported analysis.

Safety and treatment delivery

Cohort 1 grade 3–4 TRAEs 25%; grade 5 1%; immune-mediated hepatitis grade 3 in 3 patients.

Clinical interpretation and limitations

Single-arm activity signal; cannot establish comparative benefit. The two cohorts are parts of one phase II protocol and must not be counted as separate trials.

Interpret the reported hazard ratios in the context of the prespecified endpoint, statistical hierarchy, follow-up maturity, crossover/subsequent therapy and enrolled population. Median survival differences are not risk differences and must not be converted into NNT.

Absolute effect
