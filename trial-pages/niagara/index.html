Bladder Cancer · Medical Oncology

NIAGARA

In cisplatin-eligible muscle-invasive bladder cancer, does perioperative durvalumab added to neoadjuvant gemcitabine-cisplatin improve event-free and overall survival?

Population
1063 randomized patients with operable muscle-invasive bladder cancer.
Intervention / comparator
Four cycles of neoadjuvant durvalumab + gemcitabine/cisplatin, radical cystectomy, then eight cycles of adjuvant durvalumab versus neoadjuvant gemcitabine/cisplatin and cystectomy.
Primary/major efficacy result
24-month EFS: 67.8% vs 59.8%; absolute improvement 8.0 points; NNT 13.
Overall survival
24-month OS: 82.2% vs 75.2%; absolute improvement 7.0 points; NNT 15; HR 0.75 (95% CI 0.59–0.93), P=.01.

Absolute Effect

24-month EFS: 67.8% vs 59.8%; absolute improvement 8.0 points; NNT 13.

Clinical interpretation: Radical cystectomy was performed in 88.0% vs 83.2%. The trial met its EFS and OS objectives.

Toxicity / treatment burden: Grade 3–4 treatment-related adverse events were 40.6% vs 40.9%; treatment-related death 0.6% in each arm. NNH not applicable from these data.

Important limitations

Open-label design and treatment evolution after progression can affect absolute survival comparisons. Absolute effects are reported only where a reliable common timepoint or directly reported rate permits calculation; otherwise NNT/NNH is not forced from medians or hazard ratios.

Primary source

Powles T, et al. N Engl J Med. 2024;391:1773-1786.
