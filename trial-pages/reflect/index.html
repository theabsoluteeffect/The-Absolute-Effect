Phase III randomized non-inferiority; n=954.Primary-source evidence summary

Study design, population and eligibility

Design and sample

Phase III randomized non-inferiority; n=954.

Population

Unresectable HCC, Child-Pugh A, ECOG 0–1; major portal vein invasion/high liver occupation excluded.

Treatment and comparator

Intervention: Lenvatinib weight-based daily dosing.

Comparator: Sorafenib 400 mg twice daily.

Endpoints

OS non-inferiority primary.

Key efficacy and clinical outcomes

Median OS 13.6 vs 12.3 mo (HR 0.92; 95% CI 0.79–1.06): non-inferior, not superior. ORR 18.8% vs 6.5% by RECIST 1.1.

Toxicity and treatment burden

Hypertension, proteinuria, weight loss, hypothyroidism; sorafenib hand-foot skin reaction/diarrhoea.

Absolute effect and NNT/NNH

Non-inferiority only; exclusions limit generalizability. ORR varies by mRECIST vs RECIST.

NNT/NNH is not calculated unless a directly reported, comparable fixed-time risk difference supports it. Median survival and hazard ratios alone do not provide an NNT.

Critical interpretation and limitations

Non-inferiority only; exclusions limit generalizability. ORR varies by mRECIST vs RECIST.
