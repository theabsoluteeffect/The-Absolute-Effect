The Absolute Effect

EVEREST

Renal Cell Carcinoma · Adjuvant Therapy

Clinical question

Does adjuvant everolimus improve recurrence-free survival after nephrectomy for high-risk renal cell carcinoma?

Trial context

EVEREST was a randomized placebo-controlled trial testing one year of adjuvant everolimus after nephrectomy in patients at elevated risk of recurrence.

Key result

The primary recurrence-free survival comparison narrowly missed its prespecified significance threshold in the overall population. Exploratory analyses suggested greater benefit in the highest-risk subgroup, but these findings should not be treated as definitive evidence of benefit.

Absolute Effect perspective

The overall randomized result is more important than an attractive subgroup signal. Any potential recurrence reduction must be weighed against the treatment burden and toxicity of prolonged mTOR inhibition.

Clinical takeaway

EVEREST did not establish adjuvant everolimus as a routine postoperative standard for RCC. The trial illustrates why subgroup findings should not override the prespecified primary analysis.
