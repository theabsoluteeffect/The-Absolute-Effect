The Absolute Effect

EVEREST

Renal Cell Carcinoma · Adjuvant Therapy

Clinical question

Does adjuvant everolimus improve recurrence-free survival after nephrectomy for high-risk renal cell carcinoma?

Key result

EVEREST tested one year of adjuvant everolimus against placebo after nephrectomy. The primary recurrence-free survival comparison narrowly missed its prespecified significance threshold in the overall population, while exploratory analyses suggested greater benefit in the highest-risk subgroup.

Absolute Effect perspective

The overall result should not be converted into a routine adjuvant-treatment recommendation. Subgroup findings are hypothesis-generating and must be interpreted alongside treatment burden and later immunotherapy-era evidence.
