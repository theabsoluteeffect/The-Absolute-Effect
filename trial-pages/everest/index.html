The Absolute Effect / Evidence

EVEREST

Renal Cell Carcinoma · Adjuvant Therapy

Clinical question

Does adjuvant everolimus improve recurrence-free survival after nephrectomy for high-risk renal cell carcinoma?

Trial context

EVEREST evaluated adjuvant everolimus after nephrectomy in patients at intermediate or high risk of recurrent RCC. The study is important for understanding the limits of mTOR inhibition as postoperative recurrence prevention.

Absolute Effect perspective

The benefit-risk assessment requires the absolute recurrence difference to be considered together with treatment-related toxicity and the study's prespecified risk groups.
