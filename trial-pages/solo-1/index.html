SOLO-1 / GOG 3004

After response to platinum chemotherapy, does olaparib maintenance improve outcomes in advanced BRCA-mutated ovarian cancer?

Trial design and population

Population / eligibility
391 patients with advanced BRCA1/2-mutated ovarian/fallopian tube/primary peritoneal cancer responding to platinum.
Treatment vs comparator
Olaparib 300 mg orally twice daily for up to 2 years vs placebo.
Sample size
391 (reported trial population)
Featured endpoint
Five-year progression-free survival · 5 years

Registry: undefined.

Key results

Five-year PFS 48% vs 21% (HR 0.33, 95% CI 0.25–0.43). Seven-year OS 67.0% vs 46.5% (HR 0.55, 95% CI 0.40–0.76); p=0.0004 did not cross prespecified p<0.0001 boundary.
