CheckMate 577

After neoadjuvant chemoradiotherapy and R0 resection with residual pathological disease, does adjuvant nivolumab improve DFS?

Trial design and population

Population / eligibility
794 patients with stage II/III esophageal or GEJ cancer and residual pathological disease after neoadjuvant CRT and R0 resection.
Intervention vs comparator
Nivolumab 240 mg IV every 2 weeks for 16 weeks, then 480 mg every 4 weeks, up to 1 year vs placebo.
Sample size
794
Primary endpoint
Disease-free survival

Key results

Median DFS was 22.4 months with nivolumab vs 11.0 months with placebo (HR 0.69, 96.4% CI 0.56–0.86; P=.0003). Benefit was seen in squamous and adenocarcinoma subgroups. OS benefit was not established in the primary report.

Absolute Effect

Median DFS difference 11.4 months is not an NNT. DFS benefit is demonstrated; do not infer OS benefit from this endpoint.

NNT: Not calculated; median DFS difference is not a valid NNT. Use only at the stated endpoint/time horizon; median survival differences are not converted into NNT.

NNH: Not quantifiable. Grade 3–4 treatment-related AEs occurred in about 13% with nivolumab vs 6% with placebo. Immune-mediated pneumonitis, colitis, hepatitis and endocrinopathies may require prolonged treatment; discontinuation due to treatment-related events was higher with nivolumab.

Time-to-event hazard ratios and median survival are not treated as absolute risk differences. Where no defensible fixed-time NNT/NNH is available, the source-reported endpoint is visualized instead.

Safety and treatment burden

Grade 3–4 treatment-related AEs occurred in about 13% with nivolumab vs 6% with placebo. Immune-mediated pneumonitis, colitis, hepatitis and endocrinopathies may require prolonged treatment; discontinuation due to treatment-related events was higher with nivolumab.

Follow-up, maturity and limitations

Follow-up: Primary analysis median follow-up 24.4 months.

Applies to residual disease after neoadjuvant CRT and R0 resection, not pathological complete response or patients without neoadjuvant CRT. DFS was the demonstrated endpoint in the primary publication.

Clinical interpretation

One year of adjuvant nivolumab approximately doubles median DFS after trimodality treatment when residual esophageal/GEJ cancer remains at surgery.
