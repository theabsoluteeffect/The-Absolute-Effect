CROWN

First-line ALK-positive advanced NSCLC

Registry: NCT03052608 · Evidence reviewed 9 November 2026 · Aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase III randomized open-label; n=296.Primary-source evidence summary

Study design, population and eligibility

Design and sample

Phase III randomized open-label; n=296.

Population

Previously untreated advanced ALK-positive NSCLC.

Treatment and comparator

Intervention: Lorlatinib 100 mg daily.

Comparator: Crizotinib 250 mg twice daily.

Endpoints

PFS by BICR primary; long-term investigator PFS and intracranial outcomes.

Key efficacy and clinical outcomes

At ~5 y: median PFS NR vs 9.1 mo (HR 0.19); 5-y PFS 60% vs 8%; time to intracranial progression HR 0.06.

Toxicity and treatment burden

Hyperlipidaemia, oedema, weight gain, neuropathy and cognitive/mood effects.

Absolute effect and NNT/NNH

Crizotinib is an older comparator; not a head-to-head comparison with other modern ALK inhibitors.

NNT/NNH is not calculated unless a directly reported, comparable fixed-time risk difference supports it. Median survival and hazard ratios alone do not provide an NNT.

Critical interpretation and limitations

Crizotinib is an older comparator; not a head-to-head comparison with other modern ALK inhibitors.

This page is an evidence summary, not a substitute for multidisciplinary assessment or patient-specific decision-making.

Review date: 9 November 2026. Primary publication and selected follow-up sources were reviewed. Full disease-specific guideline, regulatory-label and sequencing audit remains pending; verify current local recommendations and biomarker eligibility before clinical application.
