KEYNOTE-048

In recurrent/metastatic HNSCC, how do pembrolizumab alone or with chemotherapy compare with cetuximab plus platinum/5-FU?

Trial design and population

Population / eligibility
882 patients with untreated recurrent/metastatic HNSCC.
Treatment vs comparator
Pembrolizumab alone or with platinum/5-FU vs cetuximab plus platinum/5-FU.
Sample size
882 (reported trial population)
Featured endpoint
Median OS, CPS ≥20 · primary analysis

Registry: undefined.

Key results

CPS ≥20: median OS 14.9 vs 10.7 months (HR 0.61); CPS ≥1: 12.3 vs 10.4 months (HR 0.74). Pembrolizumab-chemotherapy: total-population OS 13.0 vs 10.7 months (HR 0.77, 95% CI 0.63–0.93; p=0.0034).
