Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 75 patients with HPV16-positive recurrent/metastatic HNSCC were included in the modified intention-to-treat population: 53 were checkpoint-inhibitor naive and 22 had prior ICI resistance.

Intervention: PDS0101 therapeutic HPV16 vaccine administered subcutaneously in five protocol-scheduled doses plus pembrolizumab 200 mg intravenously every 3 weeks.

Comparator: No randomized comparator; outcomes were reported separately by prior checkpoint-inhibitor exposure.

Primary endpoint: Objective response rate by RECIST 1.1.

Follow-up and maturity: Response and survival follow-up reported by ICI-naive and ICI-resistant cohorts; single-arm phase II maturity.

Registry: NCT04260126. Arm denominators and endpoint definitions are retained from the primary report; no missing denominator is inferred.

Overall ORR was 34.0%. ORR was 40% in the ICI-naive cohort and 0% in the ICI-resistant cohort. Median PFS was 5.3 months overall; median OS was 39.3 months in ICI-naive patients and 14.8 months in ICI-resistant patients.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time effects are shown only when the primary source supplies a comparable endpoint and horizon.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3 or higher treatment-related adverse events occurred in approximately 13.8%; immune-mediated pembrolizumab events and vaccine-related local reactions are relevant.

No NNT is calculated: the study uses time-to-event medians, single-arm cohorts or continuous outcomes without a comparable fixed-time absolute risk difference.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No comparative QoL outcome was reported.

Interpretation and limitations

The combination showed activity in ICI-naive HPV16-positive disease but no objective responses in the ICI-resistant cohort. Prior immunotherapy status must be explicit when interpreting the result.

Nonrandomized, small cohorts; prior ICI exposure is a major effect modifier. The apparent long OS in ICI-naive patients cannot be interpreted as a comparative treatment effect.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The source hierarchy and analysis population are explicit.
