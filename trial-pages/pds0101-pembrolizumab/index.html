HPV16-positive recurrent/metastatic HNSCC · Medical Oncology · Phase II

PDS0101 plus pembrolizumab (VERSATILE-002)

Recurrent/metastatic; ICI-naive and ICI-resistant cohorts. Full primary JAMA article and protocol reviewed 17 November 2026.

DesignOpen-label multicentre nonrandomized phase II, Simon two-stage cohorts
Population88 enrolled; 87 safety; 75 mITT
Primary endpointORR by masked central RECIST 1.1
Follow-upLong-term survival follow-up; median follow-up not stated in abstract

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 88 patients enrolled; 87 received treatment and comprised the safety population. The modified intention-to-treat efficacy population included 75 patients: 53 ICI-naive with PD-L1 CPS ≥1 and 22 ICI-resistant after prior ICI.

Intervention: Pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every 3 weeks for up to 35 cycles plus PDS0101 1 mL subcutaneously containing 3.0 mg R-enantiomer cationic lipid and 2.7 mg HPV16 E6/E7 multiepitope peptide mixture, given on cycles 1, 2, 3, 4 and 12.

Comparator: No randomized comparator; prespecified ICI-naive and ICI-resistant cohorts analyzed separately.

Primary endpoint: Objective response rate by RECIST 1.1 assessed by masked independent central reviewer.

Follow-up: Study treatment ran from March 2021 to May 2025; long-term survival follow-up continued. Median follow-up was not stated in the abstract.

Registry: NCT04260126.

In ICI-naive patients (n=53), ORR 34.0%, median PFS 5.3 months (95% CI 2.10–9.00) and median OS 39.3 months (23.90–NE). In ICI-resistant patients (n=22), ORR 0%, median PFS 2.0 months and median OS 14.8 months (8.50–26.00). The primary endpoint was not met in the ICI-resistant cohort.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs occurred in 13.8% of the 87-patient safety population. Any treatment-related AE occurred in 55/62 (88%) ICI-naive and 21/25 (84%) ICI-resistant safety patients. Injection-site pain occurred in 60% and 44%, respectively; fatigue in 40% and 28%.

No NNT is calculated: single-arm cohort outcomes and no randomized comparator do not support a treatment NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No validated comparative QoL endpoint was reported.

Injection-site pain, swelling, discoloration and erythema are characteristic vaccine-related burdens.

Interpretation and limitations

PDS0101 plus pembrolizumab showed activity in ICI-naive HPV16-positive R/M HNSCC but no responses in the ICI-resistant cohort. The exact five-dose vaccine schedule and mITT/safety denominators are now taken from the full JAMA report.

Single-arm phase II with separate Simon two-stage cohorts; ICI-naive efficacy applies to PD-L1 CPS ≥1. No objective responses occurred in ICI-resistant disease. Median OS is not a randomized treatment effect.

Current-practice context · reviewed 17 November 2026

Investigational HPV16-targeted vaccine plus PD-1 blockade; not an established substitute for platinum-based first-line therapy or approved post-ICI treatment. NCCN provenance not verified because the complete guideline/reference list was inaccessible.
