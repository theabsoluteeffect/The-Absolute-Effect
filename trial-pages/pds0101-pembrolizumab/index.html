HPV16-positive head and neck squamous cell carcinoma · Medical Oncology · Phase II

PDS0101 plus pembrolizumab

Recurrent or metastatic HPV16-positive HNSCC after prior systemic therapy. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignOpen-label, single-arm phase II study with separate prior-ICI cohorts
PopulationPatients with recurrent/metastatic HPV16-positive HNSCC, analyzed by prior checkpoint-inhibitor exposure

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: Patients with recurrent/metastatic HPV16-positive HNSCC, analyzed by prior checkpoint-inhibitor exposure; the study included ICI-naive and ICI-resistant cohorts.

Intervention: PDS0101 therapeutic HPV16 vaccine administered subcutaneously on the protocol schedule plus pembrolizumab 200 mg intravenously every 3 weeks.

Comparator: No randomized comparator.

Primary endpoint: Objective response rate by RECIST 1.1.

Follow-up and maturity: Response and survival follow-up reported by prior-ICI cohort; single-arm maturity.

Registry: NCT04260126. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

ORR was approximately 40% in the ICI-naive cohort and 0% in the ICI-resistant cohort; median OS in the ICI-naive cohort was approximately 39.3 months. Cohort-specific denominators should be used when quoting response.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3–4 treatment-related adverse events occurred in approximately 17.7%; vaccine injection reactions and pembrolizumab immune-mediated events are relevant.

No NNT is calculated because this is a single-arm or non-comparative study without a randomized comparator.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No comparative QoL outcome is available.

Interpretation and limitations

The combination shows activity in ICI-naive HPV16-positive disease but not in the ICI-resistant cohort. Biomarker and prior-treatment context are essential.

Small, non-randomized cohorts; prior checkpoint exposure strongly modifies observed activity. Results do not establish benefit after ICI resistance.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
