Single-agent gemcitabine in refractory GCT

Does single-agent gemcitabine have activity in heavily pretreated refractory germ-cell tumours?

Phase II mixed-GCT study

Population
20 assessable

Not all evidence is randomized, comparative, or seminoma-specific.

Reported outcomes

In a phase II study of heavily pretreated GCT, 3 of 20 assessable patients (15%) had an objective response, including one complete response. Thirteen patients were platinum-refractory.

Interpretation and limitations

Later-line, single-arm evidence. The small sample and refractory population mean these results should not be compared directly with first-salvage TIP/VIP/VeIP. Not seminoma-specific.

Guideline context: ESMO–EURACAN recommends standard-dose VIP/PEI, TIP or VeIP as conventional first salvage options after relapse following cisplatin-based chemotherapy. NCCN Testicular Cancer Guidelines Insights (Version 2.2025) is a contemporary US guideline source. The study summarized here is not necessarily seminoma-specific; histology scope is stated explicitly below.

Guidelines: ESMO–EURACAN 2022 · NCCN Insights v2.2025
