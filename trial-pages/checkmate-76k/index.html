CheckMate 76K

Clinical question: In completely resected stage IIB/C melanoma, does one year of adjuvant nivolumab improve recurrence-free survival compared with placebo?

Phase III, double-blind, randomized 2:1 trial · N=790 · NCT04099251 · Primary publication: PMID 37845511.

Design and treatment

Population

790 patients with completely resected stage IIB/C melanoma.

Randomization

Nivolumab n=526; placebo n=264.

Intervention

Nivolumab 480 mg IV every 4 weeks for 12 months.

Primary endpoint

Investigator-assessed recurrence-free survival (RFS). Key secondary endpoint: distant metastasis-free survival (DMFS).

At the primary publication, minimum follow-up was 7.8 months. The trial was not powered to establish an overall-survival benefit at this analysis.

Efficacy

12-month RFS was 89.0% with nivolumab and 79.4% with placebo (HR 0.42; 95% CI 0.30–0.59; P<0.0001). DMFS also improved (HR 0.47; 95% CI 0.30–0.72).

Interpretation and limitations

Adjuvant nivolumab reduced early recurrence/death and distant recurrence/death in resected stage IIB/C melanoma. The initial report had short minimum follow-up and did not establish an OS benefit. Immune-mediated toxicity, treatment discontinuation and a rare treatment-related death must be balanced against recurrence risk and patient preferences. The trial does not compare nivolumab directly with pembrolizumab.
