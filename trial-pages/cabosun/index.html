CABOSUN

In previously untreated intermediate- or poor-risk metastatic RCC, does cabozantinib improve disease control compared with sunitinib?

Population
157 patients with untreated clear-cell metastatic RCC and intermediate/poor IMDC risk.
Intervention / comparator
Cabozantinib 60 mg daily versus sunitinib 50 mg daily, 4 weeks on/2 weeks off.
Primary/major efficacy result
Independent-review PFS: 8.6 vs 5.3 months; HR 0.48 (95% CI 0.31–0.74), P=.0008.
Overall survival
Median OS: 26.6 vs 21.2 months; HR 0.80 (95% CI 0.53–1.21). ORR 20% vs 9%.

Absolute Effect

Independent-review PFS: 8.6 vs 5.3 months; HR 0.48 (95% CI 0.31–0.74), P=.0008.

Clinical interpretation: The trial was a randomized phase II study with a small sample and PFS as the primary endpoint. OS was not statistically conclusive.

Toxicity / treatment burden: Grade 3–4 adverse events occurred in 68% vs 65%. NNH not quantifiable from a single clinically central fixed-time toxicity endpoint.

Important limitations

Open-label design and treatment evolution after progression can affect absolute survival comparisons. Absolute effects are reported only where a reliable common timepoint or directly reported rate permits calculation; otherwise NNT/NNH is not forced from medians or hazard ratios.

Primary source

Choueiri TK, et al. J Clin Oncol. 2017;35:591-597; Eur J Cancer. 2018;94:115-125.
