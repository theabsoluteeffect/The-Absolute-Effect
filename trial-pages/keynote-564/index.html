The Absolute Effect / Evidence / KEYNOTE-564
Renal Cell Carcinoma · Medical Oncology · Adjuvant

KEYNOTE-564

Clinical question

Does adjuvant pembrolizumab improve disease-free and overall survival after nephrectomy for high-risk clear-cell RCC?

Trial context
Population
994 patients with high-risk clear-cell RCC after nephrectomy, with or without completely resected metastases
Intervention
Adjuvant pembrolizumab vs placebo after nephrectomy
Key endpoint
24-month disease-free survival
Time horizon / follow-up
24 months
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

At 24 months, disease-free survival was 77.3% with pembrolizumab versus 68.1% with placebo, an absolute benefit of 9.2 percentage points (NNT 11). Overall survival at 24 months was 96.6% vs 93.5%. Grade 3+ adverse events were 32.4% vs 17.7%.

NNT: 11

Treatment burden / harm

Grade 3+ adverse events

NNH: 7

Relative effect

HR: 0.68

95% CI 0.53–0.87; P=.002

Clinical interpretation

Interpret the absolute difference together with the endpoint, time horizon, relative effect and limitations of the study design.
