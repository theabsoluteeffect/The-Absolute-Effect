The Absolute Effect / Evidence / KEYNOTE-564
Renal Cell Carcinoma · Medical Oncology · Adjuvant

KEYNOTE-564

Clinical question

Does adjuvant pembrolizumab improve disease-free and overall survival after nephrectomy for high-risk clear-cell RCC?

Trial context
Population
994 patients with high-risk clear-cell RCC after nephrectomy, with or without completely resected metastases
Intervention
Adjuvant pembrolizumab vs placebo after nephrectomy
Key endpoint
24-month disease-free survival
Time horizon / follow-up
24 months
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

At 24 months, DFS was 77.3% with pembrolizumab and 68.1% with placebo: absolute benefit 9.2 points (NNT11). At 5 years, DFS was 60.9% and 52.2% (8.7 points; approximate NNT12); OS was 87.7% and 82.3% (5.4 points; approximate NNT19). NNTs use same-horizon Kaplan–Meier estimates, not hazard ratios.
Treatment burden / harm
Grade ≥3 adverse events of any cause: 32.4% with pembrolizumab vs 17.7% with placebo in the original analysis. The 14.7-point excess is displayed descriptively; no NNH is calculated because this cumulative safety rate is not reported at a fixed-time horizon. The five-year report described safety as largely unchanged.
Relative effect
Original analysis: 24-month DFS HR 0.68 (95% CI 0.53–0.87; P=.002). Five-year analysis: DFS HR 0.71 (95% CI 0.59–0.86); OS HR 0.66 (95% CI 0.48–0.90). Fourth prespecified interim analysis; median follow-up 70 months.
Clinical interpretation
Adjuvant pembrolizumab improves DFS and OS in appropriately selected high-risk clear-cell RCC after nephrectomy (± metastasectomy). The five-year peer-reviewed update confirms durable benefit. Selection should follow trial recurrence-risk criteria and include discussion of immune-related toxicity, competing morbidity, overtreatment and patient preferences.

Current-practice context · reviewed 23 November 2026

The five-year peer-reviewed update confirms sustained DFS and OS benefit for adjuvant pembrolizumab in trial-eligible high-risk ccRCC. The official Merck ESMO 2026 programme announcement lists the final protocol-prespecified analysis (3589RO), but the full abstract text was not retrieved; seven-year numerical outcomes are therefore not added. Current selection should follow recurrence-risk criteria and a shared discussion of immune toxicity and competing risks.
