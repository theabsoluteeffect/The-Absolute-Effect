PEACE-1

In de novo metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer, does adding abiraterone/prednisone to ADT plus docetaxel improve survival?

Population
1172 randomized men with de novo metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer in a 2×2 factorial design.
Intervention / comparator
ADT ± docetaxel, with or without abiraterone 1000 mg daily + prednisone 5 mg twice daily.
Primary/major efficacy result
OS HR 0.82 (95.1% CI 0.69–0.98) in the overall population; in the ADT+docetaxel population HR 0.75 (95.1% CI 0.59–0.95).
Overall survival
rPFS HR 0.54 (99.9% CI 0.41–0.71) overall; HR 0.50 (99.9% CI 0.34–0.71) with ADT+docetaxel.

Absolute Effect

OS HR 0.82 (95.1% CI 0.69–0.98) in the overall population; in the ADT+docetaxel population HR 0.75 (95.1% CI 0.59–0.95).

Clinical interpretation: The factorial design permits assessment of abiraterone and prostate radiotherapy. In the docetaxel population, triplet therapy produced the clearest survival signal.

Toxicity / treatment burden: Grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in 63% with abiraterone versus 52% without in the ADT+docetaxel population, with hypertension the largest excess. NNH not calculated because no single fixed-time harm endpoint is sufficiently central.

Important limitations

Open-label design and treatment evolution after progression can affect absolute survival comparisons. Absolute effects are reported only where a reliable common timepoint or directly reported rate permits calculation; otherwise NNT/NNH is not forced from medians or hazard ratios.

Primary source

Fizazi K, et al. Lancet. 2022;399:1695-1707.
