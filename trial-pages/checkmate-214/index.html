CheckMate 214

In previously untreated advanced RCC, does nivolumab plus ipilimumab improve survival compared with sunitinib?

Population1,096 patients with previously untreated advanced RCC; 847 had IMDC intermediate/poor-risk disease.InterventionNivolumab + ipilimumab induction followed by nivolumab.ComparatorSunitinib.Primary populationIMDC intermediate/poor-risk disease.

Absolute Effect

18-month OS
75% vs 60% (+15 points)

Intermediate/poor-risk patients; HR for death 0.63 in the primary analysis.

42-month OS
52% vs 39% (+13 points)

Long-term follow-up showed durable survival separation.

Response and toxicity

At 42 months, ORR in intermediate/poor-risk disease was 42.1% versus 26.3%. Grade 3–4 treatment-related events occurred in 46% versus 63%; treatment-related adverse events leading to discontinuation occurred in 22% versus 12%.

Clinical interpretation

The major absolute benefit is long-term survival and durable response in IMDC intermediate/poor-risk disease.

Favorable-risk patients were exploratory and did not show the same efficacy pattern; they should not be conflated with the primary population.

NNT / NNH

Using the 18-month OS difference, NNT ≈ 7. For grade 3–4 treatment-related adverse events, the absolute reduction was 17 points, corresponding to an approximate NNT of 6 to avoid one such event versus sunitinib; toxicity profiles are qualitatively different, so this is not a complete harm comparison.

Treatment-free time

At 42 months, 18% of intermediate/poor-risk patients receiving nivolumab/ipilimumab were alive and treatment-free versus 5% with sunitinib; mean treatment-free survival was 6.9 versus 3.1 months.
