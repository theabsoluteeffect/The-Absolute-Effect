INTerpath-001 (V940 / mRNA-4157)

After resection of high-risk melanoma, does personalised neoantigen mRNA therapy added to pembrolizumab improve RFS?

One underlying study; aliases and follow-up reports consolidated. Primary discipline: Medical.

Population and eligibility

1,137 patients with completely resected high-risk stage IIB–IV cutaneous melanoma, randomised 2:1.

Completely resected stage IIB–IV melanoma; the vaccine remains investigational at this evidence cut-off.

Sample size

1,137 randomised/evaluable as reported.

Intervention

Individualised intismeran autogene 1 mg every 3 weeks for up to nine doses plus pembrolizumab 400 mg every 6 weeks for up to nine cycles.

Comparator

Placebo plus pembrolizumab on the same schedule.

Endpoints and results

Primary investigator-assessed RFS; key secondary DMFS, OS, safety, tolerability and QoL.

  • Median follow-up 24.6 months; RFS HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.45–0.75; p<0.001).
  • 2-year RFS 82.2% vs 70.0%; 2-year DMFS 88.5% vs 80.2% (HR 0.62, 95% CI 0.45–0.85; p=0.002).
  • Grade 3–5 treatment-related AEs 14.2% vs 13.4%; OS immature.
  • First 6 months showed comparable outcomes; prespecified post-six-month sensitivity analysis showed a larger effect.

Interpretation: RFS difference 12.2 points; approximate landmark NNT 9 at 2 years. Vaccine remains investigational and OS is immature.

Toxicity, discontinuation and quality of life

Trial-specific harms: Grade 3–5 treatment-related AEs 14.2% vs 13.4%; pyrexia 52.2%, injection-site pain 51.0%, fatigue 38.5% with combination.

Treatment burden/discontinuation: Individualised manufacture and up to nine additional injections add logistics and treatment burden.

Quality of life: QoL is a key secondary endpoint; no definitive numerical QoL advantage asserted here.

Subgroups, maturity and limitations

Subgroups: Effects generally consistent across prespecified subgroups; post-six-month effect is sensitivity analysis, not a replacement for ITT primary analysis.

Maturity: ESMO 2026 detailed results announced; OS immature and full peer-reviewed paper not yet independently verified in accessible full text.

Limitations: Regulatory approval not established; distinguish sponsor/meeting results from completed peer-reviewed evidence.

Clinical interpretation

Positive phase III RFS/DMFS findings for a personalised vaccine plus pembrolizumab, but the treatment remains investigational and OS is immature.

Current-practice context must be checked against regional guidelines, patient fitness, biomarkers, alternatives and regulatory status before clinical application. Clinical freshness review and browser-rendered QA remain pending.
