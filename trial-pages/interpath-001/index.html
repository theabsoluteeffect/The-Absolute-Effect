INTerpath-001 (V940 / mRNA-4157)

After resection of high-risk melanoma, does personalised neoantigen mRNA therapy added to pembrolizumab improve RFS?

One underlying study; aliases and follow-up reports consolidated. Primary discipline: Medical.

Population and eligibility

1,137 patients with completely resected high-risk stage IIB–IV cutaneous melanoma, randomised 2:1.

Completely resected stage IIB–IV melanoma; the vaccine remains investigational at this evidence cut-off.

Sample size

1,137 randomised/evaluable as reported.

Intervention

Individualised intismeran autogene 1 mg every 3 weeks for up to nine doses plus pembrolizumab 400 mg every 6 weeks for up to nine cycles.

Comparator

Placebo plus pembrolizumab on the same schedule.

Endpoints and results

Primary investigator-determined recurrence-free survival (RFS); key secondary distant-metastasis-free survival (DMFS), OS, safety, tolerability and QoL.

  • Median follow-up 24.6 months. ESMO 2026 LBA1 reported overall RFS HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.45–0.75; P<0.001). The NEJM primary paper reports piecewise RFS HR 0.99 (95% CI 0.66–1.49) during months 0–6 and 0.42 (0.30–0.58) after month 6, reflecting delayed separation of the curves.
  • 24-month RFS 82.2% vs 70.0% (absolute difference 12.2 percentage points). 24-month DMFS 88.5% vs 80.2% (absolute difference 8.3 points; ESMO overall DMFS HR 0.62, 95% CI 0.45–0.85; P=0.002). NEJM reports piecewise DMFS HR 1.01 (0.58–1.79) during months 0–6 and 0.50 (0.34–0.73) after month 6.
  • OS remains immature; ESMO 2026 reported grade 3–5 treatment-related AEs 14.2% vs 13.4%. The NEJM primary report separately reports all-cause grade ≥3 AEs in 23.6% vs 20.3%. Eight patients (1.1%) in the intismeran group died from an adverse event; five deaths occurred before intismeran was administered.
  • The first 6 months showed no clear separation; the prespecified post-six-month analysis showed a stronger effect. Overall survival is immature.

Toxicity, discontinuation and quality of life

Trial-specific harms: NEJM: all-cause grade ≥3 AEs 23.6% vs 20.3%. ESMO LBA1: grade 3–5 treatment-related AEs 14.2% vs 13.4%. Eight adverse-event deaths (1.1%) occurred in the combination arm, five before intismeran administration. Pyrexia 52.2%, injection-site pain 51.0% and fatigue 38.5% were common with combination.

Treatment burden/discontinuation: Individualised manufacture and up to nine additional injections add logistics and treatment burden.

Quality of life: QoL is a key secondary endpoint; no definitive numerical QoL advantage asserted here.

Subgroups, maturity and limitations

Subgroups: Effects generally consistent across prespecified subgroups; post-six-month effect is sensitivity analysis, not a replacement for ITT primary analysis.

Maturity: ESMO 2026 detailed results announced; OS immature and full peer-reviewed paper not yet independently verified in accessible full text.

Limitations: Regulatory approval not established; distinguish sponsor/meeting results from completed peer-reviewed evidence.

Clinical interpretation

Positive phase III RFS/DMFS findings for a personalised vaccine plus pembrolizumab, but the treatment remains investigational and OS is immature.

Current-practice context must be checked against regional guidelines, patient fitness, biomarkers, alternatives and regulatory status before clinical application. Clinical guideline-freshness review and browser-rendered QA remain pending.
