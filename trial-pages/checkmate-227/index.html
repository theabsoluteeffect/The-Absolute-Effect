In first-line metastatic NSCLC without sensitizing EGFR/ALK alterations, does nivolumab plus ipilimumab improve long-term survival over platinum chemotherapy across PD-L1 strata?

Registry: NCT02477826 · Evidence reviewed 9 November 2026 · Aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase III multi-part randomized trial; Part 1 n=1,739.Primary-source evidence summary

Study design, population and eligibility

Design and sample

Phase III multi-part randomized trial; Part 1 n=1,739.

Population

Stage IV/recurrent NSCLC without sensitizing EGFR/ALK; PD-L1 strata included.

Treatment and comparator

Intervention: Nivolumab + ipilimumab.

Comparator: Platinum-doublet chemotherapy; additional nivolumab-containing arms were studied.

Endpoints

OS in PD-L1 ≥1% primary comparison; long-term OS reported across strata.

Key efficacy and clinical outcomes

At ≥61.3-mo follow-up 5-y OS nivo/ipi vs chemo: 24% vs 14% for PD-L1≥1%; 19% vs 7% for PD-L1<1%.

Toxicity and treatment burden

Dual checkpoint blockade causes immune-mediated colitis, hepatitis, pneumonitis and endocrinopathy.
