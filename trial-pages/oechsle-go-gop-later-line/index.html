GO/GOP and secondary surgery in multiply relapsed GCT

Can gemcitabine/oxaliplatin, with or without paclitaxel and secondary surgery, produce long-term disease-free survival after multiple relapses?

Two phase II cohorts with long-term follow-up; mixed GCT

Population
76 total

Study population as reported in the cited publication; cohort sizes and denominators are stated where available.

Evidence type
Mixed GCT · phase II follow-up

Not all evidence is randomized, comparative, or seminoma-specific.

Reported outcomes

Long-term follow-up of GO (35 patients) and GOP (41 patients) cohorts found 8/76 patients (11%) disease-free for more than 2 years: 1/35 after GO and 7/41 after GOP. The authors emphasized secondary surgery after partial response.

Interpretation and limitations

Separate phase II cohorts, not a randomized GO-versus-GOP comparison. These regimens are later-line options for highly selected patients; mixed histology and no seminoma-specific efficacy claim.

Guideline context: ESMO–EURACAN recommends standard-dose VIP/PEI, TIP or VeIP as conventional first salvage options after relapse following cisplatin-based chemotherapy. NCCN Testicular Cancer Guidelines Insights (Version 2.2025) is a contemporary US guideline source. The study summarized here is not necessarily seminoma-specific; histology scope is stated explicitly below.
