Design, population and treatment

Design: Phase III randomised trial; 443 participants.

Population: First-line PD-L1-positive unresectable locally advanced or metastatic triple-negative breast cancer.

Experimental arm: Sacituzumab govitecan plus pembrolizumab.

Comparator: Chemotherapy plus pembrolizumab.

Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival assessed by blinded independent central review (BICR).

Registry: NCT05382286.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 adverse events were reported in approximately 71% versus 70%; adverse-event-related discontinuation was 12% versus 31% (experimental versus comparator arm, respectively).

Absolute-effect interpretation: A valid NNT cannot be calculated from median PFS values or the hazard ratio alone. Fixed-time PFS rates are not supplied in the data used for this page. No NNT/NNH pictogram is therefore shown.

Interpretation and limitations

The trial reported a statistically significant improvement in BICR-assessed median PFS with sacituzumab govitecan plus pembrolizumab. The absolute difference between medians was 3.4 months.

Overall survival was immature at the reported analysis. ORR was 60% versus 53%. Median PFS should not be interpreted as a fixed-time absolute benefit, and the HR should not be converted into an NNT.
