CheckMate 9LA

First-line metastatic/recurrent NSCLC without sensitizing EGFR/ALK

Registry: NCT03215706 · Evidence reviewed 9 November 2026 · Aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase III randomized open-label; n=719.Primary-source evidence summary

Study design, population and eligibility

Design and sample

Phase III randomized open-label; n=719.

Population

Treatment-naive stage IV/recurrent NSCLC without sensitizing EGFR/ALK alteration.

Treatment and comparator

Intervention: Nivolumab + ipilimumab + two cycles platinum-doublet chemotherapy.

Comparator: Four cycles platinum-doublet chemotherapy.

Endpoints

OS primary.

Key efficacy and clinical outcomes

At 6-y follow-up OS HR 0.74; 6-y OS 16% vs 10%; benefit across PD-L1 and histology.
