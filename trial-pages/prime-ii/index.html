Breast Cancer · Radiation Oncology · RT Omission

PRIME II

Clinical question

In older women with low-risk HR-positive node-negative breast cancer, what is the absolute benefit of adjuvant breast RT?

Trial context
Population
1,326 women ≥65 years with low-risk HR-positive node-negative breast cancer
Intervention
Whole-breast RT vs no RT after BCS + endocrine therapy
Key endpoint
10-year local recurrence
Time horizon / follow-up
10 years
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

10-year local recurrence was 0.9% with RT versus 9.5% without RT, an absolute reduction of 8.6 percentage points (NNT 12). Overall survival was essentially identical, 80.7% vs 80.8%. NNT is based on 10-year local recurrence.

NNT: 12

Treatment burden / harm

Additional RT toxicity

NNH: Not quantifiable from the trial's main reported toxicity data

Relative effect

HR: 10.4 for no RT vs RT

95% CI 4.1–26.1

Clinical interpretation

Interpret the absolute difference together with the endpoint, time horizon, relative effect and limitations of the study design.

Primary publication
  • Kunkler IH, et al. N Engl J Med. 2023;388:585–594.
