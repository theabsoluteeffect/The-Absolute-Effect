Breast Cancer · Radiation Oncology · RT Omission

PRIME II

Clinical question

In older women with low-risk HR-positive node-negative breast cancer, what is the absolute benefit of adjuvant breast RT?

Trial at a glance

Population: 1,326 women ≥65 years with low-risk HR-positive node-negative breast cancer.

Comparison: Whole-breast RT vs no RT after BCS + endocrine therapy.

Endpoint: 10-year local recurrence.

Absolute Effect

10-year local recurrence was 0.9% with RT vs 9.5% without RT: absolute reduction 8.6 percentage points; NNT 12.

Clinical interpretation

RT substantially reduces local recurrence, while overall survival remains essentially unchanged in this carefully selected older low-risk population.
