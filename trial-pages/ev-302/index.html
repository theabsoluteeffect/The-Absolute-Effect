Bladder Cancer · Medical · Locally advanced / metastatic first line

EV-302 / KEYNOTE-A39

Clinical question

In untreated locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma, does enfortumab vedotin plus pembrolizumab improve outcomes over platinum-gemcitabine chemotherapy?

Trial context
Population
886 patients
Intervention
Enfortumab vedotin 1.25 mg/kg IV days 1 and 8 q21d + pembrolizumab 200 mg IV q3w; control gemcitabine + cisplatin or carboplatin.
Comparator
Platinum + gemcitabine
Primary / key evidence
Median OS 31.5 vs 16.1 mo; HR 0.47. Median PFS 12.5 vs 6.3 mo; HR 0.45. ORR 67.7% vs 44.4%.
Absolute Effect

What changes for patients?

Median OS 31.5 vs 16.1 mo; HR 0.47. Median PFS 12.5 vs 6.3 mo; HR 0.45. ORR 67.7% vs 44.4%.

NNT/NNH: Not calculated unless a defensible fixed-time event-rate comparison was available in the cited primary analysis; median-survival differences alone are not converted to NNT.

Clinical interpretation

The combination produced large improvements in both PFS and OS versus platinum-gemcitabine in previously untreated advanced urothelial cancer.

Limitations / maturity

Interpret the effect in the context of the trial population, endpoint definition, follow-up and treatment era. Subsequent trials and evolving standards may limit direct applicability to contemporary patients.

Primary publication

Powles T et al. N Engl J Med. 2024;390:875–888.
