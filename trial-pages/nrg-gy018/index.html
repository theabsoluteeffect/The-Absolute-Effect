Design, population and treatment

Design: Double-blind, placebo-controlled, 1:1 randomized phase III trial with independent dMMR and pMMR cohorts.

Eligibility and population: 816 patients with measurable stage III/IVA, stage IVB or recurrent endometrial cancer. Primary efficacy cohorts included 215 dMMR patients (109 pembrolizumab, 106 placebo) and 550 pMMR patients (276, 274); prior adjuvant chemotherapy was permitted after a treatment-free interval of at least 12 months.

Intervention: Pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every 3 weeks with paclitaxel 175 mg/m² and carboplatin AUC 5 for 6 cycles, followed by pembrolizumab 400 mg IV every 6 weeks for up to 14 maintenance cycles.

Comparator: Matched placebo with the same paclitaxel/carboplatin chemotherapy and maintenance schedule.

Randomization and endpoint: Progression-free survival in the dMMR and pMMR cohorts, analyzed separately. The full randomization stratification variables and protocol amendments remain subject to protocol-level reconciliation before release.

Key efficacy outcomes

At 12 months, dMMR PFS was 74% versus 38% (HR 0.30, 95% CI 0.19–0.48; p<0.001). In pMMR disease, median PFS was 13.1 versus 8.7 months (HR 0.54, 95% CI 0.41–0.71; p<0.001). The dMMR 12-month landmark difference is 36 percentage points; landmark NNT 3 (ceil[1/0.36]).

Data maturity: Primary interim PFS analysis; OS and longer-term safety follow-up were immature at publication. Primary versus secondary endpoints are kept distinct; no survival estimate is inferred from a hazard ratio or median alone.

Safety and treatment burden

Grade ≥3 adverse events: dMMR 63.3% (69/109) versus 47.2% (50/106); pMMR 55.1% (152/276) versus 45.3% (124/274). Grade 5 adverse events occurred in 1 versus 2 dMMR patients and 6 versus 2 pMMR patients; attribution differs from all-cause grade ≥3 rates.

Delivery and discontinuation: Six 3-weekly chemotherapy cycles followed by up to 14 six-weekly maintenance infusions; immune-mediated toxicity and prolonged treatment duration require consideration.

Absolute effect and NNT / NNH

NNT 312 months

Treat approximately 3 patients with the intervention rather than comparator to prevent one additional event over the stated period.

Absolute benefit 36 percentage points; NNT = ceil(1 / 0.36) = 3. Landmark NNT derived from 12-month Kaplan–Meier estimates; cohort-specific.

Only same-endpoint, same-time-horizon absolute differences are eligible for NNT/NNH. Median survival and hazard ratios alone are not used. 12-month PFS in the dMMR cohort: 36 percentage-point absolute benefit at 12 months; NNT 3.

Interpretation and limitations

Adding pembrolizumab to carboplatin/paclitaxel improved PFS in both dMMR and pMMR advanced/recurrent endometrial cancer. The largest relative effect was in dMMR disease; toxicity and maintenance burden must be weighed.

Limitations: Primary endpoint is cohort-specific PFS; OS maturity was limited. NNT is a 12-month Kaplan–Meier landmark estimate for dMMR only, not an overall-trial NNT. Histology and molecular subgroup interpretation remain important.
