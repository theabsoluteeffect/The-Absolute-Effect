NSABP B-20

In node-negative, ER-positive breast cancer, does adding adjuvant chemotherapy to tamoxifen improve recurrence and survival?

PopulationAxillary node-negative, estrogen-receptor-positive breast cancer.ArmsTamoxifen alone versus tamoxifen plus adjuvant chemotherapy; the chemotherapy regimens included CMF-based therapy.Randomized1,577 women in the principal B-20 comparison.Modern relevanceLater prospective-retrospective work established the 21-gene recurrence score as a predictor of chemotherapy benefit within B-20.

Absolute Effect

Long-term recurrence
12-year RFS HR 0.52

Long-term follow-up showed substantially fewer recurrences with chemotherapy plus tamoxifen than tamoxifen alone.

Overall survival
12-year OS HR 0.78

The survival advantage was directionally favourable but did not reach conventional statistical significance in the long-term report (P=.063).

The original trial is best understood as evidence that chemotherapy can add benefit to endocrine therapy in selected node-negative ER-positive disease; the absolute benefit varies markedly with baseline genomic risk.

Clinical interpretation

B-20 established chemotherapy benefit as a heterogeneous phenomenon rather than an all-or-none indication. The later Oncotype DX analyses are an important part of how this trial is applied today.

In the prospective-retrospective 21-gene analysis, patients with high recurrence scores derived a large chemotherapy benefit, whereas patients with low scores derived minimal or no apparent benefit.

Limitations

The chemotherapy regimens and endocrine therapy used in B-20 predate contemporary taxanes, HER2-directed therapy, aromatase inhibitors and genomic-risk-guided treatment. Therefore the trial's relative evidence is foundational, but its raw event rates are not contemporary baseline risks.

NNT / NNH

A single overall NNT is not displayed because the clinically meaningful benefit is strongly modified by recurrence risk. The later 21-gene analysis reported a 27.6-percentage-point absolute reduction in 10-year distant recurrence for high-risk (RS ≥31) tumours, corresponding to an approximate NNT of 4 in that analysed subgroup.

NNH is not calculated from the pivotal efficacy report because a single fixed-time serious-toxicity endpoint is not appropriate for the historical regimen.
