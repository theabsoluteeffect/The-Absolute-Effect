EF-14

After chemoradiotherapy for newly diagnosed glioblastoma, does tumour-treating fields plus maintenance temozolomide improve survival?

Trial design and population

Population / eligibility
695 patients with newly diagnosed supratentorial glioblastoma without progression after chemoradiotherapy.
Intervention vs comparator
Tumour-treating fields (200 kHz, worn ≥18 h/day) plus maintenance temozolomide vs temozolomide alone.
Sample size
695
Primary endpoint
PFS and OS

Key results

Median OS was 20.9 months with TTFields plus temozolomide vs 16.0 months with temozolomide alone (HR 0.63, 95% CI 0.53–0.76; P<.001). Five-year OS was 13% vs 5%; median PFS 6.7 vs 4.0 months.

Absolute Effect

Safety and treatment burden

Distinctive toxicity is scalp skin irritation; the device requires scalp shaving, array changes and sustained daily adherence. Systemic grade ≥3 toxicity was not substantially increased in the original analysis.

Follow-up, maturity and limitations

Follow-up: Final analysis with approximately 40 months median follow-up; 5-year OS reported.

Open-label, adherence-dependent intervention; patients had to reach maintenance phase without progression. Device burden and patient preference are central.

Clinical interpretation

Adding TTFields to maintenance temozolomide improves OS in selected newly diagnosed glioblastoma patients who can manage the daily device burden.
