Clear-cell renal cell carcinoma · Medical Oncology · Phase III

LITESPARK-011

Advanced clear-cell RCC after prior immune-checkpoint inhibitor therapy. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignOpen-label randomized phase III trial
Population747 patients with advanced clear-cell RCC previously treated with an immune-checkpoint inhibitor randomized to belzutifan/lenvatinib or cabozantinib.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 747 patients with advanced clear-cell RCC previously treated with an immune-checkpoint inhibitor randomized to belzutifan/lenvatinib or cabozantinib.

Intervention: Belzutifan 120 mg orally daily plus lenvatinib 20 mg orally daily.

Comparator: Cabozantinib 60 mg orally daily.

Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival by blinded independent review; overall survival was a key secondary endpoint.

Follow-up and maturity: Primary PFS analysis reported; OS analysis was not statistically significant at the reported data cutoff.

Registry: NCT04586231. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

Median PFS 24.0 versus 16.7 months; HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.62–0.88), P=0.004. ORR 53.1% versus 40.2%. OS HR 0.85, P=0.20; no OS benefit is established.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in approximately 71% versus 65%; hypertension, anemia, hypoxia, proteinuria and other VEGF/HIF-2α toxicities require monitoring.

No NNT is calculated because Only median PFS and HR are used; no same-horizon absolute PFS estimate was verified for NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

Patient-reported symptom burden and treatment discontinuation should be interpreted alongside chronic TKI toxicity; no definitive QoL superiority is claimed here.

Interpretation and limitations

The trial supports belzutifan/lenvatinib as an active post-ICI option with PFS benefit, but it has not demonstrated an OS advantage over cabozantinib.

Post-ICI population and comparator are specific; do not compare indirectly with first-line IO/TKI trials. OS is not statistically significant.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
