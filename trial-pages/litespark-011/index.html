Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 747 adults with advanced ccRCC progressing on or after anti-PD-(L)1 therapy randomized 1:1: belzutifan/lenvatinib n=371 and cabozantinib n=376.

Intervention: Belzutifan 120 mg orally once daily plus lenvatinib 20 mg orally once daily until progression or unacceptable toxicity.

Comparator: Cabozantinib 60 mg orally once daily until progression or unacceptable toxicity.

Primary endpoint: Dual primary endpoints: PFS by BICR and OS.

Follow-up: Final data cutoff 2 February 2026; median time from randomization to cutoff 38.8 months (range 29.1–59.0).

Registry: NCT04586231

Final analysis: PFS HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.62–0.88); ORR 53.1% versus 40.2%; duration of response 22.0 versus 11.9 months. Median OS 33.7 versus 28.6 months; HR 0.85 (95% CI 0.70–1.03), one-sided P=0.04689, which did not cross the prespecified boundary 0.0223. The final abstract does not report a median PFS; earlier interim medians must not be substituted.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 TEAEs occurred in 85.4% versus 83.0%. In the FDA safety table, grade 3–4 hypertension was 33% versus 30%, anaemia 20% versus 7%, fatigue 16% versus 9%, proteinuria 10% versus 3.5%, and hypoxia 12% versus 0%.

The final abstract reports PFS as an HR and ORR/DOR; no common-horizon absolute PFS rate was used for NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL superiority reported in the final abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

The final analysis supports belzutifan/lenvatinib for PFS, response and duration of response after anti-PD-(L)1 therapy, without a statistically confirmed OS benefit. The previous page's 24.0 vs16.7-month PFS medians belonged to an earlier analysis and were removed.

Final PFS/ORR/DOR benefit persists, but OS did not meet its prespecified significance boundary. Chronic VEGF/HIF-2α toxicity and monitoring burden remain substantial.
