Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 747 patients with advanced clear-cell RCC previously treated with anti-PD-(L)1 therapy randomized to belzutifan/lenvatinib or cabozantinib.

Intervention: Belzutifan 120 mg orally once daily plus lenvatinib 20 mg orally once daily.

Comparator: Cabozantinib 60 mg orally once daily.

Primary endpoint: Progression-free survival by blinded independent review; overall survival was a key secondary endpoint.

Follow-up and maturity: Final prespecified PFS analysis reported; OS did not meet statistical significance at the reported cutoff.

Registry: NCT04586231. Arm denominators and endpoint definitions are retained from the primary report; no missing denominator is inferred.

Median PFS 24.0 versus 16.7 months; HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.62–0.88), P=0.004. ORR 53.1% versus 40.2%. OS HR 0.85 (95% CI 0.67–1.07), P=0.20; no OS advantage is established.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time effects are shown only when the primary source supplies a comparable endpoint and horizon.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 adverse events were common in both groups; hypertension, anemia, hypoxia, proteinuria and other VEGF/HIF-2α toxicities require monitoring. Exact grade 3–5 rates and treatment discontinuations require full safety-table reconciliation.

No NNT is calculated: the study uses time-to-event medians, single-arm cohorts or continuous outcomes without a comparable fixed-time absolute risk difference.

Quality of life and treatment burden

Patient-reported symptom burden should be interpreted alongside chronic TKI toxicity; no definitive QoL superiority is claimed.

Interpretation and limitations

Belzutifan/lenvatinib improves PFS over cabozantinib in the studied post-ICI setting, but OS is not significantly improved at the reported final analysis.

Post-ICI population and comparator are specific; the PFS benefit does not establish OS benefit and cannot be compared indirectly with first-line IO/TKI trials.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The source hierarchy and analysis population are explicit.
