KEYNOTE-C93 / GOG-3064 / ENGOT-en15

Clinical question: In first-line dMMR advanced or recurrent endometrial cancer, is pembrolizumab monotherapy superior to carboplatin-paclitaxel?

Median PFS 38.2 vs 8.4 months; HR 0.57 (95% CI 0.42–0.79; P=0.0002). 12-month PFS 67% vs 40% (NNT 4). OS boundary not met (HR 0.77; P=0.10). · Registry:

Design, population and treatment

Phase III, multicentre, open-label, randomised active-comparator trial at 122 centres in 24 countries. Adults with inoperable stage III/IV, persistent, advanced or recurrent dMMR endometrial cancer were randomised 1:1: pembrolizumab (n=149) or carboplatin–paclitaxel (n=150). Randomisation was stratified by disease status, histology and previous (neo)adjuvant chemotherapy.
Intervention: pembrolizumab 400 mg IV every 6 weeks for up to 18 cycles. Comparator: carboplatin AUC 5 or 6 plus paclitaxel 175 mg/m² IV day 1 every 3 weeks for 6 cycles.
Dual primary endpoints: PFS by RECIST 1.1 and blinded independent central review, and OS. Prespecified interim analysis cutoff: 24 April 2026; study ongoing.

Safety and absolute effect

OS medians were not reached; HR 0.77 (95% CI 0.52–1.15), P=.10; the prespecified OS significance boundary was not met. Grade ≥3 TRAEs occurred in 27/149 (18%) with pembrolizumab and 73/144 (51%) with chemotherapy; treatment-related discontinuation was 14/149 (9%) vs 21/144 (15%); treatment-related deaths were 2 vs 1.
At 12 months, the absolute PFS benefit was 27 percentage points; NNT 4 (1/0.27=3.70, rounded up). No NNH is assigned because grade ≥3 TRAEs were less frequent with pembrolizumab.

Interpretation and limitations

Current-practice context · 23 November 2026: Pembrolizumab monotherapy improved PFS versus carboplatin–paclitaxel in this dMMR population, but the investigators explicitly note that the trial did not compare monotherapy with the current immunotherapy-plus-chemotherapy standard. Do not interpret this as evidence to replace contemporary combination therapy. No NCCN recommendation category or reference is claimed because the current official full guideline source was not verified.
Limitations: interim analysis, OS boundary not met, and full QoL/supplementary subgroup detail was not present in the accessible abstract. NNT is restricted to 12-month PFS; no NNH is assigned because grade ≥3 TRAEs were lower with pembrolizumab.
