TIGER: TIP versus TI-CE

For relapsed or refractory GCT after first-line cisplatin, does conventional-dose TIP or high-dose TI-CE provide better outcomes as first salvage treatment?

Randomized comparison; outcome results not yet posted in sources reviewed

Reported outcomes

TIGER (Alliance A031102; NCT02375204) is a phase III trial comparing four cycles of conventional-dose TIP with TI-CE, which uses paclitaxel/ifosfamide for mobilization followed by high-dose carboplatin/etoposide and autologous stem-cell rescue.

Results pending. No efficacy bar is drawn because outcome results are not posted in the registry source reviewed. The graphic below represents trial status, not an outcome estimate.

Interpretation and limitations

As of the registry update reviewed 16 October 2026, the study was active but not recruiting, with completion projected for 2031 and no results posted. Do not infer efficacy or superiority before results are reported.

Guideline context: ESMO–EURACAN recommends standard-dose VIP/PEI, TIP or VeIP as conventional first salvage options after relapse following cisplatin-based chemotherapy. NCCN Testicular Cancer Guidelines Insights (Version 2.2025) is a contemporary US guideline source.
