Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 497 patients with advanced ESCC after first-line PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor plus platinum chemotherapy randomized: izalontamab brengitecan n=249, chemotherapy n=248.

Intervention: Izalontamab brengitecan 2.5 mg/kg IV on days 1 and 8 every 3 weeks.

Comparator: Physician-choice irinotecan, paclitaxel or docetaxel every 3 weeks.

Primary endpoint: Dual primary endpoints: PFS by blinded independent central review and OS.

Follow-up and maturity: Interim analysis with relatively short follow-up; longer survival and biomarker follow-up remain ongoing.

Registry: NCT06304974. The primary analysis population and denominators are stated explicitly.

Median PFS 4.17 versus 1.97 months; median OS 9.79 versus 7.20 months. ORR 27.7% (69/249) versus 9.3% (23/248). The published summary identifies both PFS and OS as improved; exact HR/CI/p values should be taken from the primary full report before release.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs occurred in 212/249 (85.1%) versus 147/248 (59.3%), predominantly hematologic.

No NNT is calculated: Median PFS/OS are time-to-event measures; no common-horizon absolute rates were used for NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage reported in the accessible summary.

Interpretation and limitations

PANKU-Esophagus01 is a positive randomized second-line ADC trial after PD-1/platinum therapy, with substantial grade ≥3 hematologic toxicity. The page remains held until exact effect estimates are extracted from the full primary report.

All sites in China; brain metastases excluded; short interim follow-up and biomarker data not yet available. Exact HR/CI/p values require full primary report extraction.
