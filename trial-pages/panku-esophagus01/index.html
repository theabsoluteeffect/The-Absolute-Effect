Recurrent/metastatic esophageal squamous cell carcinoma · Medical Oncology · Phase III

PANKU-Esophagus01

After progression on first-line PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor plus platinum chemotherapy. Primary trial report and authoritative dosing sources reviewed 17 November 2026.

DesignMulticentre open-label randomized phase III across 80 hospitals in China; 1:1
Population497 randomized; 249 vs248; 493 treated
Primary endpointDual primary endpoints: PFS by BICR and OS.
Follow-upFinal follow-up 17 October 2025; interim analysis reported.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 497 patients with recurrent/metastatic ESCC progressing after first-line PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor plus platinum chemotherapy randomized: izalontamab brengitecan n=249 and physician-selected chemotherapy n=248; 493 received treatment.

Intervention: Izalontamab brengitecan 2.5 mg/kg IV on days 1 and 8 every 3 weeks.

Comparator: Physician-selected irinotecan, paclitaxel or docetaxel every 3 weeks. The primary report does not specify a single dose because the chemotherapy arm was investigator-selected and heterogeneous; no uniform dose is inferred.

Primary endpoint: Dual primary endpoints: PFS by BICR and OS.

Follow-up: Final follow-up 17 October 2025; interim analysis reported.

Registry: NCT06304974

Median PFS 4.17 months (95% CI 3.61–4.53) versus 1.97 (1.61–2.73); HR 0.50 (95% CI 0.40–0.63), P<0.001. Median OS 9.79 months (7.89–11.01) versus 7.20 (6.18–7.82); HR 0.63 (0.48–0.82), P<0.001.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs occurred in 212/249 (85.1%) versus 147/248 (59.3%), predominantly hematologic.

No NNT is calculated: median time-to-event outcomes are not converted to NNT without a common-horizon absolute risk difference.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage reported.

Interpretation and limitations

PANKU-Esophagus01 is a positive phase III second-line ADC trial after PD-1/platinum progression. The primary abstract fully specifies the ADC dose and comparator choices; no uniform comparator dose exists to infer.

China-based population and high grade ≥3 hematologic toxicity. Physician-selected chemotherapy comparator is heterogeneous; exact component doses were not specified in the primary abstract.

Current-practice context · reviewed 17 November 2026

Trial conclusions are not guideline recommendations. NCCN provenance is not verified because the complete guideline/reference list was inaccessible; no recommendation category or citation number is assigned.
