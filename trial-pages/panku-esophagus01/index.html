Esophageal squamous cell carcinoma · Medical Oncology · Phase III

PANKU-Esophagus01

Previously treated advanced ESCC after platinum and PD-1/PD-L1-based first-line therapy. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignOpen-label randomized phase III trial
Population497 patients with recurrent or metastatic ESCC after prior first-line immunochemotherapy randomized to izalontamab brengitecan or physician-choice chemotherapy.
Primary endpointOverall survival and progression-free survival by independent review.
Data maturityPrimary OS/PFS analysis reported; longer-term survival and late ADC toxicity follow-up continue.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 497 patients with recurrent or metastatic ESCC after prior first-line immunochemotherapy randomized to izalontamab brengitecan or physician-choice chemotherapy.

Intervention: Izalontamab brengitecan 2.5 mg/kg intravenously on days 1 and 8 of each 21-day cycle.

Comparator: Physician-choice chemotherapy, including protocol-permitted taxane or irinotecan-based treatment.

Primary endpoint: Overall survival and progression-free survival by independent review.

Follow-up and maturity: Primary OS/PFS analysis reported; longer-term survival and late ADC toxicity follow-up continue.

Registry: NCT06304974. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

Median OS 9.79 versus 7.20 months; HR 0.63. Median PFS 4.17 versus 1.97 months; HR 0.50. Both primary comparisons were statistically significant in the published report.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events occurred in approximately 85.1% versus 59.3%; myelosuppression, gastrointestinal toxicity and ADC-related events are important.

No NNT is calculated because Only median OS/PFS and HR are used; no common fixed-time survival rates were verified for a valid NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive quantitative QoL superiority was reported in the primary abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

This is a recent positive phase III study in post-immunochemotherapy ESCC and is relevant to sequencing after PD-1/platinum therapy. Median survival differences are not converted into NNT.

Open-label study in a specific post-immunochemotherapy setting; high grade ≥3 toxicity and regional access/regulatory context matter. Do not extrapolate to first-line ESCC.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
