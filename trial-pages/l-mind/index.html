Transplant-ineligible R/R DLBCL after 1–3 prior regimens

Registry: NCT02399085 · Evidence reviewed 9 November 2026 · Aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase II open-label single-arm; n=80.Primary-source evidence summary

Study design, population and eligibility

Design and sample

Phase II open-label single-arm; n=80.

Population

R/R DLBCL after 1–3 regimens, ineligible for autologous transplant; selected population.

Treatment and comparator

Intervention: Tafasitamab + lenalidomide up to 12 cycles, then tafasitamab maintenance.

Comparator: No randomized comparator.

Endpoints

ORR primary.

Key efficacy and clinical outcomes

5-y report: ORR 57.5%, CR 40%; median PFS 11.6 mo, OS 33.5 mo; median DOR not reached.

Toxicity and treatment burden

Cytopenias, infections, fatigue and infusion reactions; neutropenia and thrombocytopenia require monitoring.

Absolute effect and NNT/NNH

Single-arm selected population; cannot compare directly with CAR-T or randomized second-line strategies.

NNT/NNH is not calculated unless a directly reported, comparable fixed-time risk difference supports it. Median survival and hazard ratios alone do not provide an NNT.

Critical interpretation and limitations

Single-arm selected population; cannot compare directly with CAR-T or randomized second-line strategies.
