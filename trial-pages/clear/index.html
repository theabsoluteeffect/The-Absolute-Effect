CLEAR

In untreated advanced clear-cell RCC, does lenvatinib plus pembrolizumab improve outcomes compared with sunitinib?

Population
1069 treatment-naive patients with advanced RCC.
Intervention / comparator
Lenvatinib 20 mg daily + pembrolizumab 200 mg IV every 3 weeks versus sunitinib 50 mg daily, 4 weeks on/2 weeks off.
Primary/major efficacy result
36-month OS: 66.4% vs 60.2%; absolute improvement 6.2 points; NNT 17.
Overall survival
Final OS HR 0.79 (95% CI 0.63–0.99); median OS 53.7 vs 54.3 months. PFS HR 0.47; median PFS 23.9 vs 9.2 months.

Absolute Effect

36-month OS: 66.4% vs 60.2%; absolute improvement 6.2 points; NNT 17.

Clinical interpretation: Objective response rate was 71.3% vs 36.7%. Benefit in PFS was observed across major baseline subgroups.

Toxicity / treatment burden: Grade ≥3 treatment-emergent adverse events occurred in 82.4% with lenvatinib/pembrolizumab versus 71.8% with sunitinib. NNH not assigned because toxicity is multifactorial rather than one central fixed-time endpoint.

Important limitations

Open-label design and treatment evolution after progression can affect absolute survival comparisons. Absolute effects are reported only where a reliable common timepoint or directly reported rate permits calculation; otherwise NNT/NNH is not forced from medians or hazard ratios.

Primary source

Motzer R, et al. N Engl J Med. 2021;384:1289-1300; extended follow-up J Clin Oncol.
