GEMSTONE-303

Clinical question: In first-line pd-l1 cps ≥5 unresectable/metastatic gastric/gej adenocarcinoma, does the investigational strategy improve clinically meaningful outcomes versus its control?

Registry: NCT03802591 · One underlying study; aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase III, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study in China; 479 patients. Evidence cut-off: 13 November 2026.

Design, population and eligibility

Design / sample size

Phase III, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study in China; 479 patients.

Eligibility

Previously untreated unresectable locally advanced or metastatic gastric/GEJ adenocarcinoma with PD-L1 CPS ≥5.

Intervention

Sugemalimab + CAPOX versus placebo + CAPOX.

Sugemalimab 1,200 mg IV every 3 weeks for up to 24 months plus CAPOX every 3 weeks for up to 6 cycles.

Comparator

placebo + CAPOX.

Primary endpoint: Co-primary OS and investigator-assessed PFS.

Key efficacy outcomes

Median follow-up 25.1–26.3 months. Median OS 15.6 vs 12.6 months, HR 0.75 (95% CI 0.61–0.92), p=0.006. Median PFS 7.6 vs 6.1 months, HR 0.66 (0.54–0.81), p<0.001.

Reported outcome
15.6

Comparator: 12.6. The bars are a simple display of the first reported pair only; consult the endpoint and time horizon above.

Safety and treatment delivery

Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs 53.9% vs 50.6%; treatment discontinuation 88.0% vs 95.4%, mostly progression.

Clinical interpretation and limitations

Positive phase III study in CPS ≥5 population, conducted in China. Biomarker assay/threshold and geographic context matter; do not extrapolate to all PD-L1-negative patients.

Interpret the reported hazard ratios in the context of the prespecified endpoint, statistical hierarchy, follow-up maturity, crossover/subsequent therapy and enrolled population. Median survival differences are not risk differences and must not be converted into NNT.

Absolute effect

No NNT/NNH is displayed unless an absolute event-risk difference at a clearly stated fixed timepoint can be calculated from reported trial data. Median survival differences and hazard ratios alone do not support an NNT. The visual above uses reported outcome data only.
