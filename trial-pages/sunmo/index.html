SUNMO

SUNMO showed a substantial PFS and response advantage for fixed-duration mosunetuzumab plus polatuzumab vedotin over R-GemOx in transplant-ineligible relapsed/refractory LBCL; overall survival remains interim.

Study identity and design

Global, open-label, randomized phase III study. Two hundred eight patients were randomized 2:1 (138 to mosunetuzumab plus polatuzumab vedotin; 70 to R-GemOx). Approximately 43.8% had one prior line; 73.6% had primary refractory disease or relapse within 12 months.

Transplant-ineligible relapsed/refractory large B-cell lymphoma after ≥1 systemic line. Stratification factors were number of prior lines and whether the disease was relapsed or refractory to the most recent therapy.

Intervention and comparator

Experimental: Mosunetuzumab SC step-up (5 mg C1D1, 45 mg C1D8 and C1D15; then 45 mg C2–8D1) plus polatuzumab vedotin 1.8 mg/kg IV C1–6D1; 21-day cycles.

Comparator: R-GemOx q14 days for up to 8 cycles: rituximab 375 mg/m², gemcitabine 1,000 mg/m² and oxaliplatin 100 mg/m² on day 1.

Stratification: Prior systemic lines (1 versus ≥2) and relapsed versus refractory status after the most recent therapy.

Key efficacy outcome

Co-primary PFS: median 11.5 versus 3.8 months; HR 0.41 (95% CI 0.28–0.61), p<0.0001. Co-primary ORR: 70.3% versus 40.0%, p<0.0001; CR 51.4% versus 24.3%.

Overall survival: interim HR 0.80 (95% CI 0.54–1.20); not mature and no statistically conclusive OS advantage at this analysis. Patient-reported outcomes were described as improved versus R-GemOx, but the accessible abstract does not provide the instrument, timepoint or effect size.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3–4 adverse events occurred in 58.5% versus 57.9%; grade 5 events in 5.2% versus 6.3% in the primary conference safety report. CRS occurred in 25.9% with mosun-Pola (21.5% grade 1, 3.7% grade 2, 0.7% grade 3); grade ≥2 CRS was <5%; no ICANS was reported.

Quality of life: Patient-reported outcomes were reported as improved with mosun-Pola versus R-GemOx; the accessible JCO abstract does not specify the instrument or quantitative timepoint.

Treatment burden / discontinuation: Eight 21-day cycles of mosun-Pola versus up to eight 14-day R-GemOx cycles; detailed discontinuation counts were not available in the accessible primary abstract.

Absolute effect and NNT / NNH

NNT is not calculated. The primary publication reports median PFS, hazard ratios and ORR, but no comparable fixed-time absolute PFS/OS risks. NNT must not be derived from medians or hazard ratios.

Interpretation and limitations

The regimen offers a fixed-duration, largely outpatient alternative for transplant-ineligible R/R LBCL and improves PFS and response over R-GemOx. OS remains interim; the magnitude of benefit should be interpreted with the open-label design and the selected comparator population in mind.

Limitations: Open-label design; OS immature; exact patient-reported outcome instrument/timepoint and detailed discontinuation table were not available in the accessible primary abstract; conference safety data are supplementary to the peer-reviewed primary efficacy report.
