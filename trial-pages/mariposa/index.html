MARIPOSA

In untreated EGFR-mutated advanced NSCLC, does amivantamab plus lazertinib improve progression-free survival over osimertinib?

Registry: NCT04487080 · Evidence reviewed 9 November 2026 · Aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase III randomized 2:2:1; n=1,074.Primary-source evidence summary

Study design, population and eligibility

Design and sample

Phase III randomized 2:2:1; n=1,074.

Population

Untreated advanced NSCLC with EGFR exon19del/L858R.

Treatment and comparator

Intervention: Amivantamab + lazertinib.

Comparator: Osimertinib; lazertinib-alone third arm.

Endpoints

PFS by BICR primary.

Key efficacy and clinical outcomes

Median PFS 23.7 vs 16.6 mo (HR 0.70); ORR 86% vs 85%; interim OS HR 0.80 (0.61–1.05).

Toxicity and treatment burden

EGFR toxicities, infusion reactions, rash, paronychia and VTE; discontinuation of all agents for treatment-related AEs 10% vs 3%.

Absolute effect and NNT/NNH

PFS superiority established; interim OS not significant. Greater treatment burden than oral osimertinib.

NNT/NNH is not calculated unless a directly reported, comparable fixed-time risk difference supports it. Median survival and hazard ratios alone do not provide an NNT.

Critical interpretation and limitations

PFS superiority established; interim OS not significant. Greater treatment burden than oral osimertinib.
