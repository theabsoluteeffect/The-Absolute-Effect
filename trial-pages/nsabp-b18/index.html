The Absolute Effect / Evidence

NSABP B-18

Breast Cancer · Neoadjuvant Therapy

Clinical question

Does neoadjuvant doxorubicin plus cyclophosphamide improve surgical outcomes and long-term disease control compared with postoperative chemotherapy in operable breast cancer?

Trial context

NSABP B-18 was a foundational randomized trial comparing chemotherapy before surgery with the same chemotherapy after surgery in women with operable breast cancer. Neoadjuvant treatment increased tumour response and breast-conserving surgery, but changing the timing of chemotherapy did not improve long-term overall survival.

Absolute Effect perspective

The main patient-important benefit of the neoadjuvant strategy in B-18 was surgical downstaging and the opportunity to observe treatment response in vivo, rather than a demonstrated survival advantage over postoperative chemotherapy.
