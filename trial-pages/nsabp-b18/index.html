The Absolute Effect

NSABP B-18

Breast Cancer · Neoadjuvant Therapy

Clinical question

Does neoadjuvant doxorubicin plus cyclophosphamide improve surgical outcomes and long-term disease control compared with postoperative chemotherapy in operable breast cancer?

Why the trial matters

NSABP B-18 is a foundational randomized trial establishing the role of preoperative systemic chemotherapy in operable breast cancer. Neoadjuvant treatment increased clinical and pathologic response and enabled breast-conserving surgery in appropriate patients, while long-term overall survival was not improved simply by changing chemotherapy timing.

Absolute Effect perspective

The key clinical consequence is not that neoadjuvant therapy creates a survival advantage over the same chemotherapy given after surgery. Its major value is response before surgery, surgical downstaging and the opportunity to assess treatment response in vivo.
