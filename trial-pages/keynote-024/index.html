KEYNOTE-024

In untreated metastatic NSCLC with PD-L1 TPS ≥50% and no sensitizing EGFR/ALK alteration, does pembrolizumab monotherapy improve survival over platinum chemotherapy?

Registry: NCT02142738 · Evidence reviewed 9 November 2026 · Aliases and follow-up publications consolidated.

Phase III open-label randomized; n=305.Primary-source evidence summary

Study design, population and eligibility

Design and sample

Phase III open-label randomized; n=305.

Population

Untreated advanced NSCLC, PD-L1 TPS ≥50%, no sensitizing EGFR/ALK alteration.

Treatment and comparator

Intervention: Pembrolizumab up to 35 cycles.

Comparator: Platinum chemotherapy; crossover permitted.

Endpoints

PFS primary; OS secondary.

Key efficacy and clinical outcomes

At 5 y: median OS 26.3 vs 13.4 mo (HR 0.62); 5-y OS 31.9% vs 16.3%, despite 66% effective crossover.

Toxicity and treatment burden

Grade 3–5 treatment-related AEs 31.2% vs 53.3% in long-term report; immune toxicity remains relevant.

Absolute effect and NNT/NNH

Do not extrapolate to TPS 1–49%; crossover affects OS interpretation.

NNT/NNH is not calculated unless a directly reported, comparable fixed-time risk difference supports it. Median survival and hazard ratios alone do not provide an NNT.
