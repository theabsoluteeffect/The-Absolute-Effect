Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 521 patients with ERBB2/HER2-positive early breast cancer randomized to anbenitamab/HB1801-based neoadjuvant treatment or trastuzumab/pertuzumab/taxane-based standard therapy.

Intervention: Anbenitamab 10 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks plus HB1801 260 mg/m² IV every 3 weeks for six neoadjuvant cycles, with protocol-permitted carboplatin AUC 6.

Comparator: Trastuzumab (8 mg/kg loading then 6 mg/kg q3w) plus pertuzumab (840 mg loading then 420 mg q3w) and docetaxel 75 mg/m² q3w for six cycles, with protocol-permitted carboplatin AUC 6.

Primary endpoint: Pathological complete response at surgery.

Follow-up and maturity: Neoadjuvant pCR analysis; EFS/OS follow-up is immature.

Registry: NCT06747338. Arm denominators are given where available; missing values are not inferred.

Key efficacy outcome

pCR 62.4% versus 51.2%; adjusted absolute difference 11.4 percentage points (95% CI 3.2–19.6), P=0.004. The source-reported adjusted difference corresponds to NNT 9; rounded crude rates differ by 11.2 points.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3–4 adverse events occurred in 29.3% versus 28.3%; no treatment-related death was reported in the primary abstract.

NNT 9

62.4% versus 51.2%; absolute benefit 11.4 percentage points at At surgery. No residual invasive cancer in breast and nodes by trial definition. Calculation: ceil(100 / 11.4) = 9; source-reported adjusted absolute difference.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage was reported.

Interpretation and limitations

BCTOP-H-N01 reports a higher pCR rate with the investigational HER2-directed regimen and similar grade 3–4 toxicity; mature EFS/OS and regulatory context remain necessary.

pCR is a surrogate and survival follow-up is immature. The source-reported adjusted absolute difference is retained rather than recalculated from rounded arm rates.
