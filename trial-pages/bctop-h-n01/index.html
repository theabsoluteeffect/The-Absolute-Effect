HER2-positive early breast cancer · Medical Oncology · Phase III

BCTOP-H-N01

Neoadjuvant treatment of HER2-positive early breast cancer. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

DesignOpen-label randomized phase III trial
Population521 patients with ERBB2/HER2-positive early breast cancer randomized to anbenitamab/HB1801-based neoadjuvant treatment or trastuzumab/pertuzumab/taxane-based standard therapy.
Primary endpointPathological complete response at surgery.
Data maturityNeoadjuvant pathological response analysis; event-free and overall survival follow-up is immature.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 521 patients with ERBB2/HER2-positive early breast cancer randomized to anbenitamab/HB1801-based neoadjuvant treatment or trastuzumab/pertuzumab/taxane-based standard therapy.

Intervention: Anbenitamab 10 mg/kg intravenously every 3 weeks plus HB1801 260 mg/m² every 3 weeks, with protocol-permitted carboplatin AUC 6.

Comparator: Trastuzumab (8 mg/kg loading then 6 mg/kg every 3 weeks) plus pertuzumab (840 mg loading then 420 mg every 3 weeks) and docetaxel 75 mg/m² every 3 weeks, with protocol-permitted carboplatin AUC 6.

Primary endpoint: Pathological complete response at surgery.

Follow-up and maturity: Neoadjuvant pathological response analysis; event-free and overall survival follow-up is immature.

Registry: NCT06747338. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

pCR 62.4% versus 51.2%; primary report gives an adjusted absolute difference of 11.4 percentage points (P=0.006). The source-reported difference corresponds to NNT 9; rounded crude rates differ by 11.2 points.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade 3–4 adverse events were approximately 23.6% versus 24.0%; hematologic toxicity and infusion-related events require review.

NNT 9Pathological complete response · At surgery

62.4% versus 51.2%; absolute benefit 11.4 percentage points. No residual invasive cancer in breast and nodes by the trial definition. Calculation: ceil(100 / 11.4) = ceil(8.77) = 9; source-reported adjusted absolute difference.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage was reported.

Interpretation and limitations

BCTOP-H-N01 reports a higher pCR rate with the investigational HER2-directed combination, with similar reported grade 3–4 toxicity; mature EFS/OS and regulatory context remain necessary.

pCR is a surrogate endpoint and survival follow-up is immature. The source-reported adjusted absolute difference is retained rather than recalculated from rounded percentages.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
