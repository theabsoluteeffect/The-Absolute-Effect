Toripalimab plus modified FOLFIRINOX

Population392 patients with histologically confirmed borderline resectable or locally advanced pancreatic cancer randomized 1:1: immunochemotherapy n=196 and chemotherapy n=196.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 392 patients with histologically confirmed borderline resectable or locally advanced pancreatic cancer randomized 1:1: immunochemotherapy n=196 and chemotherapy n=196.

Intervention: Modified FOLFIRINOX plus toripalimab; exact toripalimab and chemotherapy component doses must be taken from the JCO protocol supplement before release.

Comparator: Modified FOLFIRINOX alone.

Primary endpoint: Event-free survival in the intention-to-treat population.

Follow-up and maturity: Median follow-up 39.6 months.

Registry: NCT03983057. The primary analysis population and denominators are stated explicitly.

Median EFS 11.1 versus 9.6 months; HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.60–0.92), P=0.007. OS 20.6 versus 17.1 months; HR 0.81 (0.63–1.03), not statistically significant. ORR 30.1% (59/196) versus 18.9% (37/196). Resection 42.3% versus 43.9%; R0 among resected 84.1% (69/82) versus 74.7% (62/83).

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 AEs 47.9% (93/194) versus 49.2% (96/195). Grade ≥3 postoperative complications 30.1% (25/83) versus 25.6% (22/86).

No NNT is calculated: Median EFS and HR do not provide a common-horizon absolute risk difference; no NNT is calculated.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive QoL advantage was reported.

Interpretation and limitations

Adding PD-1 blockade improved EFS without increasing overall grade ≥3 AEs in this phase II trial, but did not establish OS benefit. The primary endpoint is EFS, not PFS.

Open-label phase II study; subgroup signal in borderline-resectable disease but not locally advanced disease is exploratory. OS is not statistically significant. Exact doses require supplement review.

Current-practice context · reviewed 17 November 2026

Trial conclusions are not guideline recommendations. NCCN provenance is not verified because the complete guideline/reference list was inaccessible; no section, reference number or recommendation category is assigned.
