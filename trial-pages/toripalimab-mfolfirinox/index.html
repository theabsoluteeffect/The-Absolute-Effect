Toripalimab plus mFOLFIRINOX

Neoadjuvant treatment for borderline-resectable or locally advanced pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma. This page separates the primary endpoint from secondary outcomes and distinguishes source-reported absolute rates from time-to-event estimates.

Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: Patients with borderline-resectable or locally advanced pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma enrolled in a randomized phase II comparison; arm-level denominators are reported in the primary paper.

Intervention: Toripalimab 240 mg intravenously every 3 weeks plus modified FOLFIRINOX every 2 weeks, followed by surgery and protocol-directed postoperative management.

Comparator: Modified FOLFIRINOX on the same schedule without toripalimab.

Primary endpoint: Event-free survival.

Follow-up and maturity: Event-free survival analysis reported; overall survival and long-term postoperative outcomes require continued follow-up.

Registry: NCT03983057. Randomization, analysis populations and endpoint definitions should be read from the linked primary report and protocol; no denominator is inferred where the abstract does not state one.

Key efficacy outcome

Median event-free survival

Toripalimab + mFOLFIRINOX
11.1 months
mFOLFIRINOX
9.6 months

Median EFS 11.1 versus 9.6 months; HR 0.74, P=0.007. The result is phase II and should be interpreted with the confidence interval and prespecified analysis population in the primary report.

Time-to-event medians and hazard ratios are reported as such; they are not converted into NNT. Fixed-time absolute effects are shown separately only when a comparable endpoint and horizon are available.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 adverse events occurred in approximately 73.4% versus 68.2%; chemotherapy-related cytopenias, gastrointestinal toxicity and immune-related adverse events are relevant.

No NNT is calculated because Median EFS and HR are reported; no verified fixed-time EFS rate was used for NNT.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No definitive quantitative QoL advantage was reported in the abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

The randomized signal supports further study of adding PD-1 blockade to neoadjuvant mFOLFIRINOX in locally advanced pancreatic cancer; it does not establish a new universal standard.

Phase II sample size, open-label treatment and complex surgical pathways. EFS includes progression and failure to reach resection; this endpoint is not interchangeable with OS.

Superiority, non-inferiority, surrogate response and mature survival are not interchangeable. The page preserves the primary endpoint hierarchy and does not infer a survival advantage from response or PFS alone.
