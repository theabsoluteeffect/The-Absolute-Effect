Clinical question: Does trastuzumab rezetecan improve progression-free survival over physician-choice chemotherapy in previously treated HER2-low advanced breast cancer?

Randomized551 (277 vs 274)
Safety population276 vs 271
RegistryNCT05814354
Primary endpointBICR-assessed PFS

Design, population and treatment

Women aged 18–75 years with unresectable locally recurrent or metastatic HER2-low breast cancer (IHC 1+ or IHC 2+/ISH-negative), ECOG 0–1, and no more than one prior chemotherapy regimen for advanced disease. Patients with hormone-receptor-positive disease had received prior endocrine therapy.

Experimental: trastuzumab rezetecan 6.4 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks. Control: investigator-selected single-agent chemotherapy (capecitabine, eribulin, gemcitabine, paclitaxel or nab-paclitaxel) according to the applicable label; there was no single uniform chemotherapy dose/schedule.

Key efficacy outcome

Median PFS · trastuzumab rezetecan
16.4 mo
Median PFS · physician-choice chemotherapy

In the overall population, median PFS was 16.4 versus 6.8 months (HR 0.27, 95% CI 0.21–0.34; P<.001). In the chemotherapy-naive subgroup, median PFS was 16.6 versus 6.8 months (HR 0.25, 95% CI 0.19–0.33; P<.001). Overall-survival maturity was not established in the abstract reviewed.

Bar lengths are normalized to the larger median PFS; they do not represent absolute event rates.

Safety and absolute effect

Grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events occurred in 232/276 (84.1%) versus 184/271 (67.9%). Decreased neutrophil count was the most common grade ≥3 treatment-related event (65.2% vs 54.2%). A quality-of-life result was not reported in the accessible primary abstract.

The previous NNH pictogram has been removed. The 16.2 percentage-point difference lacks a comparable fixed exposure horizon/equal treatment exposure; NNH would be misleading. Median PFS and hazard ratios do not support NNT.

Interpretation and limitations

The PFS effect is clinically notable, but interpretation should account for the China-only study population, physician-choice control and immature OS. The adverse-event rates should not be converted to NNH without a comparable time horizon.

Current-practice context (reviewed 19 Nov 2026): NCCN provenance and recommendation category were not verified because the complete current guideline/reference list was inaccessible. Do not equate the trial conclusion with a guideline recommendation.
