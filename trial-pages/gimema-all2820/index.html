Design, population and treatment

Eligibility and sample: 236 adults with newly diagnosed Ph-positive ALL randomized 2:1: ponatinib plus blinatumomab n=158 and imatinib plus chemotherapy n=78. Median age 55 years (range 24–82).

Intervention: Ponatinib plus blinatumomab according to the NEJM protocol; exact dose and cycle schedule are in the protocol supplement, which was inaccessible during this run and is not inferred.

Comparator: Imatinib plus multiagent chemotherapy according to the NEJM protocol; exact component doses/cycles require supplement extraction.

Primary endpoint: Event-free survival.

Follow-up: Median follow-up 23.7 months; estimated 24-month EFS reported.

Registry: NCT04722848

Key efficacy outcome

24-month event-free survival

Ponatinib + blinatumomab (158)
86%
Imatinib + chemotherapy (78)
67%

Estimated 24-month EFS 86% versus 67%, P<0.001; the primary abstract does not report an HR. Survival without MRD 68% versus 42%; complete hematologic response at end of induction 94% versus 79%; molecular response after blinatumomab consolidation 71% versus 49%. OS 90% versus 85%; cumulative relapse 5% versus 13%. After crossover, 20/31 (64%) achieved molecular response.

Safety and absolute effect

The primary abstract does not provide the complete grade 3–5 safety table. Arterial/venous events, infection, cytopenias, chemotherapy-specific toxicity and discontinuations require the NEJM supplementary appendix.

NNT 6

86% versus 67%; absolute benefit 19 percentage points at 24 months. Calculation: ceil(100 / 19) = 6.

Quality of life and treatment burden

No validated comparative QoL result in the primary abstract.

Interpretation and limitations

A frontline chemotherapy-free ponatinib/blinatumomab strategy improved 24-month EFS over imatinib/chemotherapy. Do not attach an HR not reported in the primary abstract; the exact regimen and safety remain held.

Follow-up 23.7 months; crossover from control for persistent/reappearing MRD complicates long-term comparisons. Full dose schedule and safety supplement are needed before release.
