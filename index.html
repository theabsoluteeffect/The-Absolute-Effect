Trial of the Day · 2026-09-20

TAISHAN-302: Tambotatug pelitecan versus topotecan in relapsed small-cell lung cancer

Small-Cell Lung Cancer · Medical Oncology · Relapsed SCLC · 451 patients

Treatment: Tambotatug pelitecan 2.0 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks versus topotecan, after one prior platinum-based regimen.

What did it show? Median OS was 13.3 vs 9.4 months (HR 0.46, 95% CI 0.35–0.62; P<.001). Median PFS was 7.4 vs 2.8 months (HR 0.29, 95% CI 0.23–0.37; P<.001). ORR was 59.1% vs 9.7%: +49.4 percentage points.

Critical clinical review
TAISHAN-302 showed a large improvement in OS, PFS and response with the B7-H3-directed ADC tambotatug pelitecan compared with topotecan. Important considerations include the Chinese study population, open-label design and maturity of the interim OS analysis.

How to read the evidence

Every entry should make the clinical consequence easier to see.

01 · What the trial reported

HR, RR, OR or another relative measure, exactly as reported.

02 · What changes for patients

Absolute difference at a defined time point or clinically meaningful unit.

03 · Put it in 100 patients

NNT/NNH when appropriate, always tied to a specified endpoint and time horizon.
