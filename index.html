Trial of the Day · 2026-09-18

NSABP B-17: Whole-breast radiotherapy after lumpectomy for DCIS

Breast Cancer · Radiation · DCIS · 818 women with localized DCIS and negative margins

What did it show? In NSABP B-17, adding 50 Gy whole-breast RT after lumpectomy reduced 8-year invasive ipsilateral breast recurrence from 13.4% to 3.9%, an absolute reduction of 9.5 percentage points.

Critical clinical review
Radiotherapy substantially improves local control after breast-conserving surgery for DCIS, but the demonstrated benefit is recurrence reduction rather than an overall-survival advantage. NNT is approximately 11 based on 5-year event-free survival.
