Trial of the Day · 2026-09-08

Adjuvant paclitaxel-platinum versus 5-FU-platinum in high-risk penile cancer

Journal of the National Cancer Institute · 49 men with resected node-positive penile squamous cell carcinoma and at least one high-risk feature

What did it show? At median follow-up of 69 months, median DFS was 12.4 versus 19.6 months (HR 1.16, 95% CI 0.55–2.44; P=.686) and median OS was 21.3 versus 36.7 months (HR 1.13, 95% CI 0.52–2.44; P=.754). Grade ≥3 toxicity was 77.3% versus 40.9%.

Critical clinical review
The study was stopped early and was underpowered for definitive efficacy comparisons. The severe-toxicity signal favoured paclitaxel-platinum, but the trial does not establish survival equivalence or superiority.

How to read the evidence

Every entry should make the clinical consequence easier to see.

01 · What the trial reported

HR, RR, OR or another relative measure, exactly as reported.

02 · What changes for patients

Absolute difference at a defined time point or clinically meaningful unit.

03 · Put it in 100 patients

NNT/NNH when appropriate, always tied to a specified endpoint and time horizon.
