Trial of the Day · 2026-09-08

Adjuvant paclitaxel-platinum versus 5-FU-platinum in high-risk penile cancer

Journal of the National Cancer Institute. Randomized trial in 49 men with resected node-positive penile squamous cell carcinoma and at least one high-risk feature.

Median DFS was 12.4 versus 19.6 months (HR 1.16, 95% CI 0.55–2.45; P=.686) and median OS was 21.3 versus 36.7 months (HR 1.13, 95% CI 0.52–2.44; P=.754). Grade ≥3 toxicity was 77.3% versus 40.9%.

Critical review: The study was stopped early and was underpowered for definitive efficacy comparisons. The clinically important signal is substantially lower severe toxicity with paclitaxel-platinum, but the trial does not establish survival equivalence or superiority.

How to read the evidence

Relative effect

HR, RR, OR or another relative measure exactly as reported.

Absolute effect

The change in outcomes at a defined time point or clinically meaningful unit.

NNT / NNH

Endpoint- and time-specific measures when clinically defensible.
